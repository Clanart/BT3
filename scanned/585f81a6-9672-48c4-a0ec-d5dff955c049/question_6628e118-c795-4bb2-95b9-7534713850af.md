Target: `ClementineAggregator::new_deposit` resource-driving of the N-of-N session, `nonce_aggregator`/`signature_aggregator` pipeline. Attacker action: repeatedly calls `NewDeposit` with distinct attacker-fabricated `DepositParams` on the aggregator's open port, no cert, interleaved with a legitimate user's real `NewDeposit` call for their own funded deposit. Preconditions: `client_verification=false`. Call sequence: `Noop` admits both -> both requests independently drive verifiers through `create_nonce_streams`/`nonce_aggregator`/`signature_aggregator`/`signature_distributor`, each verifier producing partial signatures keyed only by the sighash stream content, not caller identity. Binding: N-of-N partial signature produced == a spend that Citrea/the depositing user actually authorised; an unauthenticated party being able to trigger arbitrary numbers of these signing sessions means the equality is never checked against caller identity at all. Impact: Critical - verifiers can be forced to produce N-of-N partial signatures for deposit outputs the attacker specifies, independent of whether the depositing user consented, directly implicating the 'N-of-N partial signatures produced for a spend no Citrea withdrawal authorises' impact class. Proof idea: `cargo test` issuing two concurrent unauthenticated `new_deposit` calls with attacker-controlled `DepositParams`, asserting the aggregator completes the signing pipeline for both.
