# Q2598: WombatBribeManager.castVotes - vote and cast in one transaction with no time weighting

## Question

Under the attacker votes in the block immediately before a known keeper cast, is there an unprivileged sequence of `castVotes(bool swapForBnb)` that leaves `targetVote computed in castVotes` unreconciled with `totalVotes() from veWom.balanceOf(wombatStaking)`, violates the invariant that bribe share must be weighted by the time a vote was actually committed, not by the balance at the instant the bribe is queued, and delivers Critical - Direct theft of user funds?
