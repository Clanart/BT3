# Q4088: increment via liquidate: apply a transform after the gate that was supposed to boun

## Question
Does `liquidate` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1382) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the `price-feeds` buffers and their ordering reach `increment` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:137) in a state where it apply a transform after the gate that was supposed to bound its output?
