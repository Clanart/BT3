Target: `Verifier::deposit_sign` session removal/re-add race vs. `Verifier::sign_optimistic_payout`'s session lookup via `session_map.sessions.get_mut(&session_id)`, `core/src/verifier.rs` lines 900-905 and 1228-1249. Attacker action: an unprivileged caller who has learned (via a prior `nonce_gen` call they made themselves, since session ids are returned directly to any caller) a `session_id` that a DIFFERENT deposit's `deposit_sign` task is concurrently using, sends a `sign_optimistic_payout` request for that same `session_id` during the exact window it is absent from `sessions` (removed at line 904, not yet re-added until 973). Preconditions: unauthenticated `nonce_gen` handing out ids that are not scoped per-caller/per-deposit; the aggregator does not enforce that a `session_id` used in `sign_optimistic_payout` was originally created for an optimistic-payout flow. Call sequence: `sign_optimistic_payout`'s lookup fails with \"Could not find session id\" during the window, but if the attacker's request lands microseconds AFTER re-add (line 973) and BEFORE `deposit_finalize`'s pops (1228+), it succeeds and steals the reserved movetx/emergency-stop secnonce as in a prior finding, but here achieved purely by the attacker independently guessing/observing a `u128` session_id with no relation to their own deposit. Binding: session_id != publicly guessable / session ownership(session_id) == the single deposit or payout flow that created it. Impact: Critical, N-of-N partial signature produced for a spend no Citrea withdrawal authorises. Proof idea: cargo test where an attacker-controlled `sign_optimistic_payout` call targets a session_id belonging to a concurrently-running honest `deposit_sign`, asserting the call is rejected because sessions are tagged by intended use, not just by opaque id.
