Binding: `nofn_xonly_pk` cached and stored in `deposits.deposit_params` == `nofn_xonly_pk` derivable from the deposit_data.actors.verifiers that actually passed all `is_deposit_valid` checks. Proof idea: cargo test asserting `get_nofn_xonly_pk` is never called (and cached) prior to `is_deposit_valid` succeeding, or that a failed `is_deposit_valid` call discards the cached field.
