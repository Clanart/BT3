Attacker action: fund a `BaseDeposit` outpoint with exactly `bridge_amount` to the correct taproot script, get it fully signed and moved to vault via `Aggregator::new_deposit`, then resubmit the exact same `DepositParams` (same `deposit_outpoint`) in a second `new_deposit` gRPC call before the first move tx is mined. Preconditions: deposit outpoint still shows as an unspent UTXO on the Bitcoin RPC the verifier queries. Binding broken: a move txid the verifiers sign == exactly one unspent `bridge_amount` deposit output, counted once. Scoped impact: Critical, N-of-N partial signatures produced for two competing move-to-vault spends of the same deposit UTXO. Proof idea: cargo test in core/src/test extending `aggregator_double_deposit` to assert that at most one of `movetx_one`/`movetx_two` can ever confirm and that verifiers refuse to sign the second once the first's spending outpoint is observed spent.
