# Q2545: convert-to-shares-preview via redeem: satisfy the freshness gate with a timestamp the gate was n

## Question

- Entrypoint: `redeem` (`mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-stx.clar:797`), unprivileged and publicly callable

- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of user funds at rest or in motion
- Fast validation: In `local-testing/tests` on a local fork, drive `redeem` with `min-out`, then read `convert-to-shares-preview` state before and after in the same block and assert the two sides of the invariant are equal.
