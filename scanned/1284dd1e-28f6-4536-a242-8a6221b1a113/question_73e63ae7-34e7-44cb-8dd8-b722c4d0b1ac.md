In `Database::mark_payout_handled` and `get_handled_payout_kickoff_txid` (core/src/database/verifier.rs), the query keys off `payout_txid` alone: `SELECT kickoff_txid FROM withdrawals WHERE payout_txid = $1 AND is_payout_handled = TRUE`. Preconditions: withdrawal index i has been registered with UTXO U; no consumed-marker exists tying U to a single payout_txid before broadcast. Call sequence: attacker (the withdrawer) broadcasts two competing transactions spending U with different txids (differing OP_RETURN operator keys or fee), and due to a reorg (attacker-controlled via `bitcoin_syncer`-observed chain data they can shape by choosing which of their two conflicting broadcasts an honest miner includes across a reorg) a second one confirms after the first was already marked handled with a different kickoff_txid. Binding: payout_txid marked is_payout_handled == the payout_txid that is canonically confirmed at Reimburse time. Impact: Critical - stale attribution in the withdrawals table (`kickoff_txid` pointing to an orphaned payout's kickoff) can leave the operator who funded the now-canonical payout without a `get_handled_payout_kickoff_txid` match, permanently blocking their Reimburse path. Proof idea: cargo test simulating a reorg in core/src/test/reorg-style tests where two conflicting payout txs for the same withdrawal UTXO are alternately confirmed, and asserting `PayoutCheckerTask::run_once`/`mark_payout_handled` reconciles to the currently-canonical payout_txid rather than a now-orphaned one.
