# Q5524: send-tokens via collateral-remove-redeem: judge a position against an LTV belonging to a different a

## Question
Does `collateral-remove-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1211) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the zToken/underlying id mapping reached (the u100 sentinel branch) reach `send-tokens` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-market-vault.clar:259) in a state where it judge a position against an LTV belonging to a different asset set? - Exploit idea: `send-tokens` pushes an asset to a caller-chosen recipient principal. Reach it through `collateral-remove-redeem` and judge a position against an LTV belonging to a different asset set.

- Expected Immunefi impact: Critical - direct theft of another user's collateral
