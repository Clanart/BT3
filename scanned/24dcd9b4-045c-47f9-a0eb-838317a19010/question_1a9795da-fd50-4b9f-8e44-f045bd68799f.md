Target: `Verifier::is_deposit_valid`, specifically the block-height check `(block_height as u32) < start_height` (core/src/verifier.rs). Attacker action: broadcast a deposit funding tx to the correct `create_taproot_address(get_deposit_scripts(..))` script_pubkey with `value==bridge_amount` on a chain tip that later gets reorganized, submitting `NewDeposit` while the tx is confirmed at height >= start_height on a soon-to-be-orphaned branch. Preconditions: attacker can cause a shallow reorg (their own mined blocks on regtest, or race a natural reorg) right after confirmation but before deposit_finalize completes. Call sequence: `is_deposit_valid` reads `tx_info.blockhash`/`get_block_info(&blockhash).height` from `Rpc` at validation time only, no re-check occurs later in `deposit_finalize`/`send_move_to_vault_tx`. Binding: the deposit outpoint verifiers sign against == a `bridge_amount` output that remains confirmed and unspent on the canonical chain when the move tx is later broadcast, but here the underlying funding tx could vanish from the canonical chain after signatures are produced. Impact: Critical, N-of-N partial signatures produced for a spend (move-to-vault) of a deposit outpoint that Citrea's Bridge contract never actually receives real BTC for, while a fabricated move txid still gets broadcastable signatures. Proof idea: cargo test in core/src/test using regtest reorg helpers (`bitcoin_syncer` test utils) to confirm a deposit tx, run is_deposit_valid+deposit_sign, then reorg the block out, and assert the aggregator still holds/can produce a valid N-of-N signature for a move tx spending a since-reorged-out outpoint.
