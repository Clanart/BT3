## Question
Does `collateral-add` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1020) let an unprivileged attacker who controls the position's existing collateral and debt composition reach `price-resolve` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:373) in a state where it apply a transform after the gate that was supposed to bound its output?
