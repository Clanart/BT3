## Question

- Exploit idea: `send-tokens` pushes an asset to a caller-chosen recipient principal. Reach it through `supply-collateral-add` and judge a position against an LTV belonging to a different asset set.

- Expected Immunefi impact: High - permanent freezing of unclaimed yield
