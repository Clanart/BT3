In `verify_storage_proofs` (circuits-lib/src/bridge_circuit/storage_proof.rs), `storage_proof.index` is attacker-influenceable indirectly via which withdrawal the attacker registers with Citrea and later the storage_proof supplied by the operator/host references `storage_proof.index`. Preconditions: withdrawal index i has been registered and used to build a payout; the storage proof for index i is well-formed. Call sequence: an operator constructs a `BridgeCircuitInput` where `storage_proof.index` refers to withdrawal slot i but the `payout_input_index`-referenced UTXO in `payout_spv.transaction` actually corresponds to a different withdrawal slot j whose registered UTXO happens to have colliding txid bytes truncated the same way. Binding: `wd_outpoint`/`move_txid` derived from `storage_proof.index` == the withdrawal outpoint that the `payout_spv.transaction.input[payout_input_index]` actually spends. Impact: Critical - a Groth16 journal (bridge circuit output) could be accepted asserting the wrong withdrawal was fronted for a given deposit's `move_txid`, letting a false claim reach the disprove/reimburse decision. Proof idea: circuits-lib cargo test building two storage proofs for adjacent indices with crafted keys, and asserting the `assert_eq!(user_wd_txid, ...)` and `assert_eq!(vout, ...)` checks at circuits-lib/src/bridge_circuit/mod.rs lines 190-204 actually catch any index/outpoint mismatch, rejecting rather than accepting.
