# Q2583: SerializeG1Point: canon-roundtrip via VerifyBlobKZGProof [when/the]

## Question

Verify this specifically the boundary case must match the spec output, and assert `the deserialization success flag` equals `whether the bytes are the unique canonical subgroup/scalar encoding`.
