Attacker action: without any certificate, call `Setup` immediately followed by `NewDeposit` for a deposit outpoint the attacker funded with the *minimum* `bridge_amount`, racing a legitimate large deposit that a real user has just funded and whose `NewDeposit` call the aggregator-operator tooling is about to issue. Binding: the deposit outpoint whose move-to-vault tx is signed first == the deposit outpoint the intended caller (aggregator operator, on behalf of the real depositor) submitted first; an unauthenticated attacker can violate ordering assumed by any timeout-bound logic downstream. Impact: High - unauthenticated call mutates protocol scheduling state, potentially delaying a legitimate deposit's move tx past a timeout it depends on (`SETUP_COMPLETION_TIMEOUT`/`OVERALL_DEPOSIT_TIMEOUT`). Proof idea: cargo test issuing an uncertified `NewDeposit` immediately before a legitimate one and asserting whether the legitimate deposit's `create_move_to_vault_txhandler` signing completes within `OVERALL_DEPOSIT_TIMEOUT`.
