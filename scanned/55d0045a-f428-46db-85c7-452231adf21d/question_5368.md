## Question
rewards/MasterMagpie.sol: _multiClaim() reads tokenToPoolInfo[_stakingToken] for arbitrary caller-supplied addresses with no registeredToken membership check, so a never-added address yields a zero PoolInfo whose rewarder and accMGPPerShare are zero while the classification branches (vlmgp / MPGRewardPool / default) still execute. ## Target
