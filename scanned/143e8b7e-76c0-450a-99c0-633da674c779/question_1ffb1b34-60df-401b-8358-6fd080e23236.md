Attacker action: register enough `withdraw` calls on the Citrea Bridge contract (or exploit an aggregator-selected `deposit_index` near `u32::MAX/2`) so that a legitimate withdrawal's `deposit_index` makes `ind * 2` wrap around in release-mode arithmetic. Preconditions: withdrawal count on the Bridge contract can reach values where `ind*2` overflows `u32`; operator calls `get_storage_proof(l2_height, deposit_index)` for that withdrawal. Impact: Critical, BTC leaving a move-to-vault UTXO without a matching fronted withdrawal, since the aliased slot's UTXO/vout gets attributed to withdrawal i.
