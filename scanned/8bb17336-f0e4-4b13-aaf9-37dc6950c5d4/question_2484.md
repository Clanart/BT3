## Question
Does `collateral-remove-redeem` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:1211) let an unprivileged attacker who controls `amount` used for BOTH the collateral removal and the share redemption reach `resolve-ztoken` (mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar:343) in a state where it judge a position against an LTV belonging to a different asset set?
