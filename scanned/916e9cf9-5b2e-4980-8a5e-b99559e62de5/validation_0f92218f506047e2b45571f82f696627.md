### Title
Withdrawn stake accounts lose already-calculated epoch rewards because `StakesCache::check_and_store` purges zero-lamport entries before partitioned distribution runs - (File: `runtime/src/stakes.rs`, `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/distribution.rs`)

### Summary
Agave's partitioned epoch rewards mechanism computes stake rewards once, at the epoch boundary, into a `PartitionedStakeRewards` list, and then pays them out over many subsequent blocks (`REWARD_CALCULATION_NUM_BLOCKS`/`num_partitions`). At payout time, `build_updated_stake_reward` looks the stake account back up in the *live* `StakesCache` by pubkey. If a staker fully deactivates and withdraws their stake account in a block that falls between the calculation slot and the block in which their particular reward partition is processed, `StakesCache::check_and_store` unconditionally removes that pubkey's entry from the cache once its lamports hit zero. When that pubkey's partition is later processed, the lookup fails, and the reward that was already earned and calculated for that staker is silently discarded (`stake_reward_lamports_burned`) instead of being paid — mirroring the report's bug class where an entity with a legitimate pending claim is excluded from distribution because a generic "is-empty" cleanup rule already removed its tracking entry.

### Finding Description
`StakesCache::check_and_store` removes any stake or vote account from the in-memory cache the moment its lamport balance reaches zero: [1](#0-0) 

Reward calculation happens once, at the first block of a new epoch, and produces a `PartitionedStakeRewards` list that gets split across a number of subsequent blocks for actual payout: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

When each partition is processed (potentially many blocks after calculation), `build_updated_stake_reward` re-fetches the stake account from the *current* `StakesCache` snapshot by pubkey: [4](#0-3) 

If the account is not present, `DistributionError::AccountNotFound` is returned, and `store_stake_accounts_in_partition` converts the already-calculated reward into a **burned** reward rather than paying it to anyone: [5](#0-4) 

The code's own comment asserts this should be unreachable ("there should never be rewards burned") because it assumes stake-program restrictions prevent further mutation of a stake account during the reward interval: [6](#0-5) 

However, there is no such enforced restriction in the codebase. A test explicitly documents and validates that ordinary transactions (including transfers into stake accounts) are freely processed throughout the entire reward-distribution period: [7](#0-6) 

No `RewardInterval`/`get_reward_interval()` check exists anywhere in transaction admission, scheduling, or the stake program to block a `Withdraw` (or `Deactivate` + subsequent `Withdraw` at the epoch boundary) instruction from a staker during the distribution window; `RewardInterval` is only used internally within `partitioned_epoch_rewards/mod.rs` to drive the bank's own calculation/distribution state machine, not to gate incoming user transactions. Consequently, an unprivileged staker who deactivates and withdraws their entire stake balance to zero between the epoch's calculation slot and the block containing their assigned reward partition removes their own entry from `StakesCache`, and their pre-computed, cap-accounted reward is silently burned instead of delivered — exactly the "removed from tracked collection due to a stale emptiness check while a pending claim still exists" pattern from the LiquidationPool report.

### Impact Explanation
The affected staker permanently loses inflation rewards (and, if `block_revenue_sharing` is enabled, block rewards) that were already calculated and reserved for them as part of that epoch's capitalization increase — no other account receives these lamports; they are simply excluded from `stake_reward_lamports_minted` and instead counted in `stake_reward_lamports_burned`, i.e., real economic loss of legitimately earned yield for an ordinary, unprivileged user performing a completely normal action (withdrawing their own stake). This is a false-accounting/fund-loss condition triggered without any malicious actor, privileged access, or protocol violation, matching the report's "loss of unclaimed yield" impact category.

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition is trivial and requires no special timing luck for the attacker/victim: it happens whenever a staker fully deactivates and withdraws a stake account any time during the (multi-block) reward distribution window for that epoch, before their specific reward partition (deterministically hashed by `epoch_rewards_hasher::hash_rewards_into_partitions`) is processed. Since partitions can span up to `num_partitions` blocks and any staker can query/estimate roughly when their assigned partition lands, or simply withdraw immediately at the epoch boundary before distribution completes, this is easily and repeatably triggerable by any wallet holder using only standard `Deactivate`/`Withdraw` stake instructions.

### Recommendation
Before discarding a reward as `AccountNotFound`, check whether the target pubkash was removed solely due to a legitimate zero-balance withdrawal that occurred after the reward was calculated but before distribution, and pay the reward to the withdrawing owner (e.g., by crediting the withdrawal destination, or by delaying/blocking full stake-account closure while a partitioned reward is still outstanding for that pubkey — a mechanism analogous to SIMD-0123's `pending_delegator_rewards` guard already used in the vote program's `withdraw` function (see `programs/vote/src/vote_state/mod.rs`, lines 1084–1122) to prevent closing vote accounts with outstanding pending rewards). At minimum, the code's assumption that "there should never be rewards burned" should be enforced by an actual guard rather than left as an unvalidated comment.

### Proof of Concept
1. At slot `S` (last slot of epoch `N`), the bank computes and caches `PartitionedStakeRewards` for all currently-delegated stake accounts, including staker `Alice`'s account `A`, and schedules payout across `num_partitions` subsequent blocks starting at `distribution_starting_block_height` [2](#0-1) .
2. `hash_rewards_into_partitions` deterministically assigns `A`'s reward to partition `k` (block `distribution_starting_block_height + k`) [8](#0-7) .
3. Before block `distribution_starting_block_height + k` lands, Alice submits ordinary `Deactivate` then `Withdraw` instructions to fully empty account `A` (this succeeds unimpeded, as demonstrated by `test_rewards_period_system_transfer`, which explicitly shows normal transactions processing throughout the reward interval) [9](#0-8) .
4. `update_stakes_cache` → `StakesCache::check_and_store` observes `A`'s lamports are now `0` and removes `A` from the cache [1](#0-0) .
5. When block `distribution_starting_block_height + k` executes, `build_updated_stake_reward` looks up `A` in `stakes_cache_accounts` and fails, returning `DistributionError::AccountNotFound` [4](#0-3) ; `store_stake_accounts_in_partition` then adds Alice's reward amount to `stake_reward_lamports_burned` instead of paying her [10](#0-9) .
6. Alice's already-earned reward for epoch `N` is permanently lost.
