Attacker action: broadcast/observe a mined payout transaction, then submit to `bridge_circuit` a `BridgeCircuitInput.payout_spv.transaction` (a `CircuitTransaction` reconstructed via attacker-controlled Borsh bytes) that is byte-identical in version/inputs/outputs/locktime to the mined transaction but carries a different (never-broadcast, never-consensus-valid) witness. Preconditions: existing mined payout tx for a real withdrawal. Call sequence: `SPV::verify` -> `mid_state_txid()` (witness ignored) -> `get_root` succeeds against real `block_header.merkle_root` and real `mmr_inclusion_proof`. Binding: `payout_spv.transaction` (including witness) == the transaction that actually satisfied Bitcoin consensus in that block; broken because witness is unconstrained by the leaf hash. Impact: High — a forged witness is treated by the circuit as authenticated by SPV inclusion though it was never validated on-chain, which could feed a wrong signature/annex assumption into the challenge-response flow.
