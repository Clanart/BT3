### Title
Bribe reward sniping via instantaneous `vote()`/`unvote()` around `castVotes()` reward distribution allows theft of unclaimed bribe yield - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol, rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol, rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol)

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager` lets any account adjust its "vote weight" (effectively a stake balance in the corresponding `BribeRewardPool`) with no cooldown, and bribe rewards harvested by `castVotes()` are injected into the reward pool as an instantaneous lump-sum increase to `rewardPerTokenStored`, proportional to whoever is currently staked/voted at that exact moment. This lets a user vote immediately before `castVotes()`/`castVotesAndClaimBribes()` is called, claim their pro-rata share of the just-harvested bribes, and immediately unvote — capturing rewards that should have accrued only to users who kept their votes in place for the full period since the last harvest.

### Finding Description
`WombatBribeManager.vote()` calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` for positive deltas, and `unvote()`/`vote()` with a negative delta calls `withdrawFor()`, with no time lock or minimum holding period. [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

`castVotes()`/`castVotesAndClaimBribes()` harvests bribes from the Wombat `Voter` and forwards them via `_forwardRewards` into `queueNewRewards()` on each pool's `BribeRewardPool`. [3](#0-2) 

`BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward` (invoked by `queueNewRewards`) applies the entire harvested amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` in one shot, based on `totalStaked()` at that instant — it is not streamed over time: [4](#0-3) 

`BribeRewardPool.stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` immediately update the user's balance and settle rewards via the `updateRewards` modifier, using `earned()` = `balance * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

Because `rewardPerTokenStored` only jumps at the moment `queueNewRewards` is called (i.e., when `castVotes` harvests bribes), and a user's earned reward is simply `balance * Δ(rewardPerTokenStored)` since their last update, any account can:
1. Call `vote()` with a positive delta right before (or in the same transaction via `voteAndCast`) `castVotes()` executes, instantly increasing their `BribeRewardPool` balance.
2. Let `castVotes()` harvest bribes and inject the lump sum into `rewardPerTokenStored`.
3. Immediately call `claimBribe()`/`getReward()` to receive their pro-rata share of the full harvested reward.
4. Immediately call `vote()`/`unvote()` with a negative delta to withdraw their vote weight.

No checkpoint or "held since last harvest" gating exists in `poolInfos`, `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp`, or the reward pool's `Reward` struct — only the balance at the exact block of `queueNewRewards` matters. This mirrors the root cause of the referenced Velodrome H-05: rewards are attributed based on the state at reward-settlement time rather than continuously accrued exposure over the full period, so an attacker can inflate their apparent contribution instantaneously and withdraw immediately after being paid, diluting rewards that honest long-term voters (who genuinely locked/voted for the entire period) should have received in full.

### Impact Explanation
This directly steals unclaimed bribe yield from legitimate long-term voters: their expected share of the harvested bribe reward is diluted by the newly-added, momentarily-staked "vote," and the sniper walks away with a portion of the rewards without ever bearing the opportunity cost of voting during the period the bribes accrued. Since `vote`/`unvote`/`castVotes` are all normal unprivileged-wallet-reachable functions (and can even be combined into a single `voteAndCast` transaction), this is directly exploitable by any user, including via flash-borrowed or freshly-locked vlMGP if `getUserVotable` permits it.

### Likelihood Explanation
High. No special privileges, oracle manipulation, or governance access is required — only calling `vote()`/`voteAndCast()` right before a `castVotes()` call (which itself is a permissionless, incentivized action anyone can trigger) and then `unvote()`/`vote()` again afterward. Because `castVotes` timing is often predictable/mempool-visible and can even be composed atomically by the caller invoking `voteAndCast`, the attack is straightforward to execute deterministically.

### Recommendation
Do not settle the full harvested bribe amount instantly against current balances. Instead, stream newly queued rewards over a fixed duration (Synthetix-style `rewardRate`/`periodFinish`) so that `rewardPerTokenStored` increases continuously over time rather than in a single jump tied to `totalStaked()` at the harvest instant. Alternatively/additionally, enforce a minimum holding period (checkpoint-based, similar to the "voted through the full period" intent in the referenced report) before a user's vote weight qualifies for a given bribe distribution, and reject `vote`/`unvote` calls that fall within one block or one epoch of a `castVotes` execution for the same account.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker holds locked vlMGP and calls `WombatBribeManager.vote(lps, [+X])`, which calls `BribeRewardPool.stakeFor(attacker, X)` — instantly raising `totalStaked()` and the attacker's `balanceOf`. [7](#0-6) 
2. Attacker (or anyone) calls `castVotes()`/`castVotesAndClaimBribes()`, which harvests Wombat bribes and calls `queueNewRewards`, bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` by `amount * 1e18 / totalStaked()` — attributing a share to the attacker's freshly added balance. [8](#0-7) 
3. Attacker calls `claimBribe(lps)` → `getReward` → `_getReward`, receiving `earned = X * Δ(rewardPerToken)` even though `X` was staked for zero duration during bribe accrual. [9](#0-8) 
4. Attacker calls `unvote(lp)` (or `vote` with `-X`), instantly withdrawing the vote weight with no penalty or lockup. [2](#0-1) 
5. Net effect: attacker captured a share of the harvested bribes proportional to `X`, diluting the payout to genuine long-term voters who held their votes for the entire accrual period, at zero cost/risk to the attacker.

    function _getReward(address _account) internal virtual {
        uint256 length = rewardTokens.length;
        for (uint256 index = 0; index < length; ++index) {
            address rewardToken = rewardTokens[index];
            uint256 reward = earned(_account, rewardToken);
            if (reward > 0) {

                IERC20(rewardToken).safeTransfer(_account, reward);
