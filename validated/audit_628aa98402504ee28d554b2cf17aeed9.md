### Title
`resolve-ztoken`'s unguarded `(* p cached-lindex)` can abort on uint overflow once a vault's liquidity index grows unboundedly, permanently freezing zToken-collateralized positions - ([File: mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar])

### Summary
`resolve-ztoken` computes `(* p cached-lindex)` with no overflow protection, where `p` is the already-normalized underlying price (8-decimal fixed point) and `cached-lindex` is the vault's liquidity index (`INDEX-PRECISION` = 1e12 base, monotonically non-decreasing via compounding interest accrual). Since neither `lindex` nor the interest-rate points that drive its growth are capped, and there is no periodic re-basing of `lindex` back toward `INDEX-PRECISION`, sustained compounding at any nonzero rate over a sufficiently long time horizon drives `cached-lindex` toward Clarity's `u128` ceiling, at which point the multiplication traps and the entire health-check/price-resolution path aborts.

### Finding Description
The price path for a zToken collateral asset is: `price-multi-resolve` -> `price-resolve` -> `resolve-callcode` -> `resolve-ztoken`. In `resolve-ztoken`: [1](#0-0) 
`p` here is a normalized oracle price (for a BTC-denominated asset, `~1e8` scale times BTC's dollar value, i.e. order `1e12`–`1e13`). `cached-lindex` comes from `get-cached-indexes`/`accrue-and-cache`, which is simply whatever `lindex` the vault (e.g. `v0-vault-ststx`) currently holds: [2](#0-1) 

`lindex` in the vault is updated every accrual via `next-liquidity-index` / `calc-index-next`, and is never capped or reset: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Every call to `deposit`, `redeem`, `system-borrow`, `system-repay`, etc. invokes `accrue`, which compounds `lindex` by the current liquidity rate over the elapsed time delta: [5](#0-4) 
This is a geometric (compounding) process — each accrual multiplies the running `lindex` by `(1 + rate * dt / year)`. Over many accrual periods, this compounds toward `e^(rate*T)`, and there is no upper clamp on `rate` beyond the packed `u16` (0–65535 bps ≈ 0–655% APY) DAO-configured interest curve, and no clamp on `lindex` itself. Given `p ~ 1e13` and Clarity's `MAX-U128 ≈ 3.4e38`, overflow of `(* p cached-lindex)` occurs once `cached-lindex` exceeds roughly `3.4e25` (vs. its starting value of `INDEX-PRECISION = 1e12`), i.e. a ~3.4e13x growth. At sustained rates in the tens-to-hundreds of percent APY, this magnitude of compounding is reachable over a multi-decade horizon purely through normal, permissionless protocol usage (any user's deposit/redeem calls drive accrual) — no privileged action is required, only elapsed chain time and continued high-utilization interest accrual.

None of the downstream gates catch this: `resolve-callcode`, `price-resolve`, and `price-multi-resolve` only check freshness/confidence/positivity of the *resolved* price — they never get a value because the multiplication itself traps the transaction before returning. There is no `try!`-recoverable path; a raw arithmetic overflow in Clarity aborts the whole transaction.

### Impact Explanation
This is a Soundness/availability bug, not a mispricing-direction bug: once triggered, `resolve-ztoken` aborts rather than returning a wrong price, so any transaction requiring pricing of zstSTX (or other zToken) collateral — health checks, borrows, withdrawals, liquidations — reverts unconditionally. If a position collateralized by the affected zToken is already unhealthy at the time this state is reached, it becomes permanently unliquidatable, which is a protocol-insolvency-class Critical impact; otherwise it results in permanent freezing of that collateral type across the whole market (High, escalating to Critical if bad debt accumulates because liquidation is blocked market-wide for that asset).

### Likelihood Explanation
Reaching the overflow threshold requires the vault's `lindex` to compound roughly 13 orders of magnitude beyond `INDEX-PRECISION`, which — even at aggressive DAO-configured rates (up to the packed max of ~655% APY) — takes on the order of decades of continuous protocol operation with sustained high utilization and no intervening index reset. No attacker action can accelerate real chain time; this is a long-horizon design/liveness bug rather than an immediately exploitable one, since Stacks block time cannot be manipulated by an unprivileged principal. However, it is deterministic and unavoidable given the architecture: `lindex` is monotonic and uncapped, so absent a fix (e.g., re-basing, `lindex` normalization, or checked/saturating arithmetic), the overflow is a certainty over a sufficiently long protocol lifetime, and could occur far sooner if the DAO sets unusually high rate points during periods of high utilization.

### Recommendation
Add an explicit overflow-safe computation in `resolve-ztoken` (e.g., reorder the multiplication/division to `mul-div-down` style scaling that avoids intermediate `u128` overflow, or cap `lindex` growth and periodically re-base the index, minting/socializing the accrued difference so `lindex` stays within a safe bound relative to `INDEX-PRECISION`). At minimum, bound the interest-rate points (`points-rate`) to a sane maximum APY at the vault level so that even after decades of continuous compounding, `lindex * p` cannot approach `MAX-U128`.

### Proof of Concept
Clarinet simnet test plan:
1. Deploy `v0-vault-ststx` and `v0-4-market`, initialize the vault.
2. Set `points-ir` (via DAO) to the maximum packable rate (65535 bps ≈ 655% APY) at 100% utilization by borrowing up to `cap-debt`.
3. Repeatedly warp `stacks-block-time` forward (simulate many years, e.g. thousands of iterations of ~1-year jumps) and call `deposit`/`redeem` (or `accrue` directly) each time to force compounding of `lindex`, without ever calling through the market's `accrue-and-cache`.
4. After enough iterations, call `get-liquidity-index` to confirm `lindex` is now within striking distance (or already an unrepresentable large value that, multiplied by a BTC-like price ~1e13, would exceed `MAX-U128`).
5. Configure a zstSTX collateral position and, via the market's health-check entrypoint (which calls `price-multi-resolve` -> `resolve-ztoken`), assert the transaction aborts (arithmetic overflow runtime error) instead of returning a price, demonstrating the position becomes permanently unpriceable.
