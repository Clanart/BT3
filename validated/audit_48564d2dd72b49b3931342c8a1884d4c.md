### Title
Unbounded AMM price manipulation of the reward swap in `incentiveDeposit(_bullMode=true)` lets an attacker extract inflated bonus MGP from the protocol's reserve - ([File: wombat/ArbWomUp2.sol])

### Summary
`ArbWomUp2._bullMGP` swaps a fixed BUSD reward amount for MGP via a plain AMM router call with a caller-supplied `_minRec` (from `_minMGPRec`), then locks `amounts[1] * (DENOMINATOR + bullBonusRatio) / DENOMINATOR` MGP to the caller. Because the slippage floor is fully attacker-controlled and there is no oracle/TWAP check, an attacker can manipulate the BUSD/MGP pool immediately before their own `incentiveDeposit(_bullMode=true)` call to obtain an inflated `amounts[1]`, and the `bullBonusRatio` multiplier then compounds that manipulated value into extra real MGP locked to them, drained from the contract's own MGP holdings.

### Finding Description
`incentiveDeposit` computes a fixed BUSD `rewardToSend` from the attacker's WOM deposit tier (independent of MGP price) and, in bull mode, forwards it to `_bullMGP`: [2](#0-1) . Inside `_bullMGP`, the BUSD is swapped for MGP through `ROUTER.swapExactTokensForTokens(_busdAmount, _minRec, path, address(this), block.timestamp)`, and the resulting `amounts[1]` is multiplied by `(DENOMINATOR + bullBonusRatio)/DENOMINATOR` before being approved and locked to the attacker via `vlMGP.lockFor`: [3](#0-2) .

`_minMGPRec` is a parameter fully chosen by the caller, so it cannot serve as protocol-side manipulation protection — it only protects the caller from an unfavorable execution price, not the protocol from a favorable one. An attacker can, within the same transaction (or via classic sandwich/flash-loan technique), push the BUSD/MGP pool price so that MGP is temporarily cheap relative to BUSD, execute `incentiveDeposit(_bullMode=true)` so the fixed BUSD reward buys an inflated `amounts[1]`, then restore the pool price afterward. The `bullBonusRatio` bonus is applied on top of this manipulated `amounts[1]`, further amplifying the excess MGP locked. `vlMGP.lockFor` performs a real `safeTransferFrom` of MGP from `ArbWomUp2` (confirmed in `VLMGP._lock`: [1](#0-0) ), so the excess locked MGP is backed by real tokens taken from `ArbWomUp2`'s pre-funded MGP reserve, i.e., real value extracted at the expense of the protocol/other future bull-mode participants who share that same reserve and BUSD pool. No modifier, oracle check, or TWAP protects against this; `nonReentrant`/`whenNotPaused` do not address price manipulation.

### Impact Explanation
This is a real economic loss: an attacker can drain more MGP from `ArbWomUp2`'s bonus reserve than their true BUSD-reward tier entitles them to, at the expense of the protocol's pre-funded MGP and the fairness of allocation to other WOM depositors sharing the same reward pool — matching the "theft of unclaimed yield / protocol insolvency" impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition is only capital and access to the BUSD/MGP AMM pool (e.g., via flash loan), both of which are available to any unprivileged actor; the attacker fully controls `_minMGPRec` for their own call, so no protocol-imposed slippage bound blocks the manipulated price. This is repeatable each time the attacker (or any WOM depositor) has BUSD reward eligible for bull-mode conversion and the pool has exploitable depth/thinness.

### Recommendation
Do not let a caller-supplied slippage parameter be the only protection for a protocol-funded swap; enforce a protocol-side maximum acceptable output (or minimum, since receiving "too much" MGP is the actual attack vector) derived from a TWAP/oracle-based fair price, and cap/clamp `amounts[1]` against that fair value before applying `bullBonusRatio`. Alternatively, remove attacker control over swap execution price by using a trusted price oracle to size the swap, or eliminate the multiplicative post-swap bonus on raw AMM output entirely (compute bonus MGP from the BUSD input's oracle value, not from the router's realized output).

### Proof of Concept
Foundry fork test plan:
1. Fork BSC mainnet at a block where the real BUSD/MGP Pancake pool exists (or deploy a local Uniswap-V2-style pair with realistic liquidity and seed `ArbWomUp2` with `busd`, WOM deposit tier reward, and a pre-funded MGP bonus reserve, matching `setup()`).
2. As the attacker EOA: deposit enough WOM via `_deposit`-triggering path to accrue a known fixed BUSD `rewardToSend` (call `getRewardAmount` to record expected value).
3. Before calling `incentiveDeposit`, have the attacker (optionally via a flash loan) sell MGP into the pool to depress its BUSD price.
4. Call `incentiveDeposit(_amount, _minMGPRec=0 or very low, _bullMode=true)`; record `mgpAmountToLcok` from the `VLMGPRewarded` event.
5. Immediately after, have the attacker buy back MGP to restore the pool price, and compute their net profit (MGP bonus value received minus manipulation cost/fees).
6. Assert: `mgpAmountToLcok` (locked to attacker) is measurably greater than `fairMarketMGP = rewardToSend / preManipulationBUSDPerMGP * (DENOMINATOR+bullBonusRatio)/DENOMINATOR`, and that the attacker's net MGP+fee profit is positive, proving value extraction beyond their true BUSD-reward entitlement, funded from `ArbWomUp2`'s MGP balance.
