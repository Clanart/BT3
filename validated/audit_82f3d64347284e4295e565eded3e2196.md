### Title
`ReferralStorage.trigger` credits both referrer and referee as an unfunded add-on, growing rewardAmount liabilities without a corresponding MGP inflow - (File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol)

### Summary
`trigger()` computes `refererAmount` and `refereeAmount` as percentages of the referee's *total claimed reward* and adds both amounts to `refererInfo.rewardAmount` and `refereeInfo.rewardAmount` [1](#0-0) . Because `MasterMagpie._multiClaim` invokes `IReferralStorage(referral).trigger(_user, totalReward)` for every claim by a referred user regardless of who calls the multiclaim path [2](#0-1) , and because both referrer and referee sides are additive top-ups rather than a split carved out of `_amount`, each referred claim strictly increases the MGP the contract will owe via `claimReward()` [3](#0-2)  without ReferralStorage's own MGP balance increasing to match.

### Finding Description
`useCode`/`registerCode` are fully permissionless, letting an attacker set up a referrer/referee pair under their own control (two self-owned addresses, since direct self-referral is blocked by the `Circled` check) [4](#0-3) . The referee address then stakes and repeatedly claims MGP rewards through `MasterMagpie`'s claim/multiclaim flow, which unconditionally calls `trigger(_user, totalReward)` whenever a referral exists [5](#0-4) .

Inside `trigger()`, `refererAmount` and `refereeAmount` are each derived from `_amount` (the referee's full claimed reward) times a tier/boost-based percentage, and *both* are added on top to the respective `rewardAmount` ledgers [1](#0-0) . Neither amount is deducted from the referee's own claim, nor is there any check that `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))` in `ReferralStorage` grows proportionally. `claimReward()` simply transfers out `userInfo.rewardAmount` in MGP with no solvency check against total outstanding liabilities [3](#0-2) . Because the harvesting/claim loop in `_multiClaim` can be triggered repeatedly for the same staked position, and the referral bookkeeping in `trigger()` has no cap or funding-source linkage, an attacker can accumulate arbitrarily large `rewardAmount` entries for both self-controlled referrer and referee accounts purely from ordinary staking/claiming activity, with no additional deposit of MGP into `ReferralStorage` to back it.

### Impact Explanation
Every referred claim strictly grows the sum of `rewardAmount` balances recorded in `ReferralStorage` without any matching increase in the contract's MGP token balance beyond whatever was pre-funded by governance. Once cumulative `rewardAmount` claims exceed the actual MGP held by `ReferralStorage`, legitimate referrers/referees will be unable to redeem their earned rewards via `claimReward()` — a reachable insolvency condition for the referral reward pool, since the accrual mechanism is unbounded and additive on both sides rather than being carved out of a fixed, already-funded amount.

### Likelihood Explanation
The path requires no privileged role: `registerCode`, `useCode`, staking, and claiming (which drives `trigger`) are all open to any EOA. An attacker only needs two addresses under their own control and capital they already own (staked tokens generating MGP emissions) to repeatedly trigger reward accrual. The only precondition is a non-trivial `sharePercent`/tier `rewardPercentage` configuration (an admin setting, not attacker-controlled, but plausible/likely in production), making the issue latent but readily exploitable once the referral program is live and referral tiers/`sharePercent` are non-zero.

### Recommendation
Redesign `trigger()` so that `refererAmount` and `refereeAmount` are carved out of `_amount` (i.e., deducted from what the referee actually receives) rather than added on top, ensuring the referral split never exceeds the reward that was actually paid out of the emission budget. Alternatively, fund `ReferralStorage`'s referral bonuses from a dedicated, capped emissions bucket that is decremented as `trigger()` accrues rewards, and add a check in `claimReward()`/`trigger()` that prevents total outstanding `rewardAmount` liabilities from exceeding `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy/attach to `ReferralStorage` with `sharePercent` and a tier `rewardPercentage` set to non-zero values (e.g., simulate the intended production config).
2. Attacker address `A` calls `registerCode(codeA)`.
3. Attacker address `B` calls `useCode(codeA)`, establishing `myReferer[B] = A`.
4. `B` stakes MGP-earning tokens in `MasterMagpie`, then repeatedly calls the multiclaim path (`multiclaim`/`multiclaimFor`) to accrue and claim MGP rewards, each call invoking `ReferralStorage.trigger(B, totalReward)`.
5. After N iterations, assert:
   - `userInfos[A].rewardAmount + userInfos[B].rewardAmount` (referral liabilities) has grown by `N * (refererAmount + refereeAmount)`.
   - `MGP.balanceOf(address(ReferralStorage))` has NOT grown by a corresponding amount (no external funding occurred).
   - Once `A` and `B` call `claimReward()`, total transferred MGP exceeds what would be sustainable without additional funding, demonstrating the ledger is unbacked and can outpace the contract's actual MGP balance.
