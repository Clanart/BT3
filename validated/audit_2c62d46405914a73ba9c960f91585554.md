This confirms the mechanism precisely, and it matches the DODORouteProxy bug class closely enough to validate an analog.

`multiclaimOnBehalf` in `MasterMagpie.sol` claims a user's specific rewards and sends them to `msg.sender` (the compounder contract, `ManualCompound`) via `_multiClaim(_stakingTokens, _account, msg.sender, _rewardTokens)` [1](#0-0) , ultimately paying out via `_claimBaseRewarder`/`getReward`/`_sendReward`, which transfer the tracked per-user reward amount into the caller contract [2](#0-1) . But `ManualCompound.compound()` never uses the actual claimed amount — for every processing branch it reads `IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this))` and forwards/locks/converts the *entire* balance to `msg.sender`, both for non-compoundable rewards and for compoundable ones: [3](#0-2) 

This is the same root-cause pattern as the DODORouteProxy report: using the contract's total token balance as the payout amount instead of a per-call, per-user delta. `ManualCompound` is a permissionless, unprivileged entry point (any wallet can call `compound()` with arbitrary `_lps`/`_rewards` arrays for themselves) [4](#0-3) . Any leftover token balance sitting in `ManualCompound` — from a prior partial/failed operation, from a helper/convertor/locker that doesn't consume the full approved balance, or from tokens sent to the contract by mistake — is fully sweepable by the next caller who simply includes that token as one of their claimed reward tokens (or lists it as their reward for any pool that emits it), regardless of whether that dust was ever entitled to them.

### Title
Balance-based reward payout in ManualCompound allows any caller to steal dust/leftover reward tokens - (File: rewards/ManualCompound.sol)

### Summary
`ManualCompound.compound()` computes payout, lock, and convert amounts from `IERC20(token).balanceOf(address(this))` rather than from the amount actually claimed for `msg.sender` in that transaction, letting any unprivileged caller sweep residual token balances left in the contract.

### Finding Description
`compound()` calls `IMasterMagpie(masterMagpie).multiclaimOnBehalf(_lps, _rewards, msg.sender)`, which pulls the caller's specific pending rewards into the `ManualCompound` contract [5](#0-4) . Immediately after, for every requested reward token that is not registered as `compoundableRewards`, the code reads the token's **entire** balance held by the contract and transfers it to `msg.sender`: [6](#0-5) 
Similarly, for every registered compoundable reward, it reads the entire contract balance and forwards it wholesale to a convertor, locker, or helper on behalf of `msg.sender`: [7](#0-6) 
Neither branch scopes the transferred amount to what `multiclaimOnBehalf` actually credited to the current caller in the current transaction. Any pre-existing balance of that token in the contract — accidental transfers, rounding dust from `safeApprove`/`convertFor`/`depositFor` not consuming the full approved amount, or residue from a prior compound call that didn't fully drain — is indistinguishable from the caller's own freshly claimed rewards and gets paid out (or locked/converted) entirely to whoever calls next.

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged wallet can directly steal ERC20 tokens (dust or accidental transfers) that do not belong to them from `ManualCompound`, and in the compoundable branch this dust is also locked/converted and credited to the attacker via `vlMGP.lockFor`/`IConverter.convertFor`, i.e., direct misappropriation of protocol/user reward funds rather than merely being swept to an admin-controlled sink.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: it requires some residual balance to exist in `ManualCompound` (e.g., from a mis-sent transfer, or from downstream helper/convertor/locker contracts not consuming 100% of the approved amount due to rounding). Once dust exists, exploitation is trivial and permissionless — any wallet calls `compound()` referencing the affected token in `_rewards`.

### Recommendation
Track and use per-call claimed deltas (e.g., snapshot balance before `multiclaimOnBehalf` and compute `balanceAfter - balanceBefore`) instead of `balanceOf(address(this))` when determining amounts to transfer, lock, or convert in `compound()`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Some dust/leftover of reward token `T` (registered as a MasterMagpie reward token, and possibly also listed in `ManualCompound.rewards`) accumulates in `ManualCompound`, e.g., via direct transfer or rounding residue from a prior `compound()` call.
2. An attacker with a trivial staked position in any pool that emits `T` as a bonus reward calls `compound(_lps, _rewards, ...)` including `T` in `_rewards[i]`.
3. `multiclaimOnBehalf` credits the attacker's (small) actual pending amount of `T` into the contract.
4. `compound()` reads `IERC20(T).balanceOf(address(this))`, which equals the attacker's small claim **plus** the pre-existing dust, and transfers (or locks/converts) the full sum to the attacker, exceeding their legitimate entitlement.
