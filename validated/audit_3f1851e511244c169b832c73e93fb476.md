## Title
Reward-per-token sniping via permissionless `harvest()` front-running — attacker can deposit before harvest and withdraw immediately after to steal a disproportionate share of newly distributed rewards ([File: wombat/WombatStaking.sol, rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol / BaseRewardPoolV2.sol])

### Summary
`WombatStaking.harvest()` is a permissionless function that pulls pending WOM/bonus rewards from Wombat's `MasterWombat` and immediately pushes them into the relevant `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` via `queueNewRewards()`, which instantly folds the whole reward amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` based on the pool's `totalStaked()` at that exact block [1](#0-0) . Because reward accounting has no time-weighting (no `lastUpdateTime`/`periodFinish` style vesting — it is a lump-sum update at the moment of `queueNewRewards`), an attacker can deposit right before harvest, immediately be credited a full pro-rata share of the harvested rewards, and withdraw right after, extracting yield that should have accrued to long-term stakers.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` in both `BaseRewardPool.sol` and `BaseRewardPoolV2.sol` updates `rewardPerTokenStored` in one shot as soon as new rewards are queued: [2](#0-1) 

This is triggered from `WombatStaking._toMasterWomAndSendReward` → `_sendRewards` → `queueNewRewards`, invoked by the permissionless `harvest(address _lpToken)` entrypoint: [1](#0-0) 

`WombatPoolHelper.deposit()`/`withdraw()` (and the `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw` they route through) have no lockup, cooldown, or exit fee that would prevent depositing and withdrawing in the same or an adjacent transaction/block: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because `rewardPerTokenStored` is only ever bumped at the instant `queueNewRewards` executes (based on `totalStaked()` at that moment), a user who deposits immediately before a harvest and withdraws immediately after captures a full share of rewards that actually accrued from WOM emissions over the *entire* preceding period, none of which they contributed capital toward. This is directly analogous to the report's root cause: value that is conceptually earned continuously (PnL / yield) but applied in a single discrete step (`startNewEpoch()` / `_provisionReward`) is exploitable by an attacker who can predict/observe the triggering transaction and time their deposit/withdraw around it, since `harvest()` is a public, permissionless call anyone can invoke (or front-run) and there's no minimum bonding period between deposit and withdrawal.

### Impact Explanation
This constitutes direct theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate long-term stakers: the sniper's deposit dilutes `totalStaked()` at the harvest moment, inflating their own `rewardPerToken` credit while reducing the effective share retroactively earned by users who were staked throughout the accrual period. Repeated sniping around every harvest event drains yield away from honest LPs/stakers over time.

### Likelihood Explanation
`harvest()` is unauthenticated and callable by any address, and deposit/withdraw functions in `WombatPoolHelper`/`MasterMagpie` have no minimum holding period, so any unprivileged wallet can execute this deposit-harvest-withdraw sequence (either by calling harvest themselves or front-running an existing pending harvest transaction) with ordinary transactions, at negligible cost beyond gas.

### Recommendation
Convert `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`/`mWOMSVBaseRewarder`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder` reward accounting to a time-weighted streaming model (e.g., Synthetix-style `rewardRate` + `periodFinish` + `lastUpdateTime`) instead of instantaneously folding the full reward amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` at the moment of `queueNewRewards`, and/or introduce a minimum staking duration or withdrawal cooldown before a depositor becomes eligible to earn/claim newly queued rewards.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes (or itself triggers) an imminent call to `WombatStaking.harvest(lpToken)` (permissionless).
2. Attacker calls `WombatPoolHelper.deposit()` for that pool just before the harvest transaction lands, staking into `MasterMagpie` via `depositFor`.
3. `harvest()` executes, pulling WOM rewards and calling `queueNewRewards` → `_provisionReward`, which instantly increases `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` including the attacker's freshly-added stake.
4. Attacker immediately calls `WombatPoolHelper.withdraw()` to unstake, having accrued a full pro-rata share of the harvested rewards for a deposit held only momentarily, diluting the rewards other long-term stakers should have received.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
