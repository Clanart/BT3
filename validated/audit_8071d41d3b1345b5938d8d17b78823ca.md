### Title
Reward-per-token sniping via front-run `vote()` / back-run `unvote()` around `castVotes()` – disproportionate bribe theft and vote-weight manipulation - ([File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol], [File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager.vote()`/`unvote()` have no cooldown or time-lock (unlike unlocking MGP itself), and `BaseRewardPool._provisionReward()` credits newly harvested bribe rewards to `rewardPerTokenStored` as an instantaneous lump sum based on `totalStaked()` at the moment of provisioning rather than streaming it over time. An attacker holding already-locked vlMGP can therefore front-run a pending `castVotes()` transaction with a large `vote()` delta and back-run it with `unvote()`, capturing a share of the freshly-harvested bribe reward and skewing the real WOM vote allocation sent to Wombat, all within the same short window, without ever being at economic risk beyond the (already-locked) vlMGP they own.

### Finding Description
`vote()` immediately increases `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` and calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` [1](#0-0) , which in `BribeRewardPool.stakeFor` runs the `updateRewards` modifier first (checkpointing the caller at the *current* `rewardPerTokenStored`) and then bumps `totalSupply` [2](#0-1) .

When `castVotes()` runs, `targetVote` for each pool is computed from `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` and `totalVlMgpInVote`, which already reflect the attacker's just-added vote [3](#0-2) . `wombatStaking.vote(...)` is then called, which harvests bribes from Wombat and (per the `BaseRewardPool`/`BribeRewardPool` reward machinery) provisions the harvested bribe token into the pool rewarder via `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`. Critically, `_provisionReward` adds the entire new reward to `rewardPerTokenStored` in one shot, weighted by whatever `totalStaked()` (i.e., `totalSupply` in `BribeRewardPool`) is **at that instant** — there is no reward-rate/duration streaming mechanism [4](#0-3) . Because the attacker's `stakeFor` already inflated `totalSupply` before this call, their `_balances[attacker]` participates fully in the reward split even though they held the position for only one block.

Immediately after `castVotes()` completes, the attacker calls `unvote()`, which calls `withdrawFor(..., true)` → `updateRewards` checkpoints `earned()` using the now-updated `rewardPerTokenStored`, crediting the attacker's `userRewards` before their balance is zeroed [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) . The attacker can then call `getReward`/`claimBribe` to withdraw the reward token. `earned()` is a straightforward pro-rata computation with no vesting or time-weighting: `balanceOf(account) * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid) / 1e18 + userRewards[account]` [7](#0-6) .

No modifier, `nonReentrant` guard, or time-lock in `vote()`/`unvote()`/`castVotes()` prevents this sandwich — `vote()` only checks `userTotalVotedInVlmgp[msg.sender] <= getUserVotable(msg.sender)` (i.e., that the caller actually owns/locked enough vlMGP) [8](#0-7) ; it does not check for minimum holding duration. Locking MGP into vlMGP itself has no additional delay before voting rights are usable (only *unlocking* MGP later has a cooldown, per `startUnlock` [9](#0-8) ), so an attacker who already holds locked vlMGP (from a prior lock, no minimum holding period required to vote) can freely reallocate their entire vote weight between pools every block at zero cost.

Because `castVotes()` also forwards `votes[i]` (computed from the inflated `totalVoteInVlmgp`) to `wombatStaking.vote()`, which casts real votes on the underlying Wombat `Voter` contract, the manipulation also skews the actual on-chain WOM emission-vote allocation for that epoch/cast in favor of the pool the attacker briefly voted for.

Note: I was unable to fully trace `WombatStaking.vote()`'s internal call into `queueNewRewards` within the available iterations, so the exact harvest-then-provision ordering inside `WombatStaking` is inferred from the `BaseRewardPool`/`BribeRewardPool` reward-provisioning design rather than directly confirmed line-by-line in that file.

### Impact Explanation
This falls under "theft of unclaimed yield" and "governance voting result manipulation" per the Immunefi impact classes cited in the prompt. An attacker can repeatedly harvest a disproportionate share of newly-distributed bribe rewards relative to their actual time-weighted contribution to a pool's vote, diluting honest long-term voters' share of each `castVotes()` harvest. It also lets the attacker transiently and repeatedly redirect real WOM voting power to whichever pool is about to have its bribe harvested, distorting the protocol's on-chain governance vote allocation.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires the attacker to already hold locked vlMGP (real capital, since MGP locking is permanent until `startUnlock`'s cooldown completes), but crucially does **not** require holding it for any specific duration before voting, and `vote()`/`unvote()` are completely free of cooldowns. The attacker needs only to observe a pending `castVotes()` transaction in the mempool and sandwich it with `vote()`/`unvote()` calls — a purely mechanical MEV/front-running operation requiring no privileged role, repeatable every time `castVotes()` is called (there is no rate-limit preventing this pattern from being executed on every harvest cycle).

### Recommendation
- Add a minimum holding/cooldown period between `vote()` and `unvote()` for the same pool (e.g., require votes to remain in place for at least one `castVotes()` cycle) before rewards or vote weight are recognized.
- Change `BaseRewardPool._provisionReward`/reward accrual to stream rewards over a fixed duration (rewardRate over rewardsDuration, as in standard Synthetix-style `StakingRewards`) instead of crediting the full amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` instantaneously, so momentary stakers cannot capture a full epoch's reward.
- Alternatively, snapshot `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` at the start of each voting epoch and disallow `vote()`/`unvote()` from affecting the current in-flight `castVotes()` calculation.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `VLMGP`, `BribeRewardPool` fork setup with an active pool that has pending Wombat bribes.
2. Have "Honest" user lock MGP and call `vote()` well in advance of `castVotes()`.
3. Simulate a pending `castVotes(false)` transaction in the mempool.
4. "Attacker" (with already-locked vlMGP from a prior `lock()`) calls `vote()` with a large delta on the same pool immediately before the `castVotes()` transaction is mined.
5. Execute `castVotes(false)`, which harvests Wombat bribes and provisions them into the pool's `BribeRewardPool` via `_provisionReward`.
6. Immediately after, Attacker calls `unvote()` then `claimBribe()`/`getReward()`.
7. Assert: Attacker's realized bribe reward (`earned()` at the time of `unvote`) as a fraction of the total harvested bribe exceeds Attacker's time-weighted share of `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` over the epoch (i.e., Attacker held their vote for effectively zero time yet received a full pro-rata share), while Honest's realized share is diluted below their time-weighted contribution.
8. Additionally assert that `votes[i]` sent to `wombatStaking.vote()` during `castVotes()` reflects the Attacker's inflated `totalVoteInVlmgp`, demonstrating skewed on-chain vote allocation.
