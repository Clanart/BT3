### Title
Permissionless `harvestAll()` allows attacker to flash-vote into `delegatedPool` and steal a pro-rata share of previously-accrued bribes from long-term delegators - ([File: rewards/DelegateVoteRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`DelegateVoteRewardPool.harvestAll()` has no access-control or timing restriction and pulls in whatever bribes have already accrued to the pool's aggregate vote weight, then immediately splits them via `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer` using the **current** `totalSupply`. An attacker can call `WombatBribeManager.vote([delegatedPool], [+X])` to instantly inflate `totalSupply` in the same transaction before calling `harvestAll()`, capturing a share of rewards that were earned by other delegators' vote weight prior to the attacker's deposit.

### Finding Description
`DelegateVoteRewardPool.stakeFor` (called via `WombatBribeManager.vote`) increases `totalSupply`/`_balances[attacker]` and sets the attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` to the reward-per-token value *before* any new harvest via the `updateRewards` modifier [1](#0-0) . `harvestAll()` is `external` with no modifier restricting the caller, and pulls bribes that accumulated in the underlying real-pool bribe rewarders based on the DelegateVoteRewardPool's historical vote weight (earned by prior votes/prior delegators, not by the attacker) [2](#0-1) .

`_manageRewards` -> `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer` then increments `rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored` using `totalSupply` **at the time of the call**, i.e., after the attacker's fresh deposit has already been added [3](#0-2) . Because the attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` was checkpointed immediately before this increment (via the `vote()` call's `updateRewards` modifier), the attacker's subsequent `earned()` claim captures `_balances[attacker] * delta / 1e18`, where `delta` is entirely funded by bribes earned by the pool's vote weight *prior* to the attacker joining. No cooldown, vesting, or minimum staking duration exists in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` to prevent this same-block deposit-then-harvest pattern [4](#0-3) .

The `WombatBribeManager.vote` function itself has no restriction preventing an attacker with any amount of vlMGP votable weight from voting into `delegatedPool` [5](#0-4) , and `harvestAll()` can be called by any external account directly, bypassing `castVotes()` entirely [6](#0-5) .

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft of unclaimed yield: existing delegators who had already earned bribes proportional to their vote weight over time have their claimable share diluted the instant a new depositor stakes right before a harvest, transferring value to the attacker without any real economic contribution or time-weighted participation. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack only requires holding vlMGP (locked MGP) votable weight and the ability to call two public functions (`vote` and `harvestAll`) in sequence, with no special privilege, and is repeatable every time meaningful bribes have accrued to the delegate pool's underlying vote weight. The main constraint is that the attacker needs vlMGP voting power and there must be a nonzero pending harvest amount at the time of the attack, but both are attacker-controllable/observable preconditions (attacker can simply wait for/observe pending bribes and act opportunistically).

### Recommendation
Restrict who can trigger `harvestAll()` claim/distribution timing relative to fresh deposits — e.g., checkpoint (harvest) rewards immediately when the reward-affecting event occurs (already partially done via `updateRewards`) but additionally introduce a minimum staking duration or a "reward smoothing"/streaming mechanism instead of instantaneous `rewardPerTokenStored` bump, or require `harvestAll()` to be called before, not after, new `stakeFor` calls are processed within the same block (i.e., checkpoint pending harvest amounts prior to any deposit that would dilute the distribution, such as by harvesting inside `stakeFor`/`_updateVote` before altering `totalSupply`).

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test outline:
1. Deploy `DelegateVoteRewardPool`, `WombatBribeManager`, and mock underlying pool bribe rewarders; register `delegatedPool` and add a real pool with `updateWeight`.
2. Have a long-term delegator vote into `delegatedPool` and let bribes accrue in the underlying pool's bribe rewarder over multiple blocks/`castVotes()` calls (simulate `queueNewRewards` deposits into the underlying pool rewarder for DelegateVoteRewardPool's vote weight).
3. In a single transaction from the attacker: call `WombatBribeManager.vote([delegatedPool], [+X])` then immediately `DelegateVoteRewardPool.harvestAll()` then `DelegateVoteRewardPool.getReward(attacker)`.
4. Assert `earned(attacker, rewardToken) > 0` right after this single-block sequence, and compare it to `earned(longTermStaker, rewardToken)` — show the attacker receives a disproportionate share (X/totalSupply_after) of rewards accrued before they staked, verifying dilution of the long-term staker's entitlement versus a baseline scenario where the attacker never staked.
