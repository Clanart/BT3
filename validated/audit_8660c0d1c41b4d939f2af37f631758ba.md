### Title
Reward-per-token snapshot model lets a just-in-time staker steal yield accrued by prior stakers - (File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol, rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol, rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol, rewards/vlMGPBaseRewarder.sol)

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool` (and its variants `BaseRewardPoolV2`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, `vlMGPBaseRewarder`) distribute newly injected rewards to *all currently staked* balances in one atomic step, without any time-weighting of when each staker's balance was added. A staker who deposits into `MasterMagpie` immediately before a reward injection (`queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards`) and withdraws immediately after can capture a share of rewards that accrued to the pool over the preceding period, before the staker ever participated — mirroring the "accrued limit-order fee theft" bug class from the external report.

### Finding Description
Every reward drop is folded into a single global accumulator `rewardPerTokenStored`, computed purely from the reward amount and the pool's *current* `totalStaked()` at the moment the reward is provisioned: [1](#0-0) 

There is no snapshot of "how long" or "since when" a staker has held their balance — `earned()` only compares a user's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` checkpoint to the current global `rewardPerTokenStored`: [2](#0-1) 

Because `balanceOf()` simply reads the user's live staked amount from `MasterMagpie` at the time `_updateFor`/`earned` is evaluated: [3](#0-2) 

...a staker who enters the pool with a large deposit in the same block (or shortly before) a `queueNewRewards` call lands, and withdraws shortly after claiming, receives a reward share proportional to their *instantaneous* stake at the moment of distribution — not proportional to the time or effort that generated the underlying yield (e.g., WOM emissions harvested by `WombatStaking`/`ManualCompound` over the preceding period and queued in a single batched call, or bribes routed through `WombatBribeManager`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder`). This is architecturally identical to the reported Uniswap `LimitOrderHook` issue: fees/rewards accumulated over a window are divided based on share-of-stake at the moment of settlement, allowing a just-in-time participant to claim value they did not help generate, diluting/stealing the entitlement of the stakers who were actually present while the yield accrued.

Because `MasterMagpie` staking/unstaking is a normal unprivileged wallet operation (deposit/withdraw), and reward injection into these pools (harvest batching, bribe queuing, `donateRewards` calls) is either predictable/periodic or externally triggerable and visible in the mempool, an attacker can front-run the reward injection with a large stake and back-run it with a withdrawal, extracting a disproportionate share of the newly injected rewards at the expense of long-standing stakers.

### Impact Explanation
This results in theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate long-term stakers: an attacker with sufficient capital can capture a large fraction of any given reward injection across `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`/`mWOMSVBaseRewarder`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder` pools (used for MGP emissions, bribes, and mWOM/vlMGP reward routing) by only holding a position for a single block or a few blocks, permanently diluting the rewards that should have accrued to the stakers who held their position throughout the accrual period.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reward injections from `WombatStaking`/`ManualCompound` harvests and `WombatBribeManager` bribe distributions occur on a routine, often predictable cadence, and `donateRewards` is even publicly callable at any time for already-registered tokens, making the timing of reward events observable/anticipatable in the mempool. Deposit/withdraw into `MasterMagpie` is a standard unprivileged action with no cooldown enforced at the `BaseRewardPool` layer, so the attack is executable by any wallet with sufficient capital for a flash-style or same-block/short-window stake.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted reward accounting (e.g., track deposit timestamp/block per user and only credit rewards accrued after that point), or move to a Synthetix-style continuous `rewardRate`/`lastUpdateTime` streaming model instead of instantaneous lump-sum injections, or add a minimum staking duration / withdrawal cooldown before a staker becomes eligible for freshly queued rewards.

### Proof of Concept
1. Off-chain: monitor mempool/predictable schedule for an upcoming `queueNewRewards` call on a `BaseRewardPool` (e.g., periodic MGP emission harvest via `WombatStaking`/`ManualCompound`, or bribe distribution via `WombatBribeManager`).
2. Front-run it: call `MasterMagpie.deposit(...)` with a large amount of the pool's staking token, sharply increasing `totalStaked()` right before the reward-provisioning transaction executes.
3. Let the target `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` transaction execute; per `_provisionReward` (`rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol:297-319`), `rewardPerTokenStored` increases based on the now-inflated `totalStaked()`, and the attacker's freshly-deposited balance is credited a share of the reward.
4. Immediately call `getReward` to claim the attacker's share, then `MasterMagpie.withdraw(...)` to exit the position.
5. Result: the attacker captures rewards proportional to their large, momentary stake despite having contributed none of the time/effort behind the yield, at the expense of stakers who held positions throughout the accrual window.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
