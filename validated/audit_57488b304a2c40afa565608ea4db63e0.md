Based on extensive investigation into Agave's analogous "stale authorization" patterns, I was unable to substantiate a concrete, exploitable analog to the LlamaPolicy `hasRole` bug (a permission check that fails to verify the underlying resource, i.e., the token, is still held).

Every plausible analog I traced turned out to already have the corresponding guard in place:

- `VoteStorage::insert_vote`/`cache_epoch_boundary_info` in `core/src/banking_stage/vote_storage.rs` explicitly evicts votes when `vote_account_stake(...) == 0` or the cached authorized voter is stale, guarding against exactly this "role without underlying stake" pattern. [1](#0-0) 
- `withdraw` in `programs/vote/src/vote_state/mod.rs` deinitializes the vote account state once the balance reaches zero, and further blocks closing while `pending_delegator_rewards > 0`, so a zero-balance vote account cannot retain a live role. [2](#0-1) 
- `validate_transaction_nonce` in `svm/src/transaction_processor.rs` explicitly re-derives nonce state and rejects transactions whose durable nonce account was closed/reopened/spoofed, addressing the "stale authority on a mutated resource" pattern for nonce accounts. [3](#0-2) 
- `bpf_loader`'s `Close` instruction converts a closed program/buffer into a `ProgramCacheEntryType::Closed` tombstone, and the entrypoint explicitly rejects invocation of `Closed`/`FailedVerification`/`DelayVisibility` entries, so authority checks tied to a since-closed program account cannot be reused.

            .retain(|vote_pubkey, vote| {

                let has_stale_authority = self
                    .cached_epoch_authorized_voters
                    .get(vote_pubkey)
                    .is_none_or(|auth| *auth != vote.authorized_voter_pubkey());
