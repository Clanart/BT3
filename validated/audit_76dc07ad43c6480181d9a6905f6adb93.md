### Title
Liquidation premium lets a borrower self-liquidate to extract extra collateral and push the shortfall onto bad-debt socialization instead of using the normal `repay` path - (File: `mainnet/contracts/market/v0-4-market.clar`)

### Summary
`liquidate()` in `v0-4-market.clar` pays whoever calls it a bonus (`liq-penalty`) worth up to `liq-penalty-max` on top of the debt repaid, and nothing in the function stops the borrower from calling it on their own position [1](#0-0) . When a position is deeply underwater (LTV at or above `LTV-LIQ-FULL`), a borrower who calls `liquidate` against themselves receives back their own remaining collateral inflated by the liquidation bonus while only a proportionally reduced amount of debt is marked "repaid," with the rest pushed into bad-debt socialization onto other suppliers [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) . This is strictly more profitable for the borrower than calling the normal `repay()` path (which reduces debt 1:1 with no bonus and does not unlock collateral beyond the health-gated `LTV-BORROW` limit).

### Finding Description
The liquidation math computes the seized collateral as debt repaid scaled up by the bonus factor `(BPS + liq-penalty)/BPS`: [4](#0-3) 

and the penalty itself scales up to `liq-penalty-max` as the position's LTV worsens toward and past `LTV-LIQ-FULL`: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

When collateral is insufficient to cover `debt * (1+penalty)` (i.e. LTV ≥ 100%, collateral value < debt value), `process-collateral-asset` caps the seized collateral at the user's full remaining balance, and `calc-final-liquidation-amounts` recomputes the "debt repaid" amount *downward* to be consistent with the bonus math (`debt-repay-real = coll-usd * BPS/(BPS+penalty)`), rather than crediting the full collateral value against debt: [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) 

The result: the caller pays only `debt-final` (worth less than the full collateral value) and receives 100% of the remaining collateral. When `no-collateral-left` becomes true, the unpaid remainder of the borrower's debt is explicitly written off via `socialize-debt-asset`, spreading the loss across the protocol's debt-asset suppliers: [9](#0-8) 

Critically, `liquidate()` never checks that `contract-caller`/`liquidator` differs from `borrower` — only that `contract-caller` equals `tx-sender` (basic non-proxy check) and that the position is unhealthy: [10](#0-9) [11](#0-10) 

`collateral-receiver` even defaults to `liquidator` itself, so the borrower/liquidator pockets the collateral directly: [12](#0-11) 

By contrast, the normal `repay()` path reduces scaled debt 1:1 with no bonus and does not release collateral at all; collateral can only be withdrawn subject to the conservative `LTV-BORROW` health check. Thus once a position crosses into the liquidation zone (`LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL`/`LTV-LIQ-FULL`), the borrower has a strictly dominant strategy: self-liquidate through `liquidate()` to (a) unlock 100% of remaining collateral immediately (bypassing the `LTV-BORROW` withdrawal gate that would otherwise block this while the position is unhealthy) and (b) capture the liquidation bonus, while offloading the unpaid debt difference onto the pool's other lenders via bad-debt socialization — exactly the same value-extraction pattern as the referenced StakeWise finding, where the liquidation premium is more profitable than the intended "healthy" reduction path (`burn`/`repay`).

### Impact Explanation
This causes protocol insolvency: the self-liquidator extracts collateral value in excess of what backs their debt, and the shortfall is not absorbed by the liquidator (as intended for third-party liquidations) but is instead socialized onto other suppliers of the debt asset, who suffer a loss when redeeming their deposits. This is a direct transfer of value from the lending pool to the borrower, i.e., theft of user funds at rest, satisfying the Critical impact bar.

### Likelihood Explanation
Any borrower can trivially trigger this once their own position crosses `LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL`/`LTV-LIQ-FULL` — no third party, oracle manipulation, or governance action is required; they simply call `liquidate()` naming themselves as both `borrower` and (implicitly) `liquidator`/`collateral-receiver`. The only friction is the same-block borrow-lockout check, which does not prevent self-liquidation in a later block: [13](#0-12) 

### Recommendation
Add an explicit check in `liquidate()` (and `liquidate-multi`) that rejects `borrower == liquidator` (or `borrower == contract-caller`), so that the incentive-bearing liquidation path can only be used by third parties, consistent with the deliberate design that debt reduction by the position owner should go through the non-incentivized `repay()` path.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower opens a position near `LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL`; price movement (or accrued interest) pushes `current-ltv` past `LTV-LIQ-PARTIAL` toward `LTV-LIQ-FULL`, so `(>= current-ltv ltv-liq-partial)` passes the health-check assertion at [14](#0-13) .
2. Borrower calls `liquidate(borrower=self, collateral-ft, debt-ft, debt-amount, min-collateral-expected=0, collateral-receiver=none, price-feeds)` from their own account.
3. `calc-liquidation-params` computes `liq-penalty` up to `liq-penalty-max` at [15](#0-14) ; `process-collateral-asset`/`calc-final-liquidation-amounts` caps seized collateral at the borrower's full remaining balance while marking a smaller `debt-final` as repaid at [8](#0-7) .
4. The borrower (as `actual-receiver`) receives their full remaining collateral back at [12](#0-11) , while the unpaid debt difference is written off via `socialize-debt-asset` at [9](#0-8) , transferring the deficit to other suppliers of the debt asset.

;; Calculate final liquidation amounts with proportional adjustments
;; If collateral was capped, recalculates debt proportionally
;; Returns: { debt-final-usd: uint, debt-final: uint }
(define-private (calc-final-liquidation-amounts
  (debt-actual-usd uint)
  (coll-actual uint)
  (coll-expected uint)
  (coll-price uint)
  (coll-decimals uint)
  (debt-price uint)
  (debt-decimals uint)
  (liq-penalty uint))
  
  (let ((coll-actual-usd (normalize (* coll-actual coll-price) coll-decimals false))

                           (calc-liq-debt-repay-real coll-actual-usd liq-penalty)

      debt-final-usd: debt-final-usd,
      debt-final: debt-final
    }))
