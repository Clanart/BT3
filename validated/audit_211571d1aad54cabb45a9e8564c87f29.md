### Title
Bribe sniping via `voteAndCast` — reward-per-token model credits new votes for the full harvested bribe with no time-weighting (Theft of unclaimed yield) - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol, rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol)

### Summary
`voteAndCast()` calls `vote()` (which immediately credits the caller's stake via `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor`) and then `castVotes()` (which harvests bribes and forwards them to the same rewarder) in the same transaction. Because `BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward` distributes the entire harvested bribe amount instantly and pro-rata by `totalStaked()` at that exact block (no streaming/vesting duration), a voter who stakes one instruction before the bribe is queued captures a full pro-rata share of the whole epoch's harvested bribe, diluting voters who had their votes committed for the entire preceding period.

### Finding Description
`vote()` immediately updates `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` and calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))`, which increases `totalSupply` and the caller's `_balances` before any bribe has been queued: [1](#0-0) .

`voteAndCast` executes `vote()` then `castVotes()` atomically in one call: [2](#0-1) . `castVotes()` invokes `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, which harvests bribes from Wombat and (via the rewarder's `queueNewRewards`) feeds them into `BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward`: [3](#0-2) .

`_provisionReward` computes `rewardPerTokenStored` instantly, splitting the freshly harvested bribe across `totalStaked()` at that exact block — there is no time-based streaming/vesting window like a `periodFinish`/`rewardRate` design: [4](#0-3) . `rewardPerToken()` simply returns the stored value with no time interpolation: [5](#0-4) .

Consequently, a caller who increases their `stakeFor` balance immediately before the same-transaction `castVotes()` harvest is included in `totalStaked()` at the moment `rewardPerTokenStored` jumps, and via `_earned()` (`userShare * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`) receives full credit for the entire bribe delta despite having contributed zero time-weighted exposure to that epoch: [6](#0-5) .

Regarding the specific invariant framed in the question (`getVoteForLp(lp)` vs `poolInfos[lp].totalVoteInVlmgp`): because `vote()` and `castVotes()` run atomically inside `voteAndCast()`, `wombatStaking.vote()` reconciles the on-chain Wombat vote with `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` by the end of the same transaction — that specific reconciliation does not actually diverge. The real, exploitable defect is the reward-crediting timing described above, which is a genuine "instant vs. time-weighted" reward accounting flaw shared by all `BaseRewardPoolV2`-derived rewarders (including `BribeRewardPool`).

### Impact Explanation
Any unprivileged holder of vlMGP/voting power can call `voteAndCast()` right as bribes are due to be harvested (which they can also trigger themselves) and receive a pro-rata share of the entire harvested bribe as if they had voted for the whole period, at the expense of voters who genuinely held their votes throughout the epoch. This is a redistribution of unclaimed yield away from long-standing voters toward same-block snipers — matching the "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" impact class. It does not, however, break solvency or allow direct drain of principal/staked tokens, since votes/withdrawals are still balance-accounted correctly.

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged role is required; `voteAndCast`, `vote`, and `castVotes` are all public/external and callable by any EOA. The only precondition is having enough available (`getUserVotable`) voting power to shift `_deltas`, which is affected by lock/cooldown mechanics elsewhere in the vlMGP system, not by any special access control in `WombatBribeManager`. This can be repeated every time a meaningful, unharvested bribe balance accumulates on a pool.

### Recommendation
Adopt a streamed reward-rate model (e.g., Synthetix-style `rewardRate` / `periodFinish` with `lastUpdateTime`) for `BaseRewardPoolV2`/`BribeRewardPool` instead of crediting the full `_amountReward` to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single instant. Alternatively, snapshot vote balances at the start of a cast cycle and only allow `stakeFor` changes made prior to the current epoch's snapshot to be eligible for that epoch's harvested bribes, or add a cool-down between newly increased vote weight and eligibility for freshly harvested rewards.

### Proof of Concept
1. Deploy the protocol's Wombat mock/testnet fixtures with a bribe accrued on some LP (`_lp`) for its rewarder over time while an honest, long-term voter (`Alice`) holds all the current `totalVoteInVlmgp` for that pool.
2. Have an attacker acquire the minimum vlMGP needed to call `voteAndCast([_lp], [delta], false)`, where `delta` is a small increase relative to Alice's existing vote.
3. Before the transaction, record `earned(attacker, rewardToken)` (should be 0) and `earned(alice, rewardToken)`.
4. Execute `attacker.voteAndCast([_lp], [delta], false)` in a single transaction, which (a) calls `stakeFor(attacker, delta)` inside `vote()`, then (b) triggers `castVotes()` which harvests the pool's pending bribe and queues it via `queueNewRewards`.
5. Assert that immediately after the transaction, `earned(attacker, rewardToken)` is non-zero and proportional to `delta / totalStaked()` at that block — i.e., the attacker earns a share of the entire bribe despite having staked for zero blocks prior to the harvest — while Alice's marginal share is diluted relative to her true time-weighted contribution.
6. Confirm `getVoteForLp(_lp)` and `poolInfos[_lp].totalVoteInVlmgp` remain equal at the end of the transaction (showing the vote-accounting invariant itself holds), isolating the bug specifically to the reward-per-token instant-crediting logic in `BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward`.

```text
    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;

        if (totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingTokenDecimals) /
                totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
```
