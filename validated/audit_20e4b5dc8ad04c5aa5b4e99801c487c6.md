### Title
Mutable, Live-Read Asset Decimals Break Cross-Chain Amount Scaling in `pallet-hyper-fungible-token`, Enabling Inflated Mint/Release on Destination Chain - (File: `modules/pallets/hyper-fungible-token/src/lib.rs`, `modules/pallets/hyper-fungible-token/src/impls.rs`, `modules/pallets/hyper-fungible-token/src/module.rs`)

### Summary
`pallet-hyper-fungible-token` validates `remote_decimals >= local_decimals` only once, at `register_token`/`update_token` time [1](#0-0) , but every subsequent `send`, `on_accept`, and `on_timeout` call re-reads the *current* local decimals live from the mutable `fungibles::metadata::Inspect::decimals()` of the underlying `pallet-assets` asset [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) . On the `gargantua` runtime, asset creation and metadata are controlled by the asset's own `Owner`/`Admin`, and asset creation itself is permissionless (`CreateOrigin = AsEnsureOriginWithArg<EnsureSigned<AccountId>>`) [4](#0-3) . Since HFT registration never transfers `pallet-assets` ownership, the original (unprivileged) asset owner retains the ability to call `set_metadata` and change `decimals` at will, forever, even after the asset is registered into the bridge by governance.

### Finding Description
The scaling helpers in `impls.rs` compute:

```rust
pub fn convert_to_erc20(value: u128, erc_decimals: u8, local_decimals: u8) -> U256 {
    U256::from(value) * U256::from(10u128.pow(erc_decimals.saturating_sub(local_decimals) as u32))
}
``` [5](#0-4) 

`erc_decimals` comes from the `Precisions` storage set once at `register_token`/`update_token` time (both `CreateOrigin`-gated) [6](#0-5) . `local_decimals`, however, is *not* snapshotted — it is fetched live on every call via `fungibles::metadata::Inspect::decimals(asset_id)` in `send()` [7](#0-6) , `on_accept()` [3](#0-2) , and `on_timeout()` [8](#0-7) .

`pallet-assets` decimals metadata is purely cosmetic — it does not constrain or affect the raw `Balance` unit accounting — and is mutable by the asset's `Owner`/metadata-setter independently of the HFT pallet's `CreateOrigin`, as the benchmarking helpers themselves demonstrate (`MutateMetadata::set(asset_id, who, name, symbol, decimals)`) [9](#0-8) . On `gargantua`, `pallet_assets::Config::CreateOrigin` is `AsEnsureOriginWithArg<EnsureSigned<AccountId>>` — any signed account can create an asset and become its owner [4](#0-3) . HFT's `register_token` only checks `config.decimals >= local_decimals` at the moment of registration and never re-validates or re-checks ownership of the metadata afterward [10](#0-9) .

The exploit path:
1. Attacker (unprivileged, signed account) creates asset `X` on `gargantua` via permissionless `pallet_assets::create`, becoming its `Owner`/`Admin`/`Issuer`, and sets `decimals = 6`.
2. Governance later registers `X` in HFT via `register_token` with a remote EVM chain configured at `decimals = 18`; the invariant `18 >= 6` passes and `Precisions` stores `18` for `(X, EVM_chain)` permanently.
3. HFT registration does not touch `pallet-assets` ownership — the attacker remains `Owner` of `X` and retains `set_metadata` rights.
4. Attacker calls `pallet_assets::set_metadata` again, lowering `decimals` to `0` (or any value below `6`). This mutates only cosmetic metadata; it does not change the semantics of raw balance units.
5. Attacker mints/holds raw units of `X` (they control `Issuer`/mint rights on their own asset) and calls HFT's `send()`. The pallet burns/escrows exactly `amount` raw units, unaffected by the decimals change — but `erc_decimals.saturating_sub(local_decimals)` is now `18 - 0 = 18` instead of the intended `18 - 6 = 12`, inflating `convert_to_erc20` by `10^6`.
6. The oversized `erc20_amount` is embedded in the ISMP `Message` and dispatched to the destination `HyperFungibleToken`/`WrappedHyperFungibleToken` contract, which mints/releases `10^6`× more tokens than were actually burned/escrowed on the source chain.

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct fund-theft / false-amount-acceptance primitive: an unprivileged actor (the original creator of a bridged asset, a normal and expected role distinct from the bridge's governance `CreateOrigin`) can unilaterally corrupt the amount-scaling invariant that HFT's registration logic assumed was immutable, causing the destination chain to mint/release wrapped tokens far in excess of what was locked or burned on the source chain. This directly violates the "bridged assets... must move exactly once and only to the rightful beneficiary and amount" invariant.

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires no relayer, prover, validator, or bridge-governance compromise — only that (a) the attacker created and still owns the bridged asset's metadata (a routine, permissionless action on `gargantua`), and (b) the asset has been onboarded into HFT by governance without a corresponding ownership transfer or decimals pinning, which the current code never enforces or even checks for.

### Recommendation
Snapshot and store `local_decimals` immutably in HFT's own storage (e.g., in `TokenRegistration`/a new `LocalDecimals` map) at `register_token` time, and use that stored value in `send`, `on_accept`, and `on_timeout` instead of re-reading live, mutable `fungibles::metadata::Inspect::decimals()`. Alternatively, require asset ownership/metadata-lock transfer to the pallet or `CreateOrigin` before/at registration, and reject `send`/`on_accept` if the live decimals value ever diverges from the value validated at registration.

### Proof of Concept
1. On `gargantua`, call `pallet_assets::create` as attacker → own asset `X`, `set_metadata(X, decimals=6)`.
2. Governance calls `pallet_hyper_fungible_token::register_token` for `X` with `chains[EVM].decimals = 18` (passes `18 >= 6`) — see check at [11](#0-10) .
3. Attacker calls `pallet_assets::set_metadata(X, decimals=0)` — no HFT-side gating exists on this call.
4. Attacker mints/holds raw units of `X`, calls `pallet_hyper_fungible_token::send` with `asset_id=X`, `amount=N`. Trace `erc_decimals=18`, `decimals=Inspect::decimals(X)=0` at [12](#0-11) , giving `erc20_amount = N * 10^18` instead of the intended `N * 10^12`.
5. Destination `HyperFungibleToken` contract mints/releases `N * 10^18` raw ERC20 units to the attacker's chosen recipient upon receiving the ISMP `Message`, a `10^6`× overpayment relative to the `N` raw units actually burned/escrowed on the source chain.
