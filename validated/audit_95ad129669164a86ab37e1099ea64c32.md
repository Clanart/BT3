### Title
Reward tier can be inflated via a momentary mWomSV lock, letting the entire `incentiveDeposit` amount be paid at a higher `rewardMultiplier` bracket - (File: wombat/ArbWomUp3.sol)

### Summary
`getRewardAmount()`'s `!_lock` branch multiplies the *entire* `_amountToConvert` by `rewardMultiplier[getUserTier(_account)]`, where `getUserTier` is computed from `mWomSV.getUserTotalLocked(_account)` read live at call time [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) . Because `mWomSV.lock()` credits the user's locked balance instantly with no vesting delay, an attacker can lock a tiny amount right before calling `incentiveDeposit`, get the whole (much larger) deposit rewarded at the higher tier multiplier, and later withdraw the tiny stake once the unlock cooldown elapses.

### Finding Description
`incentiveDeposit(_amount, _convertRatio, false, 1)` calls `this.getRewardAmount(_amount, msg.sender, false)` [3](#0-2) . In the `!_lock` branch, `mgpReward` is `_amountToConvert * rewardMultiplier[getUserTier(_account)] / DENOMINATOR` [4](#0-3) , i.e. the reward-multiplier bracket for the user's *current* mWomSV stake is applied to the entire converted amount, not just to the portion "backed" by the qualifying stake (contrast with the `_lock` branch, which correctly walks `rewardTier`/`rewardMultiplier` incrementally per bracket [5](#0-4) ).

`getUserTier` derives the bracket purely from `mWomSV.getUserTotalLocked(_account)` at the moment of the call [2](#0-1) , which in turn reads `IMasterMagpie(masterMagpie).stakingInfo(...)` minus the amount currently in cooldown [6](#0-5) . `mWomSV.lock()`/`lockFor()` credit this balance immediately via `_lock`, with no time-weighting or minimum holding period [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) . Unlocking requires `startUnlock` + `coolDownInSecs` + `unlock` [9](#0-8) , but that cooldown only gates *withdrawal*, not the initial credit used by `getUserTier`. Since the reward is already computed and MGP already locked into `vlMGP` for the attacker inside `incentiveDeposit` (single transaction), the later cooldown on unstaking the bait amount is irrelevant to preventing the exploit.

Exploit flow:
1. Attacker calls `mWomSV.lock(smallAmount)` — just enough to push `getUserTotalLocked(attacker)` across a `rewardTier[i]` boundary.
2. In the same or a following transaction, attacker calls `ArbWomUp3.incentiveDeposit(largeAmount, ratio, false, 1)`. `getRewardAmount` computes `getUserTier(attacker)` = the elevated tier `i`, and applies `rewardMultiplier[i]` to the full `largeAmount`, not just `smallAmount`.
3. Attacker receives MGP reward (locked into `vlMGP` via `vlMGP.lockFor`) computed at the inflated rate for the whole deposit.
4. Attacker later calls `mWomSV.startUnlock(smallAmount)`, waits `coolDownInSecs`, then `mWomSV.unlock(slotIndex)` to reclaim the bait `smallAmount`, having paid no more than transient opportunity cost for the tiny stake.

No modifier (`nonReentrant`, `whenNotPaused`) blocks this since it is a straightforward two-call sequence across two contracts, not reentrancy. No receipt-token or reward-index accounting corrects for the "durability" of the qualifying stake — the check is a single point-in-time balance read.

### Impact Explanation
This lets an attacker convert an arbitrarily large `_amount` of WOM at a MGP-reward multiplier they have not genuinely earned, while committing only a minimal, momentarily-locked stake. Because `getRewardAmount` caps the payout only by the contract's current `mgp` balance (`mgpleft`) [10](#0-9) , this directly drains the finite incentive/reward pool that should be distributed proportionally to genuine long-term lockers, resulting in theft of unclaimed MGP yield from the program and unfair depletion of rewards for legitimate depositors.

### Likelihood Explanation
Feasible for any unprivileged attacker holding WOM: lock a small qualifying amount into `mWomSV`, immediately call `incentiveDeposit` with a large amount and `_mode=1`/`_lock=false`, then unwind the small stake after the cooldown. No special privileges, oracle manipulation, or governance access needed — repeatable each time the attacker has fresh capital to route through `incentiveDeposit`, and capital efficiency scales favorably (only the tier-boundary-crossing amount need be locked, not the full deposit).

### Recommendation
Base the reward multiplier for the `!_lock` branch on a durable/time-weighted stake measure (e.g., require the tier-qualifying balance to have been continuously locked for a minimum duration, or apply the same bracketed/incremental calculation used in the `_lock` branch so only the portion actually backed by the user's locked stake receives the higher multiplier) instead of a live, instantaneously-inflatable `getUserTotalLocked` snapshot.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test outline:
1. Deploy/mock `MasterMagpie`, `mWomSV`, `ArbWomUp3`, `SmartWomConvert`, `mWom`, `wom`, `mgp`, `vlMGP` with `rewardTier = [0, T1, T2]`, `rewardMultiplier = [m0, m1, m2]` such that `m2 >> m0`.
2. Fund `ArbWomUp3` with sufficient `mgp`.
3. Attacker: `mWomSV.lock(T2)` (just at/above tier-2 threshold), then immediately `wom.approve` + `ArbWomUp3.incentiveDeposit(largeAmount, ratio, false, 1)`. Assert `vlMGP` balance for attacker == `largeAmount * m2 / DENOMINATOR`.
4. Attacker then `mWomSV.startUnlock(T2)`, `vm.warp(coolDownInSecs + 1)`, `mWomSV.unlock(0)`, reclaiming ~`T2` mWom.
5. Compare against "honest" user B who genuinely holds `T2` locked for the long term and deposits the same `largeAmount` with `_lock=true` (bracketed calc via `calDoubledCounted`); assert B's received MGP is materially lower than the attacker's, despite both starting from the same net capital committed after the attacker unwinds — demonstrating the multiplier was granted without a durable qualifying stake.
