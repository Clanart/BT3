### Title
mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit hardcodes full rewardable amount, allowing in-cooldown mWomSV holders to claim 100% of rewards with zero forfeit - (File: rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol)

### Summary
`mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` sets `rewardableAmount = _amount` unconditionally instead of scaling by `mWomSV.getRewardablePercentWAD(_account)`, unlike the analogous and correctly implemented `vlMGPBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit`. This means any mWomSV holder in cooldown, who under the intended design should forfeit a proportional share of rewards to fully-locked holders, always receives 100% of accrued rewards.

### Finding Description
In `rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol`, `_calExpireForfeit` is:
```solidity

``` [2](#0-1) 

`mWomSV.getRewardablePercentWAD` is fully implemented and designed to reduce the rewardable percentage for accounts with `amountInCoolDown > 0` that are still within their cooldown window (returning less than 100% for such users), confirming the intended design requires partial forfeiture during cooldown:

The exploit path is fully reachable by an unprivileged holder: `mWomSV.startUnlock` (external, no special role) puts a fraction of the user's mWomSV into `amountInCoolDown`, then `MasterMagpie.multiclaimFor` → `mWOMSVBaseRewarder.getReward` (gated only by `onlyMasterMagpie`, callable by any MasterMagpie user through the normal claim flow) → `_sendReward` → `_calExpireForfeit`. None of the modifiers (`onlyMasterMagpie`, `updateReward`, `nonReentrant`) check or enforce forfeiture — they only control access to the reward-claim function itself, not the calculation logic, so the hardcoded bypass is unguarded.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft/misallocation of unclaimed yield: reward tokens that the protocol design intends to redistribute to fully-locked mWomSV holders (via `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer(forfeitAmount, ...)`) are instead retained in full by users who are partially unlocking/in cooldown. Every claim by any in-cooldown holder permanently prevents that forfeited-yield redistribution from happening, diluting rewards owed to fully-locked stakers. This matches the "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
Trivially exploitable by any unprivileged mWomSV holder with zero special capital or privilege: they only need to call `startUnlock` on part of their balance (a normal, public user action) and then claim rewards through the standard `MasterMagpie.multiclaimFor` flow, which any holder can call for themselves. It's fully repeatable on every reward claim, requires no flash loans, front-running, or governance/admin power, and works every single time because the bug is deterministic (`rewardableAmount = _amount` unconditionally).

### Recommendation
Update `mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` to mirror `vlMGPBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit`: compute `uint256 rewardablePercentWAD = mWOMSV.getRewardablePercentWAD(_account);` and `uint256 rewardableAmount = _amount * rewardablePercentWAD / 1e18;` before computing `forfeitAmount = _amount - rewardableAmount`.
