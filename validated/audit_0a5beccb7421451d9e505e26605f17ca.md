### Title
Zero-`totalStaked()` window in `_provisionReward` allows an attacker to capture the entire queued reward pool with a 1-wei stake - (File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol)

### Summary
`_provisionReward` diverts newly queued rewards into `queuedRewards` whenever `totalStaked() == 0`, and folds the entire accumulated `queuedRewards` into `rewardPerTokenStored` on the very next provision that occurs while `totalStaked() > 0`, dividing by whatever `totalStaked()` happens to be at that moment. Because `totalStaked()` reads live balance from `MasterMagpie` (`IERC20(stakingToken).balanceOf(operator)`), an attacker who is (or engineers themselves to be) the last staker to withdraw can force this zero-window, then restake a trivial amount immediately before the next `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` call flushes the queue, capturing a reward share wildly disproportionate to their stake.

### Finding Description
`totalStaked()` is computed live as `IERC20(stakingToken).balanceOf(operator)` [1](#0-0) . In `_provisionReward`, if `totalStaked() == 0` at the time of a reward provision, the reward amount is added to `rewardInfo.queuedRewards` instead of being distributed; on the next provision where `totalStaked() > 0`, the full accumulated `queuedRewards` is added to the incoming amount and divided by the *current* `totalStaked()` to update `rewardPerTokenStored` [2](#0-1) .

This design assumes `totalStaked() == 0` only occurs incidentally/rarely, but nothing prevents an attacker from deliberately withdrawing to force `totalStaked()` to zero (either by being the sole staker at that time, or by withdrawing at the precise block where all other stakers have temporarily zero balance), waiting for a manager to call `queueNewRewards` (a routine, non-privileged-attacker-dependent operation), and then restaking a minimal amount (e.g. 1 wei) before the next provision flushes `queuedRewards`. Since `rewardPerTokenStored` is updated using `totalStaked()` at flush time (which would be `1`), the entire queued reward — potentially accumulated across the zero-window — gets attributed almost entirely to the attacker's negligible stake via the `_earned`/`rewardPerToken` accounting (`_earned` uses `rewardPerToken(_rewardToken) - userRewardPerTokenPaid` scaled by the user's share) [3](#0-2) .

No modifier or check in `queueNewRewards`, `donateRewards`, or `_provisionReward` guards against this: `onlyManager` only restricts who can queue rewards, not when, and there's no minimum-stake or time-weighted accounting to prevent a near-zero stake from being flushed proportionally against the entire zero-window's queued balance [4](#0-3) .

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct theft of user/protocol funds: rewards intended for prior epochs of staking are captured entirely by an attacker's negligible (1 wei) stake, at the expense of legitimate stakers who would otherwise receive them once they re-stake. This matches the Immunefi "direct theft of user funds" / "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires the attacker to either be the sole staker in the pool at some point (plausible for newer/smaller pools or reward-token-specific pools within `BaseRewardPoolV2`) or to coordinate/time their withdrawal to align with all other stakers momentarily being at zero balance (harder, but possible via front-running mass-withdrawal events, e.g., end of an epoch or migration). The manager's `queueNewRewards` call is a normal, expected periodic operation (not attacker-controlled, but publicly observable/predictable in timing for many reward-distribution schedules), so the attacker can front-run/back-run around it. Capital required is minimal (1 wei stake plus gas), and the exploit is repeatable across any reward token in any pool that experiences a zero-`totalStaked` moment. Feasibility is highest in low-TVL or newly-launched pools.

### Recommendation
Do not divide by the *current* `totalStaked()` when flushing accumulated `queuedRewards`; instead, either (a) prevent restaking from diluting historically queued rewards by tracking the eligible stake at the time the reward was queued, (b) require a minimum bootstrap stake/timelock before a pool can receive real distributions, or (c) route `queuedRewards` accumulated during a `totalStaked() == 0` window to a separate mechanism that distributes pro-rata only to stakers active during that queuing period (or refunds/holds for governance), rather than instantaneously flushing to whichever stake amount exists at the next provisioning call. At minimum, add a safeguard requiring `totalStaked()` to exceed a minimum threshold (or have been non-zero for a minimum duration) before flushing `queuedRewards` into `rewardPerTokenStored`.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, `BaseRewardPoolV2` for a staking token and a reward token; register a manager.
2. Have `stakerA` deposit `100e18` staking tokens; call `queueNewRewards(10e18, rewardToken)` normally to confirm baseline distribution works (`rewardPerTokenStored` increases, `queuedRewards == 0`).
3. Have `stakerA` fully withdraw so `totalStaked() == 0`.
4. As `manager`, call `queueNewRewards(1000e18, rewardToken)` — assert `rewards[rewardToken].queuedRewards == 1000e18` and `rewardPerTokenStored` unchanged.
5. As `attacker`, deposit `1 wei` of staking token (so `totalStaked() == 1`).
6. As `manager`, call `queueNewRewards(0, rewardToken)` (or any small next-epoch amount) to trigger the flush — assert `rewardPerTokenStored` jumps by `(1000e18 * 10**decimals) / 1`.
7. As `attacker`, call `getReward(attacker, attacker)` and assert the transferred reward amount is approximately `1000e18` (the entire queued pool), despite the attacker only ever holding 1 wei of stake.
8. Assert this reward vastly exceeds any reward `stakerA` (who held 100e18 for the entire prior period) can subsequently claim, demonstrating fund misallocation/theft relative to legitimate stakers' contribution.

```text
    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;

        if (totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingTokenDecimals) /
                totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
```
