### Title
Reward-per-share dilution lets a dust vlMGP voter harvest a disproportionate share of accrued bribes when `BribeRewardPool.totalStaked()` is momentarily tiny - ([File: rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`BribeRewardPool.totalStaked()` returns `totalSupply`, the sum of vlMGP vote weight currently staked in the pool via `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor`, and `queueNewRewards` (inherited `_provisionReward` in `BaseRewardPoolV2`) divides the harvested bribe amount by this instantaneous `totalStaked()` to update `rewardPerTokenStored`. Because the bribe amount harvested by `wombatStaking.vote()` reflects bribes accrued on Wombat's real voter contract over time (independent of the current `totalSupply` snapshot in `BribeRewardPool`), an unprivileged voter can enter with a negligible vlMGP delta right when `totalSupply` for a pool is near zero and capture nearly the entire `rewardPerTokenStored` increment for that harvest.

### Finding Description
`vote()` in `wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol` calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` on a positive delta and `withdrawFor(..., true)` on `unvote()`, directly mutating `BribeRewardPool.totalSupply` [1](#0-0) , [2](#0-1) . `totalStaked()` in `BribeRewardPool` simply returns this `totalSupply` [3](#0-2) . `_provisionReward` in `BaseRewardPoolV2` computes `rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored += (_amountReward * 10**decimals) / totalStaked()` at the moment `queueNewRewards` is invoked [4](#0-3) .

The bribe amount itself, however, is produced by `WombatStaking.vote()` from `voter.vote(_lpVote, _deltas)`, which returns bribes accrued on the external Wombat voter/bribe contracts since the last cast - a function of real historical vote weight held over the elapsed epoch, not of `BribeRewardPool.totalSupply` at the instant of harvest [5](#0-4) . `castVotes()` in `WombatBribeManager` is a public, permissionless function that anyone can call to trigger this harvest and forward rewards via `queueNewRewards` [6](#0-5) .

Because the harvested bribe is decoupled in time/origin from the current staker snapshot in `BribeRewardPool`, if large voters unvote a pool (immediately settling their own past-earned rewards via `withdrawFor(..., true)`, which uses the pre-harvest `rewardPerTokenStored`) and `totalSupply` collapses to near zero, an attacker can:
1. Call `vote([pool], [1])` with 1 wei of vlMGP, calling `stakeFor` and making `totalSupply ≈ 1`.
2. Call (or wait for someone to call) `castVotes()`/`harvestSinglePool()`, which harvests the pool's accrued Wombat bribe and calls `queueNewRewards`, dividing the entire bribe by `totalStaked() ≈ 1`, producing a massive `rewardPerTokenStored` increment.
3. Call `claimBribe`/`unvote` to withdraw the reward via `earned()`/`_getReward` in `BribeRewardPool` [7](#0-6) .

No modifier, cooldown, or minimum holding period gates `vote`, `castVotes`, or `unvote`, and the `updateRewards` accounting only checks `rewardPerTokenStored` deltas, not time-weighted stake [8](#0-7) , so nothing prevents this instantaneous entry/exit around a reward drop.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft of unclaimed bribe yield: the attacker captures bribe rewards that were accrued based on real, historical Wombat vote weight belonging to other (larger, longer-term) voters or that should be shared pro-rata among genuine future stakers of that pool, using only 1 wei of vlMGP vote weight and gas costs. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" Immunefi impact class named in the scope.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are cheap and fully attacker-controlled: only 1 wei of vlMGP voting power is needed, no privileged role is required, `vote`, `castVotes`/`harvestSinglePool`, `claimBribe`, and `unvote` are all public/external functions reachable by any EOA. The attacker can monitor `previewBribes`/`previewBnbAmountForHarvest` (both public view functions) to identify pools with a large pending bribe and low `totalVoteInVlmgp`, and can either wait for a natural mass-unvote event or front-run a visible large `unvote()` transaction in the mempool. The attack is repeatable across pools and across harvest cycles.

### Recommendation
Decouple bribe distribution from the instantaneous `totalStaked()` snapshot: distribute each harvested bribe based on a time-weighted average of vote weight since the last harvest (e.g., checkpointing `totalSupply` integrals, similar to a TWAP/streaming rewards model), or impose a minimum vote-lock duration before a vote counts toward `stakeFor`/eligibility for the next `queueNewRewards` call, or require `castVotes` to only distribute pro-rata to stakers whose vote has been active for at least one full cast cycle.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `WombatStaking`, `BribeRewardPool` for a test pool with two voters: `LargeVoter` (holds substantial vlMGP, votes a large amount) and `Attacker` (holds 1 wei of vlMGP).
2. Simulate bribe accrual on the mocked Wombat voter/bribe contracts proportional to `LargeVoter`'s long-held vote (e.g., accrue over several blocks/epochs).
3. Have `LargeVoter` call `unvote(pool)` (settles their own earned rewards at old `rewardPerTokenStored`, reduces `BribeRewardPool.totalSupply` to ~0).
4. Attacker calls `vote([pool], [1])`, making `totalSupply == 1`.
5. Attacker (or anyone) calls `castVotes()`/`harvestSinglePool([pool])`, triggering `wombatStaking.vote()` → `queueNewRewards` with the full accrued bribe amount, dividing by `totalStaked() == 1`.
6. Assert `earned(Attacker, rewardToken)` ≈ full bribe amount (minus protocol/caller fees), despite Attacker having contributed negligible real vote weight/time.
7. Attacker calls `claimBribe([pool])` and confirms actual token transfer of the disproportionate amount, then `unvote(pool)` to exit with no lockup penalty.
