### Title
Reward-sniping via `vote()`/`castVotes()`/`unvote()` composition lets an attacker capture bribe rewards accrued by real long-term voters without contributing real on-chain vote weight - ([File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol])

### Summary
`BribeRewardPool` (extending `BaseRewardPool`) distributes bribes using a discrete, non-time-weighted `rewardPerTokenStored` snapshot model: whenever new rewards are queued (harvested via `castVotes()` from real Wombat bribes), the amount is split pro-rata across whoever currently holds a `stakeFor` balance in the pool at that instant [1](#0-0) . Since `vote()` in `WombatBribeManager` immediately calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` on the *local* accounting the moment a user votes—well before that delta is ever reflected in the real Wombat on-chain vote via `castVotes()`—an attacker can insert (or remove) stake in a pool's `BribeRewardPool` right around the moment `castVotes()` harvests and queues real bribes, capturing a share of yield that was actually earned by voters who held real, time-weighted Wombat gauge influence.

### Finding Description
`vote()` updates `poolInfos[lp].totalVoteInVlmgp` and calls `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` on the pool's `BribeRewardPool` synchronously and instantly [2](#0-1) . The actual on-chain Wombat vote (`voter.getUserVotes(wombatStaking, lp)`) is only updated later, inside `castVotes()`, which computes a delta between `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` (local) and `getVoteForLp` (real) and pushes it via `wombatStaking.vote(...)` [3](#0-2) . That same `wombatStaking.vote()` call is what harvests pending real Wombat bribes for each pool, which are then funneled into the pool's `BribeRewardPool` reward accounting.

The reward pool's accrual logic (`BaseRewardPool.rewardPerToken`/`_provisionReward`) is a Synthetix-style snapshot model: `rewardPerTokenStored` only increases when new rewards are provisioned, and is split over `totalStaked()` **at the instant of provisioning**, not weighted by how long each staker's balance existed during the period the bribe was earned [1](#0-0) [4](#0-3) . `stakeFor`'s `updateRewards` modifier correctly snapshots the caller's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` *before* increasing `totalSupply`/`_balances`, so it cannot steal rewards already queued before it staked [5](#0-4) . However, it does nothing to prevent the new stake from being included in the **very next** reward-provisioning event, even if that next provisioning event corresponds to bribes earned by real on-chain votes accumulated entirely before the attacker's stake existed.

Because `vote()`, `castVotes()`, and `unvote()` are all public/external and `voteAndCast()` composes vote+cast in a single atomic transaction [6](#0-5) , an unprivileged attacker can:
1. Call `vote(poolB, +X)` (or `voteAndCast`), instantly increasing their local `BribeRewardPool` stake for poolB via `stakeFor` [7](#0-6) .
2. Immediately trigger (or front-run) `castVotes()`, which harvests real Wombat bribes for poolB (earned by legitimate long-term voters whose real on-chain vote weight, not the attacker's, generated the bribe) and queues them into poolB's `BribeRewardPool`.
3. Since the queuing/distribution uses instantaneous `totalStaked()` including the attacker's freshly added stake, the attacker's `earned()` becomes non-zero and can be claimed via `claimBribe()`/`claimAllBribes()` [8](#0-7) .
4. Call `unvote(poolB)` to withdraw the local stake (which also triggers `withdrawFor(..., true)` auto-claiming remaining bribe) [9](#0-8) , and repeat on another pool.

No existing modifier (`nonReentrant`, `onlyOperator`, pool `isActive` check) prevents this, because the vulnerability is not reentrancy or access control — it is a mismatch between the reward-accrual snapshot granularity (per-provisioning-event, non-time-weighted) and the real, time-weighted nature of Wombat bribe accrual that the local accounting is supposed to mirror.

### Impact Explanation
This allows theft of unclaimed yield belonging to legitimate long-term voters: bribe rewards earned by real, sustained on-chain Wombat gauge votes get diluted/redirected to an attacker who briefly parks (or shifts) `vlMGP`-derived local stake in a pool's `BribeRewardPool` immediately around a `castVotes()` harvest, without ever contributing real time-weighted vote influence to that pool. This matches the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield."

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are minimal and attacker-controlled: any vlMGP holder can call `vote()`/`voteAndCast()`/`unvote()` at will (`castVotes()` is explicitly permissionless), and the attack is repeatable across every active pool and every `castVotes()` invocation, requiring only the capital of whatever vlMGP the attacker already holds (which can be reused across pools sequentially since `unvote()` frees it immediately). The attack is more effective the larger the pending, un-harvested real bribe is relative to existing `BribeRewardPool` stake, i.e., precisely when `castVotes()` has been delayed, as described in the question.

### Recommendation
Change bribe distribution so that a user's share of a reward-provisioning event is weighted by their real time-weighted holding period (e.g., checkpoint-based/streaming rewards, or requiring a minimum bonding/vesting period before newly staked local vote weight is eligible for the next distribution), or restrict `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` from `vote()`/`unvote()` so that local `BribeRewardPool` balances only change in the same transaction that `castVotes()` actually commits the corresponding real on-chain Wombat vote (i.e., merge vote-intent accounting and stake accounting so a delta is only staked once it has been reflected on-chain for at least one full bribe-accrual period).

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, a mock `IWombatVoter`/`IWombatStaking` that lets the test simulate real bribe accrual over time for `poolA`/`poolB`, and `BribeRewardPool` instances for both pools with a bribe reward token.
2. Have `userReal` call `vote(poolB, +100e18)` and let `castVotes()` be called once to actually commit `userReal`'s vote on-chain for poolB; simulate a period elapsing during which poolB accrues `R` bribe tokens in the mock Wombat bribe contract tied to `userReal`'s real, sustained on-chain vote.
3. Immediately before the next `castVotes()` call that will harvest `R`, have `attacker` call `voteAndCast(poolB, +100e18, false)` (or `vote()` then `castVotes()` back-to-back) — attacker's local `stakeFor` runs before `queueNewRewards(R, token)` is invoked on poolB's `BribeRewardPool`.
4. Assert `IBribeRewardPool(poolB.rewarder).earned(attacker, token) > 0` even though `voter.getUserVotes(wombatStaking, poolB)` never included attacker's weight during the period `R` was accrued.
5. Assert `attacker` can successfully `claimBribe([poolB])` and receive a proportional share of `R`, then `unvote(poolB)` to fully exit, demonstrating yield extraction disproportionate to zero real time-weighted on-chain influence, at the expense of `userReal`'s expected full share of `R`.
