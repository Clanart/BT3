### Title
Instantaneous flash-lock/unlock sandwich inflates referrer boosted reward via `ReferralStorage._calBoosted` - ([File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol])

### Summary
`ReferralStorage.trigger` computes a referrer's boosted reward share by reading `userInfos[_referer].factor` and `totalBoostFactor` at the exact moment the referee's claim executes, with no time-weighting or minimum holding period. Because `VLMGP.lock`/`VLMGP.startUnlock` synchronously call `ReferralStorage.updateTotalFactor` and both are unprivileged, callable-anytime functions, an attacker can inflate their own `factor` for a single block, capture an inflated `refererAmount` when a victim's claim happens to trigger in that same window, and immediately reverse the position.

### Finding Description
`VLMGP._lock` and `VLMGP.startUnlock` call `IReferralStorage(referralStorage).updateTotalFactor(_for)` synchronously on every lock/unlock: [1](#0-0) [2](#0-1) 

`updateTotalFactor` recomputes `userInfo.factor = sqrt(getUserTotalLocked(_account))` and updates `totalBoostFactor` immediately, with no cooldown, no time-weighted average, and no minimum lock duration: [3](#0-2) 

`trigger`, invoked by `masterMagpie` when a referee claims, reads the referrer's *current* `factor` via `_calBoosted` and immediately writes the resulting `refererAmount` permanently into `refererInfo.rewardAmount`: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

Because `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` are point-in-time state rather than time-weighted or checkpointed, an attacker who is a registered code owner with a pending referee claim can:
1. Observe the referee's claim transaction in the mempool.
2. Front-run with `VLMGP.lock(largeAmount)`, which calls `updateTotalFactor(attacker)`, spiking `userInfos[attacker].factor` and `totalBoostFactor` in the same block.
3. Let the victim's claim execute `MasterMagpie -> ReferralStorage.trigger(referee, amount)`, which computes `boostesd = _calBoosted(attacker)` using the inflated factor and writes an inflated `refererAmount` into `refererInfo.rewardAmount` — this write is unconditional and permanent, not reversible by later factor changes.
4. Immediately call `VLMGP.startUnlock(largeAmount)` to begin exiting, which triggers another `updateTotalFactor` call that reduces `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` back down, but has no effect on the reward already credited in step 3.

None of the existing modifiers stop this: `lock`/`startUnlock` only carry `whenNotPaused`/`nonReentrant`, which do not prevent same-block state manipulation across separate transactions, and `trigger` has no time-lock or averaging safeguard on `factor`. The `_onlyMasterMagpie` and `_onlyVlMGP` modifiers correctly gate call origin but do nothing to prevent the underlying factor from being manipulated within a single block window.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft/diversion of unclaimed yield: the attacker captures a `refererAmount` in `trigger()` disproportionate to their sustained qualifying stake, funded from the same MGP reward pool that would otherwise back other referrers' legitimate rewards or admin-funded reward reserves. Since `refererInfo.rewardAmount` is claimable via `claimReward()` (paid out via `MGP.safeTransfer`), the inflated credit represents a real, quantifiable, permanent increase in claimable MGP for the attacker with no corresponding sustained collateral (`getUserTotalLocked`) once `startUnlock` follows. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" / "direct theft of user/protocol funds" impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are attacker-controlled and unprivileged: registering a referral code (`registerCode`), having at least one referee with a pending claim, and holding sufficient MGP capital to flash the lock (returned to attacker after `startUnlock`/cooldown, at worst subject to VLMGP's unlock penalty schedule, not principal loss). The only requirement is mempool visibility to front-run the referee's claim transaction and ordering it before `trigger` executes in the same block — feasible via standard MEV/front-running techniques on public mempools, and repeatable for every future qualifying referee claim. No governance, oracle, or admin rights are needed.

### Recommendation
Do not use instantaneous `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` in `_calBoosted`. Instead: (1) time-weight the boost factor (e.g., checkpoint balances and require a minimum holding duration before a lock counts toward `factor`), or (2) snapshot/lag the referrer's factor by one block or an epoch boundary so it cannot be altered in the same transaction/block as the `trigger` call, or (3) apply a cooldown after `lock` before the new factor becomes effective for boost calculations in `ReferralStorage`.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `ReferralStorage`, `VLMGP` (mocked `MasterMagpie`), and MGP token; fund `ReferralStorage` with MGP for `claimReward` payouts.
2. Register code owners `refA` (control) and `attacker`; have `refA` lock a fixed baseline MGP amount to establish `totalBoostFactor` baseline; set `refereeVictim.codeIUsed` to attacker's code.
3. Case A (no attack): compute `trigger(refereeVictim, amount)` with attacker's `factor` = 0 (no lock); record `refererInfo.rewardAmount` for attacker.
4. Case B (sandwich attack): in the same transaction/block, call `VLMGP.lock(largeAmount)` as attacker, then call `trigger(refereeVictim, amount)` (simulating MasterMagpie), then call `VLMGP.startUnlock(largeAmount)` as attacker.
5. Assert: `refererInfo.rewardAmount` credited to attacker in Case B is materially higher than any amount that would be produced from attacker's residual/sustained factor (i.e., near-zero after unlock), and higher than a control referrer's proportional share computed against the un-inflated `totalBoostFactor`.
6. Assert attacker regains their MGP principal after unlock/cooldown while retaining the inflated `rewardAmount`, demonstrating capital-light, repeatable extraction of unclaimed yield.

        UserInfo storage refereeInfo = userInfos[_referee];

        uint256 refereePercentage = (basic + boostesd) *  sharePercent / DENOMINATOR;
        uint256 refererAmount = _amount * refererPercentage / DENOMINATOR;
        uint256 refereeAmount = _amount * refereePercentage / DENOMINATOR;

        refererInfo.rewardAmount += refererAmount;
        refereeInfo.rewardAmount += refereeAmount;

        emit RefererRewardHarvested(_referer, refererAmount);
        emit RefereeRewardHarvested(_referee, refereeAmount);
