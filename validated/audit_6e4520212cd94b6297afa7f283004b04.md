### Title
Referral rebate boost is computed on an instantaneous, manipulable `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` snapshot, allowing self-referral reward inflation - (File: `rewards/ReferralStorage.sol`)

### Summary
`ReferralStorage.trigger()` computes the referrer's rebate percentage from `_calBoosted(_referer)`, which reads `userInfos[_referer].factor` and `totalBoostFactor` at the exact moment of the call [1](#0-0) . These values are updated synchronously and instantly whenever `vlMGP.lock`/`lockFor`/`startUnlock` is called via `updateTotalFactor()` [2](#0-1) , with no time-weighting, vesting, or snapshotting of the boost factor. An attacker controlling both a referee and a "sybil" referrer address can lock a large amount of MGP into `vlMGP` for the referrer immediately before triggering their own claim, inflate `refererAmount` for that single `trigger()` call, and then start unlocking, since nothing ties the boost to a durable holding period.

### Finding Description
`_calBoosted` is a pure spot-read of `userInfos[_account].factor / totalBoostFactor` [3](#0-2) . `factor` is `sqrt(getUserTotalLocked(_account))` and is recalculated on every `updateTotalFactor` call, which is invoked from `vlMGP` on lock/unlock-related state changes (confirmed at least in `startUnlock`) [4](#0-3) , and based on the `_onlyVlMGP` modifier pattern this is also expected to fire on `lock`/`lockFor` [5](#0-4) .

Because the boosted percentage is derived from the current locked amount rather than a time-integrated or checkpointed value, an attacker who controls both the referee (claiming) and the referrer (sybil) address can:
1. Call `vlMGP.lockFor(largeMGP, sybilReferrer)`, which synchronously updates `userInfos[sybilReferrer].factor` and `totalBoostFactor` upward.
2. In the same or immediately following transaction, trigger a MasterMagpie claim as the referee, which calls `ReferralStorage.trigger(referee, amount)` — this reads the now-inflated `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` and computes an inflated `refererPercentage`/`refererAmount`, crediting it to `refererInfo.rewardAmount` (permanently, added via `+=`) [6](#0-5) .
3. Call `vlMGP.startUnlock()` to begin unwinding the lock (the factor reduction happens only after this call, i.e., after the reward has already been captured).

The `_onlyMasterMagpie` modifier only restricts who can call `trigger`, not the state used inside it, so it does not stop this manipulation [7](#0-6) . There is no cooldown or minimum holding period enforced before a lock counts toward `factor`.

Note: full withdrawal of the locked MGP is not instantaneous — `unlock()` requires the cooldown slot's `endTime` to pass (`coolDownInSecs`) [8](#0-7) . So this is not a true same-block "flash loan reversal" for the principal, but the attacker's own capital is only temporarily locked for `coolDownInSecs`, not lost — while the inflated rebate is captured immediately and permanently in `refererInfo.rewardAmount`, independent of whether the lock is later kept or withdrawn.

### Impact Explanation
This allows theft of unclaimed MGP referral yield: an attacker inflates their own (sybil) `refererAmount` recorded in `ReferralStorage.userInfos`, later withdrawable via `claimReward()` [9](#0-8) . Since `totalBoostFactor` is shared among all vlMGP holders who registered a referral code, temporarily spiking one's own factor also dilutes/distorts the boosted share other honest referrers would have earned for rewards accrued in the same window, since the percentage is computed off the instantaneous ratio rather than time-weighted contribution. This matches "theft of unclaimed yield" impact.

### Likelihood Explanation
Feasible for any unprivileged actor who: (1) controls a referee and a self-created referrer/sybil address (trivial — referral codes are self-registrable via `registerCode`/`useCode` with no KYC), and (2) holds MGP capital to lock (their own capital is only temporarily encumbered for `coolDownInSecs`, not consumed). No special privileges are required, and the attack is repeatable each time the attacker (as referee) has a MasterMagpie reward claim to trigger. The main constraint is the size of `_amount` (claimed reward) multiplied by the achievable boost percentage — profitability scales with the size of the referee's claim and the amount of MGP the attacker is willing to temporarily lock to dominate `totalBoostFactor`.

### Recommendation
Do not compute referral boost from an instantaneous/spot `factor`; instead use a time-weighted or checkpointed factor (e.g., minimum holding duration before a lock counts, or an exponential moving average / snapshot taken at a fixed cadence) so that only durable locked positions influence `refererPercentage`. Alternatively, snapshot `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` at claim-eligibility time (e.g., at the start of the reward accrual period the claim is redeeming) rather than at the moment `trigger()` executes.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `VLMGP`, `ReferralStorage`, minimal `MasterMagpie` mock exposing `trigger` via the `_onlyMasterMagpie` path.
2. Attacker registers referral code as `sybilReferrer`; attacker's `referee` address calls `useCode` to link to `sybilReferrer`.
3. Baseline: have a second, honest referrer/referee pair with a modest, durable vlMGP lock; call `trigger(honestReferee, amount)` and record `refererAmount`.
4. Attack: within one transaction/block, have attacker call `vlMGP.lockFor(largeMGP, sybilReferrer)`, then call `trigger(referee, amount)` (simulating MasterMagpie claim) with the same `amount` as baseline, then call `vlMGP.startUnlock(largeMGP)` for `sybilReferrer`.
5. Assert `refererAmount` credited to `sybilReferrer` in step 4 is significantly larger than the honest referrer's `refererAmount` in step 3 for an identical `amount`, despite `sybilReferrer`'s lock existing only momentarily versus the honest referrer's durable lock — demonstrating the reward is not proportional to sustained locked value/time.
