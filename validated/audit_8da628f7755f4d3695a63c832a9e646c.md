### Title
ReferralStorage.trigger accrues `rewardAmount` without any corresponding MGP transfer into the contract, making claims first-come-first-served and insolvent - (File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol)

### Summary
`trigger()` is called by `MasterMagpie` on every `multiclaimFor` and increments `refererInfo.rewardAmount` / `refereeInfo.rewardAmount`, but no MGP tokens are moved into `ReferralStorage` at that time. `claimReward()` later pays out via `MGP.safeTransfer(msg.sender, userInfo.rewardAmount)` straight from whatever balance the contract happens to hold, so the ledger of promised rewards is not backed by an equal reserve of tokens.

### Finding Description
`trigger(address _referee, uint256 _amount)` <cite repo="bsaldua/contracts--015" path="rewards/ReferralStorage.sol" start="172,195" end="172,195" /> is invoked from `MasterMagpie._multiClaim` whenever any staker claims MGP rewards and has an active referral relationship: [1](#0-0) 

Inside `trigger()`, `refererInfo.rewardAmount` and `refereeInfo.rewardAmount` are simply incremented in storage: [2](#0-1) 

There is no `MGP.safeTransferFrom(...)` (or equivalent) call anywhere in `trigger()`, nor is there any other function in `ReferralStorage.sol` that tops up the contract's MGP balance to match the sum of all `rewardAmount` entries — the only place MGP leaves the contract is `claimReward()`, which pays from `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))` implicitly via `safeTransfer`: [3](#0-2) 

I searched the repository for any funding/provisioning path into `ReferralStorage` (`safeTransferFrom` targeting `MGP`, `fundReward`, `depositReward`, etc.) and found none — the only `MGP`-related transfer pattern in the codebase is `MGP.safeTransferFrom` used inside `VLMGP.sol`, not `ReferralStorage.sol`. This means the sum of `userInfos[x].rewardAmount` across all referrers/referees is an unbacked, unfunded liability: whoever calls `claimReward()` first drains real tokens, and later legitimate claimants revert on `safeTransfer` due to insufficient balance (Insufficient balance is not explicitly checked; the underlying ERC20 transfer will simply revert).

Regarding the specific precondition mentioned in the question ("attacker cancels a cooldown so their real lock rises with no factor refresh") — this affects `_calBoosted`/`updateTotalFactor`-based boost sizing, not the funding of the contract. I could not verify this precondition is required or even relevant: the unfunded-accrual issue is a structural property of `trigger()`/`claimReward()` that manifests during completely ordinary, permissionless use (any two accounts using `useCode`/`registerCode` plus normal `multiclaimFor` calls), independent of any cooldown-cancellation trick.

### Impact Explanation
This is a genuine accounting/insolvency defect: `ReferralStorage`'s internal ledger (`rewardAmount`) is not reconciled against `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))` at accrual time. In practice this results in a race where early claimants receive full payouts and later claimants' `claimReward()` calls revert once the balance is exhausted — a form of protocol insolvency for the referral reward sub-system. However, note that an attacker cannot mint themselves *extra* MGP beyond their own accrued entitlement this way; the "exploit" is really "be first to call `claimReward()`", not a way to steal funds beyond what's already recorded to their own account. The severity is best framed as a systemic design flaw causing DoS/insolvency for other referral participants rather than direct fund theft by an unprivileged attacker.

### Likelihood Explanation
No special privileges or capital are required — any two addresses can call `registerCode`/`useCode` and then have MGP rewards routed through `multiclaimFor`, triggering `trigger()` accrual with zero cost. The only real precondition is that `ReferralStorage` is deployed without any external funding mechanism actually being wired up in production (which I could not verify beyond what's present in this repo — it's possible an off-chain/admin funding process exists outside these contracts, but no such function exists in the code I reviewed).

### Recommendation
Have `MasterMagpie` (or `trigger()` itself, if given a source of funds) transfer the exact MGP amount being credited into `ReferralStorage` at the moment `trigger()` runs, e.g. `MGP.safeTransferFrom(masterMagpie, address(this), refererAmount + refereeAmount)`, so every increment to `rewardAmount` is immediately and atomically backed by tokens held in the contract. Add an invariant check/test asserting `sum(userInfos[*].rewardAmount) <= MGP.balanceOf(address(this))` at all times.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `ReferralStorage`, `MasterMagpie`, `MGP` token; wire `masterMagpie` address into `ReferralStorage`.
2. Register referrer/referee pair via `registerCode`/`useCode`.
3. Have `masterMagpie` call `trigger(referee, amount)` multiple times without ever transferring MGP into `ReferralStorage` (mirroring real `_multiClaim` behavior, since no such transfer exists in the code).
4. Assert `userInfos[referer].rewardAmount + userInfos[referee].rewardAmount > MGP.balanceOf(address(referralStorage))`.
5. Call `claimReward()` from referer — succeeds, draining the (possibly externally donated) balance.
6. Call `claimReward()` from referee — reverts with `InsufficientRewardBalance`/underlying ERC20 revert despite `rewardAmount > 0`, demonstrating the broken invariant and denial of legitimate claims.
