### Title
Protocol fee shares minted on gross accrued interest before bad-debt is written off, permanently overpaying the treasury from depositor funds - (File: `mainnet/contracts/vault/v0-vault-usdc.clar` and equivalent `v0-vault-*.clar` files)

### Summary
Every vault's `accrue()` mints treasury fee shares (`reserve-inc`/`treasury-lp`) proportional to the *gross* interest accrued on scaled debt, without any knowledge of whether that debt is ultimately collectible. When a position later becomes underwater and is liquidated with residual bad debt, `socialize-debt` write-down only reduces `total-borrowed`/`assets`/`lindex` for depositors — it never claws back the treasury shares that were already minted against the now-uncollectible portion of that interest. This is the same "fee accounted before position health/coverage is verified" root cause as the reported Overlay bug, just relocated from a single-transaction fee calculation into the accrue→liquidate lifecycle.

### Finding Description
In `accrue()` (present in `v0-vault-usdc.clar`, `v0-vault-stx.clar`, `v0-vault-sbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-ststx.clar`, `v0-vault-ststxbtc.clar`, `v0-vault-usdh.clar`, all identical logic): [1](#0-0) 
computes `debt-delta` from index growth on `scaled-principal`, takes `reserve-inc = debt-delta * fee-reserve / BPS`, converts it to `treasury-lp` vault shares, and unconditionally mints them to `.dao-treasury` via `ft-mint? zft treasury-lp .dao-treasury`. This mint happens on *every* accrual, for *every* borrower's scaled debt, including borrowers who are already or will become undercollateralized.

Later, when `liquidate()` determines a position has no collateral left to cover its debt, it calls the bad-debt socialization path: [2](#0-1) 
which for each remaining debt asset calls `vault-socialize-debt` (mapped to the vault's `socialize-debt`): [3](#0-2) 
`socialize-debt` reduces `principal-scaled`, `total-borrowed`, `assets`, and writes down `lindex` proportionally to the loss — but it does **not** touch the zToken `total-supply`, and specifically does not burn or claw back the `treasury-lp` shares previously minted to `.dao-treasury` for the interest portion of that now-uncollectible debt.

This mirrors the Overlay defect exactly in structure: a fee/share amount is recorded (minted) *before* the system verifies whether the underlying value it is drawn from is actually available (i.e., before the position's health/collateral-coverage outcome is known). When the debt later proves unrecoverable, the fee that was already paid out is not reversed, so the fee is effectively "double counted" — once as minted treasury shares, and again as socialized loss borne entirely by remaining depositors.

### Impact Explanation
The treasury permanently retains zToken shares that were minted based on interest that was never actually collected from the borrower. When `socialize-debt` writes down `lindex`, the loss is spread only across the remaining supply (which still includes the treasury's un-clawed-back shares), diluting depositors further while the treasury share count is untouched. This is a transfer of value from depositors' principal/yield to the treasury that should not have accrued — this lands on High impact: theft of unclaimed yield, since depositors lose yield-bearing share value to cover a fee that was booked against interest that was never actually earned/collectible.

### Likelihood Explanation
This triggers deterministically any time a borrower's position accrues interest and is later liquidated with residual bad debt requiring socialization (`no-collateral-left` branch in `liquidate()`), which is an ordinary, expected occurrence in undercollateralized market conditions — no attacker action or unusual configuration is required.

### Recommendation
Reduce (burn or reduce mint-eligible) the treasury's `treasury-lp`/reserve balance proportionally when `socialize-debt` is invoked, or defer/accrue protocol fee minting only against interest that has been confirmed collectible (e.g., net of any subsequently socialized bad debt), analogous to the recommended fix of adjusting `_userValueAdjusted`/`_feeAmount` only after the position's debt-coverage outcome is known in the original report.

### Proof of Concept
1. Borrower opens a debt position in `v0-vault-usdc`; over time interest accrues and `accrue()` repeatedly mints `treasury-lp` zUSDC shares to `.dao-treasury` proportional to `fee-reserve` on the growing debt ( [4](#0-3) ).
2. Collateral price drops; position becomes underwater. `liquidate()` seizes all remaining collateral (`coll-removed`), leaving `no-collateral-left = true` and residual scaled debt ( [5](#0-4) ).
3. `liquidate()` calls the bad-debt socialization fold ( [6](#0-5) ), which invokes `socialize-debt` in the vault, writing down `lindex`/`total-borrowed`/`assets` ( [7](#0-6) ).
4. The `treasury-lp` shares minted in step 1 remain fully intact in `.dao-treasury`'s balance — they are not reduced by the write-down, so the treasury's redeemable share of the vault's real assets exceeds what was actually earned, at the expense of remaining depositors.

(define-public (socialize-debt (scaled-amount uint))
  (let ((scaled-principal (var-get principal-scaled))
        (borrowed (var-get total-borrowed))
        (idx (var-get index))
        (current-assets (var-get assets))
        (current-lindex (var-get lindex))
        (old-total-assets (total-assets))
        (debt-reduction (mul-div-down scaled-amount idx INDEX-PRECISION))
        (principal-reduction (if (> scaled-principal u0)
                                (mul-div-down scaled-amount borrowed scaled-principal)
                                u0))
        ;; Write down lindex proportionally to loss in total-assets
        (new-lindex (if (and (> old-total-assets u0) (> old-total-assets debt-reduction))
                       (mul-div-down current-lindex (- old-total-assets debt-reduction) old-total-assets)
                       u0)))
