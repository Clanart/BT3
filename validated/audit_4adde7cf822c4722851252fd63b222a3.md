### Title
Reward sniping via front-run stake / instant withdraw in `queueNewRewards` / `_provisionReward` - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool._provisionReward` distributes an entire incoming reward amount instantly by bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` proportional to `totalStaked()` at that exact block, with no time-weighting, vesting, or streaming period. Because `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` are permissionless with no cooldown or minimum staking duration, an attacker can front-run a manager's `queueNewRewards` call, capture a full pro-rata share of the reward the instant it lands, and withdraw immediately after, diverting yield from stakers who were actually staked for the accrual period.

### Finding Description
`queueNewRewards` (onlyManager) calls `_provisionReward`, which does: [1](#0-0) 
`rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored` is incremented once, in full, based on `this.totalStaked()` measured at the moment of the call — there is no `periodFinish`/`rewardRate`/`lastUpdateTime` streaming mechanism as in typical Synthetix-style pools. `earned()` then computes reward purely from the user's current `balanceOf` (live MasterMagpie stake) times the delta in `rewardPerTokenStored` since their last checkpoint: [2](#0-1) 
`balanceOf` reads live stake from `MasterMagpie.stakingInfo`: [3](#0-2) 
Deposits and withdrawals in `MasterMagpie` are fully permissionless, unprivileged, with no lock/cooldown for the base staking token pools: [4](#0-3) 

Exploit flow:
1. Attacker observes a pending `queueNewRewards(amount, token)` transaction from a manager in the mempool.
2. Attacker front-runs with `MasterMagpie.deposit(stakingToken, largeAmount)`, inflating `totalStaked()`.
3. Manager's `queueNewRewards` executes; `rewardPerTokenStored` jumps by `(amount * 10**decimals) / totalStaked()`, and attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` was checkpointed at the pre-jump value (0 or lower) by the `_deposit` → `_harvestBaseRewarder` → `updateFor` call.
4. Attacker calls `withdraw` immediately (next block), which triggers `_harvestAndUnstake` → `_harvestBaseRewarder`/`getReward`, crediting `earned()` = `balanceOf(attacker) * (rewardPerTokenStored - paid) / 10**decimals`, i.e., the attacker's full pro-rata share of the entire reward despite staking for a single block.
5. Because `totalStaked()` at the time of provisioning already included the attacker's inflated balance, the attacker's presence also dilutes the reward share going to genuine long-term stakers relative to what they'd have received had the attacker not sniped it, while the attacker captures value with effectively zero holding-time cost.

No modifier, cooldown, minimum-stake-duration check, or streaming/vesting mechanism prevents this; `nonReentrant`/`whenNotPaused` on `deposit`/`withdraw` are irrelevant to this issue since the attack requires no reentrancy.

### Impact Explanation
This results in **theft of other stakers' unclaimed yield**: honest stakers who held the position throughout the actual accrual period receive a diluted share of the reward because a large flash-stake absorbed disproportionate reward relative to time staked. This maps to the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield." The economic loss is proportional to `attacker_share_of_totalStaked_at_provisioning * amountReward`, which can be made arbitrarily large by the attacker staking a sufficiently large amount relative to existing `totalStaked()` immediately before the reward drop.

### Likelihood Explanation
- No special privileges are required — `deposit`/`withdraw` on `MasterMagpie` are open to any EOA/contract.
- The only capital needed is the staking token itself (and possibly a flash loan if the staking token is flash-loanable, or simply large existing capital), held for one block.
- `queueNewRewards` calls are typically periodic/predictable (manager-driven reward distributions), making mempool front-running straightforward with standard MEV tooling (or simply same-block sandwiching via a bundle/private relay, or even just being fast on a public mempool).
- This is fully repeatable on every reward distribution event and does not require any privileged role, misconfiguration, or governance action.

### Recommendation
Replace the instantaneous lump-sum reward crediting with a time-weighted streaming model (e.g., Synthetix `StakingRewards`-style `rewardRate`/`periodFinish`/`lastUpdateTime`, so rewards accrue continuously over a duration rather than being fully credited to whoever holds `totalStaked()` share at the exact block of `queueNewRewards`). Alternatively/additionally, introduce a minimum staking duration or withdrawal cooldown before a user's stake counts toward `earned()` reward calculations, and/or checkpoint stake balances used for reward-share calculation prior to the block in which `queueNewRewards` executes (e.g., snapshotting).

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, `BaseRewardPool` (or `BaseRewardPoolV2`), a mock ERC20 staking token, and a mock reward token; register the pool via `add`.
2. Have "Alice" (honest staker) `deposit` a normal amount and let one epoch pass with no other stakers.
3. In block N, have "Attacker" call `deposit(stakingToken, largeAmount)` immediately before the manager's `queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, rewardToken)` transaction (same block, prior tx index, or sequential blocks emulating front-run).
4. Manager executes `queueNewRewards`.
5. In block N+1, Attacker calls `withdraw(stakingToken, largeAmount)` (or `getReward` via `MasterMagpie.multiclaim`).
6. Assert: `earned(attacker, rewardToken)` > 0 and proportionally equals `largeAmount / (largeAmount + aliceAmount) * rewardAmount`, despite the attacker having staked for a single block versus Alice's full epoch — i.e., assert attacker's `earned()` reward per unit time staked vastly exceeds Alice's, demonstrating violation of time-proportional reward conservation.
7. Compare against the reward Alice would have received absent the attacker's stake (`rewardAmount` fully attributable to Alice) to quantify the diverted/stolen amount.
