### Title
Reward-theft via same-transaction stake-then-harvest in `voteAndCast`/`castVotes` - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol)

### Summary
`voteAndCast()` calls `vote()` (which credits new vlMGP stake into `BribeRewardPool` via `stakeFor`) immediately followed by `castVotes()` (which harvests bribes from Wombat and pushes them into the same `BribeRewardPool` via reward provisioning). Because reward accounting is Synthetix-style (`rewardPerTokenStored` based purely on `totalStaked()` at provisioning time), an attacker can inject a large stake one instruction before the harvest and capture a full proportional share of bribes that accrued over the entire prior epoch, diluting genuine long-term voters.

### Finding Description
`vote()` increases the caller's balance in the pool's `BribeRewardPool` through `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))`. [1](#0-0) 

`stakeFor` is guarded by `updateRewards(_for, rewardTokens)`, which snapshots `userRewards`/`userRewardPerTokenPaid` for the account's *old* balance before `totalSupply`/`_balances[_for]` are incremented: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

Immediately after, `castVotes()` triggers `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, which harvests pending bribes and (via the reward pool's `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`) increases `rewardPerTokenStored` by `_amountReward * 1e18 / totalStaked()` where `totalStaked()` already includes the attacker's freshly staked balance from the `vote()` call executed one instruction earlier in the same transaction: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

Because the attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` was just set to the *pre-harvest* `rewardPerTokenStored` value inside `stakeFor`'s `updateRewards` modifier, the subsequent `_earned` calculation attributes the entire just-queued `rewardPerToken` delta to the attacker's newly-added balance, exactly as if that balance had been staked for the whole period the bribe accrued: [6](#0-5) 

`castVotes()` has no cooldown or minimum bonding period between `vote()` and reward harvesting — `lastCastTime` is only recorded, never enforced as a delay: [7](#0-6) 

The reward the attacker just captured is independently claimable via `getReward`/`claimBribe`, decoupled from unstaking: [8](#0-7) [9](#0-8) 

No existing modifier, reentrancy guard, or time-weighting mechanism (e.g., checkpointing stake duration, or updating rewards using the pre-stake `totalStaked()` for the harvest) prevents this. This is not a mismatch between `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp` and `IBribeRewardPool.balanceOf` (those two remain internally consistent, since both are updated together in `vote()`); rather, the vulnerability is that `balanceOf` itself is used unweighted-by-time to distribute a lump-sum bribe.

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged attacker who has locked vlMGP can call `voteAndCast()` (or manually sequence `vote()` then `castVotes()`) to insert a large stake immediately ahead of a bribe harvest and claim a proportional share of that epoch's entire bribe distribution, diluting the rightful share of voters who held their position for the whole period. This is a direct transfer of value (bribe rewards) away from legitimate long-term voters to the attacker, matching a reward-theft / fund-diversion impact.

### Likelihood Explanation
- Requires only vlMGP the attacker already controls (no privileged role needed).
- Fully reachable through public functions `vote()`/`castVotes()`/`voteAndCast()`.
- Repeatable every time a meaningful amount of bribes is queued for harvest, and profitability scales with the size of the pending, un-harvested bribe pool and the size of the attacker's last-second stake relative to total stake.
- The only capital cost is the vlMGP being staked (temporarily), which is not consumed and can be un-staked later.

### Recommendation
Decouple reward provisioning from instantaneous `balanceOf`/`totalStaked()`: use a stake that is time-weighted (e.g., checkpoint balances prior to the block in which the harvest occurs, or require a minimum bonding delay after `stakeFor` before a stake becomes eligible for freshly queued rewards). Alternatively, disallow `vote()`+`castVotes()` (or `voteAndCast()`) from executing in the same transaction/block, or snapshot `totalStaked()`/eligible balances at the start of the epoch rather than at harvest time.

### Proof of Concept
Hardhat test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, a `BribeRewardPool`, mock `WombatStaking`/`IWombatVoter` returning a controllable bribe amount on `vote()`.
2. Victim locks vlMGP and calls `vote()` early, staking into the pool; let bribes accrue (mock harvester queues rewards once with victim as sole staker) to establish a baseline `rewardPerTokenStored`.
3. Before the next harvest, attacker locks vlMGP and, in a single transaction, calls `voteAndCast(...)` with a large positive delta for the same pool.
4. Assert: attacker's `earned(attacker, rewardToken)` immediately after the transaction is > 0 and proportionally equal to `attackerStake / totalStaked() * bribeJustHarvested`, despite attacker having staked zero blocks prior to the harvest.
5. Assert victim's realized share of that specific harvested bribe drops relative to what they would have received had the attacker's stake not been added right before the harvest (i.e., victim's `earned` is diluted by `totalStaked()` including the attacker's fresh stake).
6. Confirm attacker can call `claimBribe`/`claimAllBribes` to withdraw the stolen share, then `vote()` with a negative delta to unstake, completing the round-trip with net profit and no exposure to time risk.
