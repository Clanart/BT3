The CVE describes an internal InnoDB hang/crash triggerable by a high-privileged attacker within MySQL's own engine. The closest reachable analog in this codebase — a spend-bundle-triggered mempool/CLVM processing hang — is already extensively mitigated and tested:

- `MempoolManager.pre_validate_spendbundle()` enforces `max_tx_clvm_cost`, an atom/pair density check (`too many atoms`/`too many pairs`), a `validation_timeout` duration guard, and a ns/cost outlier guard, all specifically designed to prevent CLVM-execution-based DoS from a single submitted spend bundle. - `Mempool.create_bundle_from_mempool_items()`/`create_block_generator2()` bound total cost, spend count, atoms, and pairs per block, with `MAX_SKIPPED_ITEMS` and timeout-based early exit to avoid unbounded block-building work.
