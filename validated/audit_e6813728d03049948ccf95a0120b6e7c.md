### Title
Permissionless `harvestAll()` allows reward-sniping via instantaneous stake before bribe distribution - ([File: rewards/DelegateVoteRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`harvestAll()` in `DelegateVoteRewardPool` has no access-control modifier, so any address can trigger it at will. [1](#0-0)  Because reward distribution converts a lump-sum harvested bribe amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` using the *current* `totalSupply`/`balanceOf` snapshot rather than time-weighted accrual, an attacker can inflate their `_balances` right before calling `harvestAll()` and capture a share of externally-accrued bribes proportional to their instantaneous stake, diluting the share earned by depositors who held their position for the entire accrual period.

### Finding Description
`stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` are restricted to `onlyOperator` (the `WombatBribeManager`), but any vlMGP holder can trigger them indirectly by calling `WombatBribeManager.vote()` with the `delegatedPool` as target, which calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` on `DelegateVoteRewardPool`. [2](#0-1)  This immediately checkpoints the caller's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at the *pre-harvest* `rewardPerTokenStored` value via the `updateRewards` modifier, and increases `totalSupply`/`_balances[caller]`. [3](#0-2) 

Immediately afterward, the attacker (or anyone) calls `harvestAll()` directly - it carries no `onlyOperator`, `onlyOwner`, or timing restriction. [1](#0-0)  This pulls in bribes that accrued externally over time (based on `DelegateVoteRewardPool`'s external votes cast via `_updateVote()`), and distributes them via `_manageRewards` → `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`, which computes:
```
rewardPerTokenStored += (amountReward * 1e_decimals) / totalSupply
```
using the totalSupply *at the instant of the call*. [4](#0-3)  Because `earned()` credits every account proportionally to `balanceOf(account) * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`, a staker who just entered gets full proportional exposure to the newly added `rewardPerTokenStored` delta, identical to accounts that contributed stake for the entire bribe-accrual period. [5](#0-4)  There is no time-weighting, vesting, or streaming (e.g., a `rewardRate` accrued per second as in Synthetix-style `StakingRewards`) — the whole lump sum is claimable at the instant of `harvestAll()`.

Existing checks do not prevent this: `onlyOperator` only gates `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` calls (which the attacker can still reach legitimately through `vote()`), and `harvestAll()` itself is unguarded. The `updateRewards` modifier correctly checkpoints the caller's own rate at stake time, but this checkpoint is exactly what lets a zero-duration staker piggyback on the very next `rewardPerTokenStored` bump.

### Impact Explanation
This falls under "theft of unclaimed yield" - value that should accrue to long-standing `delegatedPool` depositors is redirected to an attacker who briefly inflates their balance immediately before calling the permissionless `harvestAll()`, then can immediately unvote (via `WombatBribeManager.unvote`/`vote` with negative delta, which claims via `_getReward`) to realize the gain with negligible holding duration and capital lock-up. Since bribe amounts can be economically significant (voting bribes across all Wombat pools funneled through the delegate pool), repeated sniping proportionally dilutes rewards earned by genuine long-term voters.

### Likelihood Explanation
- No special privileges are required: any vlMGP holder can call `WombatBribeManager.vote()` to increase their delegate-pool stake, then call the fully public `DelegateVoteRewardPool.harvestAll()`.
- The attack is repeatable every time unclaimed external bribes exist and have not yet been harvested (e.g., ahead of the next scheduled `castVotes()` call, which itself triggers `harvestAll()`). [6](#0-5) 
- Capital requirement is limited to the vlMGP lock needed to gain vote weight; no flash loan is needed since votes are based on locked vlMGP balances, but the timing of stake/harvest/unstake requires no cooldown in the reviewed code, making the front-run trivially executable in a single transaction or block.

### Recommendation
Restrict `harvestAll()` to trusted callers (e.g., `onlyOperator`/`onlyOwner`/a rate-limited keeper role), and/or change the internal reward accounting to a time-weighted/streamed model (e.g., accrue `rewardRate` over a duration rather than crediting a lump sum against instantaneous `totalSupply`). Alternatively, snapshot `totalSupply`/eligible balances at the time bribes were earned externally rather than at harvest time, and/or add a minimum holding period before newly staked balances become eligible for already-accrued rewards.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `DelegateVoteRewardPool` with a mock `IWombatBribeManager` operator and a mock reward token.
2. Simulate two depositors: `victim` stakes `100e18` via `stakeFor` (as operator) at `t0` and remains staked.
3. Advance time / accrue bribes externally in the mock bribe manager such that `claimAllBribes(address(this))` inside `harvestAll()` will return a fixed `1000e18` reward amount attributable to votes cast over the whole period `victim` was staked.
4. Just before calling `harvestAll()`, have `attacker` call `stakeFor` (via mock operator, simulating `vote()`) with `900e18`, making `totalSupply = 1000e18`.
5. Call `harvestAll()` (unprivileged, direct call, no modifier).
6. Assert `earned(attacker, rewardToken)` ≈ `900e18/1000e18 * 1000e18 = 900e18` even though `attacker` held stake for 0 blocks, while `victim`, who held stake for the entire accrual period, only receives `100e18` — disproportionate to accrual duration.
7. Assert that if `attacker` had not front-run the harvest (staked after harvest instead), `attacker.earned == 0` and `victim.earned == 1000e18`, demonstrating the reallocation caused purely by call ordering of the permissionless `harvestAll()`.
