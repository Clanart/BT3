### Title
Instant lump-sum reward distribution in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` allows front-run "flash-vote" sandwich to steal bribe rewards owed to time-weighted voters - (File: `rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol`, `rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol`, `wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol`)

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager.vote` immediately increases a pool's `totalSupply` in `BribeRewardPool` via `stakeFor`, and `castVotes` triggers `wombatStaking.vote`, which harvests previously-accrued bribes and (via `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`) distributes them as a lump sum proportional to whatever `totalSupply` is at that instant. Because reward accrual is not time-weighted (no streaming rate or vesting), an attacker can inject a large vote delta immediately before `castVotes` is mined, capture a share of rewards that accrued entirely before they voted, and then withdraw via `unvote`.

### Finding Description
`BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward` (`rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol:290-314`) distributes newly queued rewards by adding `amount * 1e_decimals / totalStaked()` to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single lump sum at the moment the reward is queued — there is no time-based streaming (`rewardRate`/`periodFinish`) and no minimum holding period. [1](#0-0) 

`WombatBribeManager.vote` lets any user with sufficient `getUserVotable` (locked vlMGP) capacity increase `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp` and immediately call `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)`, which bumps `BribeRewardPool.totalSupply` instantly. [2](#0-1) 

`castVotes` computes the vote deltas to submit to `wombatStaking.vote(...)` using the pool's current `totalVoteInVlmgp` (already including any front-run delta), and that call harvests bribes that accrued since the last cast and forwards them to each pool's `BribeRewardPool` via `queueNewRewards`, which uses the *current* `totalSupply` (already inflated by the attacker's just-injected stake) to compute `rewardPerTokenStored`. [3](#0-2) 

`BribeRewardPool.stakeFor` runs the `updateRewards` modifier before increasing `totalSupply`/`_balances`, which finalizes the attacker's already-accrued rewards at the *old* `rewardPerTokenStored` and sets `userRewardPerTokenPaid` to that old value — meaning any subsequent bump to `rewardPerTokenStored` (from the harvested bribes) is earned in full by the attacker's newly added balance, with zero time exposure. [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

`unvote`/`withdrawFor` has no cooldown or lock, so the attacker can immediately reduce their stake back to zero right after the reward bump, having captured a disproportionate share of the rewardPerToken increase relative to actual time-weighted participation. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

No existing mechanism (no `nonReentrant`-style time lock, no minimum staking duration, no reward vesting in this reward path) prevents this: `_earned` computes reward purely from `balanceOf * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`, which is agnostic to how long the balance existed. [8](#0-7) 

### Impact Explanation
Long-term voters who held their vote position through the entire period between two `castVotes` calls have their share of that period's harvested bribes diluted by an attacker's freshly injected, zero-duration stake. This is a direct theft of unclaimed yield — the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield" — because the reward pool's fixed distribution is redirected proportionally to capital that was present for less than one block instead of the full accrual window.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker only needs: (1) enough already-locked vlMGP capacity (`getUserVotable`) to satisfy `NotEnoughVote` check, and (2) the ability to front-run/observe a pending `castVotes`/`castVotesAndClaimBribes` transaction (public mempool visibility, no special permission). This is a standard, low-cost, repeatable MEV/sandwich pattern executable by any unprivileged EOA every time a `castVotes` (or `castVotesAndClaimBribes`, `voteAndCast`) transaction is broadcast, since `castVotes` triggers a lump-sum harvest+distribution rather than a stream.

### Recommendation
Convert bribe reward distribution to a time-weighted/streamed model (e.g., `rewardRate` over a fixed duration as in Synthetix `StakingRewards`) instead of an instantaneous `rewardPerTokenStored += amount/totalStaked()` lump sum, or impose a minimum holding/vesting period (checkpointed against `block.timestamp`) before newly staked vote weight becomes eligible for the currently-pending reward batch, and/or snapshot `totalSupply`/eligible balances at the start of each voting epoch (before `wombatStaking.vote` is invoked) rather than using the live, front-runnable `totalSupply`.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `BribeRewardPool`, mock `WombatStaking`/`veWom`/bribe contracts, and vlMGP with lockable balances for a long-term voter (Alice) and an attacker (Bob).
2. Alice locks vlMGP and calls `vote(pool, +100e18)` at `t0`; let bribes accrue in the underlying Wombat bribe contract over a long period (simulate `t0` to `t0+N days`).
3. Just before broadcasting/mining a `castVotes(false)` call, have Bob (who already holds locked vlMGP capacity) call `vote(pool, +100e18)` in the preceding transaction (same block or immediately prior).
4. Execute `castVotes(false)`, which triggers `wombatStaking.vote` → harvest → `queueNewRewards` on `BribeRewardPool`.
5. Immediately after, Bob calls `unvote(pool)` to withdraw his `100e18` vote weight.
6. Assert: `BribeRewardPool.earned(bob, rewardToken) > 0` despite Bob having zero time-weighted exposure, and compare `earned(bob)` vs `earned(alice)` — expect Bob's share to be ~50% of the harvested reward (equal to Alice's, despite holding stake for a single transaction vs. the entire accrual period), violating the intended time-weighted distribution.
