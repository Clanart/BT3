### Title
`ReferralStorage.trigger()` accrues referral rewards without ever funding the contract with MGP - Protocol insolvency ([File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol])

### Summary
`ReferralStorage.trigger()` is invoked by `MasterMagpie` on every reward claim and only increments `refererInfo.rewardAmount` / `refereeInfo.rewardAmount`; it never transfers or mints any MGP into the `ReferralStorage` contract. Because `claimReward()` and `multiclaimFor` later pay out from `MGP.safeTransfer(msg.sender, rewardAmount)` against whatever balance the contract happens to hold, the sum of all outstanding `rewardAmount` entries is not backed by tokens actually held, making the system insolvent by construction and first-come-first-served for claimants.

### Finding Description
`trigger()` is restricted to `masterMagpie` via `_onlyMasterMagpie` [1](#0-0) , and is called from `MasterMagpie._multiClaim` (reached from the permissionless `multiclaimFor` path) every time a referred account claims rewards: `IReferralStorage(referral).trigger(_user, totalReward)` [2](#0-1) .

Inside `trigger()`, the function computes `refererAmount` and `refereeAmount` from the referee's claimed `_amount` and simply does:
```
refererInfo.rewardAmount += refererAmount;
refereeInfo.rewardAmount += refereeAmount;
```
with no corresponding `MGP.safeTransferFrom(...)`, mint, or any other token movement into `ReferralStorage` [3](#0-2) .

Later, `claimReward()` pays out strictly from the contract's own MGP balance: `MGP.safeTransfer(msg.sender, userInfo.rewardAmount)` [4](#0-3) . There is no deposit/fund function anywhere in `ReferralStorage.sol` that tops up the contract's MGP balance in step with the bookkeeping done in `trigger()` — the state variables and constructor only wire up `vlMGP`, `MGP` (the token address), and `masterMagpie` [5](#0-4) .

Because every `multiclaimFor` call by any referred account (referee) increases the aggregate `rewardAmount` liability without moving a single token into the contract, the sum of all `rewardAmount` values across `userInfos` diverges unboundedly from the actual MGP balance held by `ReferralStorage`. Any account can accrue a claim (via being referred and having rewards routed through `MasterMagpie.multiclaimFor`), but only whoever calls `claimReward()` first — up to the size of whatever balance exists — will succeed; everyone else's `MGP.safeTransfer` will revert due to insufficient balance. No modifier, pause, or accounting check in the contract enforces that accrued `rewardAmount` is bound by held tokens.

### Impact Explanation
This is a genuine unbacked-liability design flaw: `ReferralStorage`'s internal ledger (`rewardAmount`) can grow arbitrarily through routine (or attacker-amplified, e.g., self-referral loops across many accounts calling `multiclaimFor` in the same block) protocol usage while the contract's real MGP balance stays at whatever was incidentally transferred in (potentially zero). This matches "Critical - Protocol insolvency": legitimate referrers/referees accrue entitlements that can never all be honored, and whichever claimant transacts first drains the available balance, permanently freezing the rest.

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged role is required. Any account can register/use a referral code (`registerCode`, `useCode`) and route reward claims through the permissionless `MasterMagpie.multiclaimFor`, which unconditionally invokes `trigger()` for every claim [2](#0-1) . Because this happens on essentially every normal reward-claim flow (not just a crafted attack), the underfunding condition is triggered by routine protocol usage, making it highly likely to manifest without any special capital or skill, and repeatable across every claim cycle.

### Recommendation
Have `trigger()` (or the caller in `MasterMagpie`) actually move the corresponding MGP amount into `ReferralStorage` at the moment of accrual (e.g., `MGP.safeTransferFrom(masterMagpie, address(this), refererAmount + refereeAmount)`), or fund `ReferralStorage` from the same minting/treasury source used for `_sendMGP`/`_sendMGPForVlMGPPool` in `MasterMagpie`, so that every increment to `rewardAmount` is atomically backed by a token transfer into the contract before `claimReward()`/`multiclaimFor` can be called against it. Additionally, add an invariant check (e.g., in `claimReward()`) that the contract's `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))` never drops below total outstanding `rewardAmount` liabilities.

### Proof of Concept
Hardhat/Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, `VLMGP`, and `ReferralStorage`; wire `ReferralStorage.masterMagpie` to `MasterMagpie` and register a staking pool with MGP emissions.
2. Have account `A` call `registerCode(codeA)`; have accounts `B1..Bn` call `useCode(codeA)` to become referees of `A`.
3. Have `B1..Bn` stake into `MasterMagpie` and accrue MGP rewards, then call `multiclaimFor` (permissionless) for all of them in a single block — this repeatedly calls `ReferralStorage.trigger(_referee, totalReward)`, growing `userInfos[A].rewardAmount` and each `userInfos[Bi].rewardAmount` without any MGP being sent to `ReferralStorage`.
4. Assert `MGP.balanceOf(address(ReferralStorage)) == 0` (or far less than) while `sum(userInfos[*].rewardAmount) > 0`.
5. Call `claimReward()` from `A` — expect it either reverts (`InsufficientRewardBalance`/ERC20 transfer revert) proving unbacked accrual, or succeeds only because of incidental funding, after which subsequent claimers (`B1..Bn`) revert on `MGP.safeTransfer` due to insufficient balance, demonstrating first-come-first-served insolvency.
6. Assert that `sum(userInfos[*].rewardAmount)` after step 3 exceeds `MGP.balanceOf(address(ReferralStorage))`, proving the entitlement-vs-backing invariant is broken.

        refereeInfo.rewardAmount += refereeAmount;
