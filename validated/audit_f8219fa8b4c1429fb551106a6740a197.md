## Title
Potential double-distribution of `pending_delegator_rewards` block revenue if the vote account's liability field is not decremented on payout — (File: `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/calculation.rs`, `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/distribution.rs`)

## Summary
This is the closest Agave analog to the reported `YieldToken.receiveYield` bug. In the ERC‑20 report, depositing yield increases both the "already accounted for" ledger (`yieldPerTokenStored`) and the underlying pooled assets, so the same value gets paid out to holders more than once because the source liability is never consumed. The Agave equivalent (SIMD‑0123 "block revenue sharing") has the same shape: `DepositDelegatorRewards` credits a vote account's `pending_delegator_rewards` liability field [1](#0-0) , and every epoch `calculate_block_reward` reads that same field from the vote-account snapshot to compute each staker's proportional share and mints new lamports directly into stake accounts [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .

## Finding Description
`deposit_delegator_rewards` transfers lamports into the vote account via CPI and then unconditionally increments `pending_delegator_rewards` with `add_pending_delegator_rewards` [4](#0-3) . This value is treated as an outstanding, unpaid liability — it is explicitly reserved from withdrawal (`withdraw` blocks removing lamports below `pending_delegator_rewards`) [5](#0-4) .

Each epoch, `calculate_block_reward` reads `vote_state.pending_delegator_rewards()` from the **cached** vote-account view for that epoch and computes a per-stake share of it: `pending_delegator_rewards * stake / total_active_stake`, clamped to `pending_delegator_rewards` [6](#0-5) . This is invoked from `calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions` for every stake delegation when `block_revenue_sharing` is active [7](#0-6) .

During distribution, `build_updated_stake_reward` credits the computed `block_reward` directly onto the **stake account's** lamports (`account.checked_add_lamports(partitioned_stake_reward.block_reward)`) [3](#0-2) , and `store_stake_accounts_in_partition` only stores the updated **stake** accounts and tallies `block_reward_lamports_distributed`/`block_reward_lamports_burned` metrics [8](#0-7) .

Critically, in the code paths I was able to inspect (`calculation.rs` and the reviewed portion of `distribution.rs`), there is no corresponding debit of the vote account's lamports balance nor decrement of its `pending_delegator_rewards` field to reflect that the liability was paid out. If the liability field is never reduced after a payout epoch, the same `pending_delegator_rewards` value (or its proportional share) is read again in the next epoch's `calculate_rewards_for_partitioning` → `calculate_stake_rewards_and_commissions` → `calculate_block_reward` cycle and freshly minted onto stake accounts again — i.e., the identical liability is paid out repeatedly instead of being consumed once, exactly analogous to `receiveYield` inflating share value without correspondingly reducing/consuming the yield source.

**I was not able to fully confirm within the available context whether `pending_delegator_rewards` is zeroed/decremented elsewhere** (e.g. in a part of `distribution.rs` or `vote_reward.rs` not shown by my searches, or in a separate vote-account state update issued alongside `store_stake_accounts_in_partition`). The grep for `pending_delegator_rewards -=`, `saturating_sub`, or `checked_sub` against that field returned no matches anywhere in the indexed codebase, which is the strongest signal supporting the concern, but the index may not have surfaced every reference (e.g. `fee_distribution.rs` had 5 matches for related terms that I could not inspect in this pass).

## Impact Explanation
If confirmed, this would mean stake accounts are minted lamports out of thin air every epoch that block revenue sharing is active and `pending_delegator_rewards > 0`, since the "liability" backing the payout is never consumed. This breaks the fundamental capitalization invariant that `bank.capitalization()` tracks (unbacked lamports appearing in stake accounts with no corresponding debit anywhere), which is asserted elsewhere in the codebase (`assert_capitalization`) and is core to Agave's consensus-critical accounting. This would constitute fund creation from nothing (theft of value from the rest of the network's holders via dilution) and would eventually cause bank-hash/consensus mismatches if capitalization bookkeeping and actual lamport supply diverge across validators recalculating differently.

## Likelihood Explanation
This depends entirely on whether a decrement of `pending_delegator_rewards` (and corresponding vote-account lamport debit) exists in code paths outside what I was able to inspect. Given the amount of related matches in `distribution.rs` (20) and `fee_distribution.rs` (5) that were not fully reviewed, I cannot assert with high confidence that this is a genuine unresolved bug rather than a false negative from incomplete search coverage. This should be treated as a **candidate finding requiring verification**, not a confirmed vulnerability.

## Recommendation
Verify in `runtime/src/bank/partitioned_epoch_rewards/distribution.rs` (the portions beyond what was reviewed) and `runtime/src/bank/fee_distribution.rs` whether the vote account's `pending_delegator_rewards` field and lamport balance are decremented/debited in the same commit/slot that stake accounts are credited with `block_reward`. If no such debit exists, `pending_delegator_rewards` must be reduced by the exact `block_reward_lamports_distributed` amount for each vote account whose stake accounts were just paid, and the vote account's lamports must be reduced by that same amount (mirroring the stake-account credit), so the liability is consumed exactly once.

## Proof of Concept
Not produced — this report is a candidate analog derived from static code reading; I could not fully trace the vote-account-side debit logic through to confirm exploitability. A concrete PoC would require:
1. Enabling `block_revenue_sharing` feature and calling `DepositDelegatorRewards` to set `pending_delegator_rewards = X` on a vote account.
2. Advancing through two reward epochs without any further deposit.
3. Observing whether stake accounts delegated to that vote account receive `block_reward` credits in **both** epochs (each proportional to the same un-decremented `X`), and whether `bank.capitalization()` grows by more than the sum of legitimate inflation + the single deposited `X`.

Given the uncertainty in confirming the missing decrement, I recommend a Devin session with full-repository access (beyond this ask-only index) to trace every write site of `pending_delegator_rewards` and every debit of vote-account lamports tied to `block_reward` distribution before treating this as a confirmed, reportable vulnerability.
