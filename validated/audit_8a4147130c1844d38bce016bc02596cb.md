### Title
`updateEndRemainingAllocation()` is permissionlessly re-callable and lets an attacker shrink the bonus denominator to steal from `totalBonus` at other claimants' expense - (`File: rewards/Airdrop.sol`)

### Summary
`Airdrop.updateEndRemainingAllocation()` is a `public` function with no access control and no "already set" guard, so it can be invoked directly (not only through `claim()`) at any time after `periodsEndTime[4]`. Because `totalRemainingAllocation` monotonically shrinks as more users claim, calling this function again re-snapshots `totalEndRemainingAllocation` to a smaller value, letting a caller who resets it immediately before their own `claim()` receive an inflated share of the fixed `totalBonus` pool at the expense of everyone who claims afterward with the now-inconsistent denominator.

### Finding Description
`getBonusAmount` computes a user's bonus as: [1](#0-0) 
The correctness of this formula (i.e., that the sum of all bonuses paid across every remaining claimant equals `totalBonus`) relies on `totalEndRemainingAllocation` being a single, immutable snapshot taken exactly once, at `periodsEndTime[4]`, of the sum of allocations of every user who still has to claim.

However, `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` has no protection against being invoked more than once: [2](#0-1) 
`claim()` only auto-invokes it when `totalEndRemainingAllocation == 0`: [3](#0-2) 
but since `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` is `public`, any unprivileged address can call it directly at any later time, unconditionally overwriting `totalEndRemainingAllocation` with the current (already-shrunk) `totalRemainingAllocation`, because every prior `claim()` call subtracts `userAllocation` from `totalRemainingAllocation` regardless of timing: [4](#0-3) 

Exploit flow:
1. At `periodsEndTime[4]`, the attacker (or anyone) calls `claim()`, which lazily sets `totalEndRemainingAllocation = D0` (the large initial pool of unclaimed allocations).
2. Over time, honest users claim their fully-vested allocations using denominator `D0`, correctly consuming their proportional share of `totalBonus`, and `totalRemainingAllocation` shrinks toward `D1 < D0`.
3. The attacker front-runs their own `claim()` with a direct call to `updateEndRemainingAllocation()`, resetting `totalEndRemainingAllocation` to the smaller current value `D1`.
4. The attacker's `claim()` then computes `bonusAmount = attackerAllocation * totalBonus / D1`, a larger share than they would have received under the original, correct `D0` snapshot, since `totalBonus` itself is unaffected by the reset but the divisor shrank.

Because different claimants end up using different, inconsistent denominators (`D0` for earlier claimers, `D1` for later ones) against the same fixed `totalBonus`, the sum of bonuses actually paid out no longer conserves to `totalBonus`. The last claimant(s) to exploit this can extract more MGP than their fair proportional share, depleting the contract's token balance and causing later legitimate claimants to receive less than their entitled bonus or hit `InsufficientBalance()` reverts, freezing their funds.

No existing modifier, reentrancy guard, or accounting check prevents this, since `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` has no `onlyOwner`/`internal` restriction and no "set-once" flag.

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct theft of unclaimed/yield funds: an unprivileged caller can manipulate a shared global denominator to redirect part of the `totalBonus` pool (funds forfeited by other users) to themselves beyond their fair proportional share, at the expense of other remaining claimants whose bonus is diluted or whose claim can revert due to insufficient contract balance. This matches the Immunefi impact classes "direct theft of user funds" and "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield."

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged role is required — `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` and `claim()` are both `external`/`public` with no access control. The only precondition is that the attacker (or an accomplice) has a nonzero `allocations[]` entry (i.e., is registered for the airdrop, which is a normal, expected state for many addresses) and can time a call to `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` immediately before their own `claim()` after other claimants have already reduced `totalRemainingAllocation`. This is trivially repeatable by any of the many airdrop-eligible addresses and requires no special capital, only ordinary transaction sequencing/front-running.

### Recommendation
Make the snapshot immutable: guard `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` so it can only set `totalEndRemainingAllocation` once (e.g., `if (totalEndRemainingAllocation != 0) return;` or track a separate boolean flag), and/or restrict it to be called only internally from within `claim()`'s existing `== 0` check, removing public re-invocation entirely.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `Airdrop` with `startTime = block.timestamp + 1`; register several addresses (`user1..userN`) plus attacker addresses `A` and `B` with allocations.
2. Warp to a time within period 0-3 and have `user1..userK` call `claim()` early to build up `totalBonus` via forfeiture (`totalBonus` increases per `claim()`'s forfeiture branch).
3. Warp to exactly `periodsEndTime[4]`; call `claim()` from `A`, capturing `totalEndRemainingAllocation` as `D0` (assert via public getter).
4. Warp forward and have remaining honest users (`userK+1..userM`) call `claim()`, consuming `totalRemainingAllocation` down to a smaller value.
5. Have attacker's address `B` call `updateEndRemainingAllocation()` directly (not via `claim()`), then immediately call `claim()` from `B`. Assert `totalEndRemainingAllocation` dropped to `D1 < D0` and that `B`'s `bonusAmount` (recovered from the transfer event/balance delta) exceeds `B_allocation/D0 * totalBonus` (the fair share under the original snapshot).
6. Have the remaining legitimate claimants attempt `claim()` and assert either reduced bonus payout or an `InsufficientBalance()` revert, demonstrating the contract can no longer pay out the intended amounts — proving the conservation invariant `sum(bonusAmount_i) == totalBonus` is violated.
