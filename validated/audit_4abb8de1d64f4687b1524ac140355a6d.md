**Invariant being tested:** Can a privileged or semi-privileged actor pre-set some identifier/address/code that is consumed in a "first-come, first-served" DB check, redirecting bridge funds or reimbursement to the wrong party?

**Candidate 1 — `insert_unspent_kickoff_sigs_if_not_exist` (silent `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`, no mismatch check)** [1](#0-0) 

Unlike `insert_operator_if_not_exists` and `insert_operator_kickoff_winternitz_public_keys_if_not_exist`, this function has **no mismatch check** after a conflict. However, it is only reachable through `verifier::set_operator`, which is gated by mTLS — only the aggregator certificate may call `SetOperator` on a verifier. [2](#0-1) 

A compromised aggregator is an explicit out-of-scope privileged assumption.
