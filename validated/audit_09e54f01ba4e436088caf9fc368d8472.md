### Title
Deposit/withdraw permanently DoS'd if any registered fee receiver reverts on transfer - (File: `wombat/WombatStaking.sol`)

### Summary
`WombatStaking._sendRewards` iterates over the full `feeInfos` array and pays out every active fee (via `safeTransfer` or `IBaseRewardPool.queueNewRewards`) inside the same transaction that stakes/unstakes LP and forwards WOM rewards to the pool's `BaseRewardPool`. This mirrors the reported `FeeDistributor` bug class: a single unpayable fee receiver in a push-based, all-in-one-transaction payout loop can block the whole operation permanently.

### Finding Description
`_sendRewards` is called from `_toMasterWomAndSendReward`, which itself is invoked as part of ordinary user-triggered flows (`deposit`/`withdraw` on `WombatStaking`, reached from `WombatPoolHelper.deposit`/`withdraw`) [1](#0-0) . Inside `_sendRewards`, for every configured fee entry the contract either does `IBaseRewardPool(feeInfo.to).queueNewRewards(...)` or `IERC20(rewardToken).safeTransfer(feeInfo.to, feeTosend)`, with no per-fee try/catch — a revert in any single iteration reverts the entire loop and therefore the entire user transaction: [2](#0-1) 

Because this function is invoked unconditionally on every deposit/withdraw that produces WOM/bonus rewards, if any one `feeInfo.to` becomes unable to accept the token (e.g., a token with a blacklist/pause mechanism such as USDC-style compliance tokens blacklisting that specific receiver, or the receiver contract running out of gas/hitting a require in its own logic), **every subsequent deposit and withdraw for that pool reverts**. Since `WombatPoolHelper.withdraw` and `deposit` route the LP-token-level stake/unstake through this exact same code path [3](#0-2) , users cannot withdraw their principal LP tokens either, not just miss out on rewards — the entire pool becomes stuck.

This is the same root cause pattern as the referenced `FeeDistributor` report: a payout function that must satisfy multiple receivers atomically, where one broken/blacklisted receiver blocks funds for everyone else, with no isolation and no admin/user way to bypass or reroute the failing leg within the same transaction (the only remedy, `removeFee`, is owner-only and requires the owner to notice and act, but that's a mitigation, not a design guard).

### Impact Explanation
This causes a protocol-wide, indefinite freeze of user principal (staked LP) and unclaimed WOM/bonus yield for the affected pool, not merely of the fee amount itself. Every ordinary user who wants to deposit or withdraw from that Wombat pool is blocked until governance intervenes by calling `removeFee`/`setFee`. Given the composability with fee tokens like `mWom`/`WOM` (which route through `IMWom`/`IConverter`), any external condition that makes one of these calls revert (e.g., converter or `mWom` deposit failing) similarly freezes the whole pool.

### Likelihood Explanation
Reachable by any regular user simply calling `deposit`/`withdraw` (no privileged role needed to trigger the freeze) once a fee-receiver-side condition causes a revert; the failure condition itself (blacklisting, pausing, or the receiver reverting on an edge case such as `totalStaked()==0` in `BaseRewardPool` interactions) is plausible in production given push-based transfers to third-party fee recipients and converters.

### Recommendation
Isolate each fee payout with a `try/catch` (or low-level `call` with success checked but not required to revert the whole transaction) so a single failing fee receiver cannot block the reward/deposit/withdraw pipeline. Failed fee amounts should be tracked and made separately claimable/retryable (an "escrow and pull" pattern), analogous to the migration/pull-based remediation recommended in the original report, rather than being pushed unconditionally within the critical user-facing transaction.

### Proof of Concept
1. Owner (in good faith) configures a fee entry via `setFee`/`addFee` with `isAddress = true` pointing at a treasury address, or `isAddress = false` pointing at a `BaseRewardPool`.
2. That receiver later becomes unable to accept the specific reward token (e.g., a compliance-token blacklist event on the treasury address, or a bug/edge case causing `queueNewRewards`/`safeTransfer` to revert).
3. Any user calls `WombatPoolHelper.deposit` or `withdraw`, which internally calls `WombatStaking._toMasterWomAndSendReward` → `_sendRewards`, which loops through `feeInfos` and hits the reverting transfer [4](#0-3) .
4. The whole transaction reverts, meaning the user cannot deposit, withdraw, or have rewards routed to the `BaseRewardPool` for that pool at all — funds and yield remain stuck until the owner manually removes the broken fee entry.
