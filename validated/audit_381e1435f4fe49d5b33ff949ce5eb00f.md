### Title
Attacker can front-run `queueNewRewards` by locking mWOM immediately before reward provisioning to capture a pro-rata share of unclaimed yield with zero staking duration - (File: `wombat/mWomSV.sol`, `rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol`)

### Summary
`mWOMSVBaseRewarder` (and the analogous `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`) distribute newly queued rewards by dividing `_amountReward` by the *current* `totalStaked()` at the moment `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` is called, with no time-weighting or reward-streaming/vesting mechanism. Because `mWomSV.lock()` is unprivileged and immediately increases the locker's `balanceOf`/`totalStaked` via `MasterMagpie.depositMWomSVFor`, an attacker can lock a minimal amount of mWOM in the block immediately preceding a manager's `queueNewRewards` call and be counted in the pro-rata split, then harvest that share via `MasterMagpie.multiclaim`, receiving yield despite having staked for zero elapsed time.

### Finding Description
`mWOMSVBaseRewarder._provisionReward` (called from `queueNewRewards`) computes: [1](#0-0) 
`rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored += (_amountReward * 10**mWOMSVDecimal) / totalStaked()`, where `totalStaked()` reads `mWOMSV.totalSupply()` live: [2](#0-1) , and `balanceOf(_account)` reads the live `MasterMagpie.stakingInfo` amount: [3](#0-2) .

`mWomSV.lock()` is a public, unprivileged, non-time-gated entry point that immediately credits the caller's stake in `MasterMagpie` via `depositMWomSVFor`: [4](#0-3)  and [5](#0-4) . `MasterMagpie.depositMWomSVFor` is gated only by `_onlyMWomSV`, callable freely from `mWomSV.lock()`: [6](#0-5) , and `stakingInfo` immediately reflects the new `user.amount`: [7](#0-6) .

Because `rewardPerToken` is a single global accumulator with no per-block checkpoint or minimum holding period, any address holding stake at the instant `_provisionReward` executes is entitled to `stake_i / totalStaked * amountReward`, regardless of how long that stake existed. `getReward`/`multiclaim` is reachable by the attacker for their own account without any privileged role — `getReward` only requires `onlyMasterMagpie`, and `MasterMagpie.multiclaim` is a public unprivileged function that internally invokes the rewarder's claim path for `msg.sender`: [8](#0-7) . No existing modifier (`whenNotPaused`, `nonReentrant`, `onlyManager` on `queueNewRewards`) prevents an attacker from locking in the block prior to the reward-provisioning transaction and then claiming.

### Impact Explanation
This causes a measurable transfer of unclaimed yield away from long-term stakers to the attacker: the attacker's payout is `attackerStake / totalStakedAtProvisioning * amountReward`, extracted with zero economic contribution (no time held, no risk borne). This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class. The magnitude scales with the size of the attacker's lock relative to total supply, so the loss to other stakers is proportional dilution rather than total-fund drainage.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires only: (1) ability to acquire/hold mWOM (bought on market — no special token needed since flash-loanable minting of mWOM is not evidenced here), (2) visibility of the manager's `queueNewRewards` transaction in the mempool to front-run it, and (3) willingness to have that principal committed to a `startUnlock`+`coolDownInSecs` cycle before it can be withdrawn (the reward claim itself, however, is available immediately after locking, independent of unlock). This is repeatable every time rewards are queued and needs no privileged role, matching the unprivileged-attacker threat model. The main mitigating factor is that gains are proportional to stake size, so a "small mWOM balance" precondition (as stated in the question) yields a correspondingly small, but nonzero, illegitimate payout — which the PoC explicitly targets ("assert attacker's payout is nonzero").

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighting or a checkpoint/vesting mechanism so newly queued rewards are streamed over time (e.g., a `periodFinish`/`rewardRate` model as in Synthetix `StakingRewards`) rather than instantly credited to whatever `totalStaked()` happens to be at the call block. Alternatively, snapshot eligible stakers prior to reward injection, or require a minimum staking duration before a lock position becomes reward-eligible.

### Proof of Concept
Hardhat test outline:
1. Deploy `mWomSV`, `MasterMagpie`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder` with a reward token, and register a `rewardManager`.
2. Have a legitimate staker (`victim`) lock a large amount of mWOM well in advance (multiple blocks earlier) so they have an established `rewardPerTokenPaid` checkpoint.
3. Use `hardhat_setAutomine(false)` / manual block mining to place two transactions in the same block:
   - `attacker` calls `mWomSV.lock(1)` (or a small nominal amount).
   - `rewardManager` calls `mWOMSVBaseRewarder.queueNewRewards(amount, rewardToken)`.
4. Mine the block.
5. `attacker` calls `MasterMagpie.multiclaim([mWomSVAddress])`.
6. Assert `rewardToken.balanceOf(attacker) > 0` even though `block.timestamp` at claim equals `block.timestamp` at lock (zero elapsed staking duration), and assert the amount equals `attackerStake / totalStaked * amountReward` per `_provisionReward` math.
7. Compare against expected reward if reward were instead distributed only to `victim` (time-weighted) to quantify the diverted yield.
