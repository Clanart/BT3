### Title
`ReferralStorage.claimReward()` lacks a solvency check, letting the first claimant drain MGP and permanently freeze other referrers' credited `rewardAmount` - ([File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol])

### Finding Description
`trigger(address _referee, uint256 _amount)` is called by `MasterMagpie` on every referee harvest and simply increments accounting balances in storage without any transfer of MGP into the contract at that moment: [1](#0-0) 

`claimReward()` only guards against a zero personal balance, never against the contract's actual MGP holdings: [2](#0-1) 

Because `rewardAmount` credited via `trigger()` is pure internal accounting (a `mapping`/struct field) and is never reconciled against `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))`, there is no enforced 1:1 backing invariant between the sum of all `userInfos[*].rewardAmount` and the MGP tokens actually custodied by `ReferralStorage`. If multiple referrers' `rewardAmount` are credited in a block (or across blocks) and the contract's MGP balance is insufficient to cover all of them simultaneously, whichever `claimReward()` transaction executes first (attacker included) succeeds via `MGP.safeTransfer`, while later callers' `safeTransfer` calls revert due to `ERC20: transfer amount exceeds balance`. Since `userInfo.rewardAmount` is only zeroed out on a successful transfer, the reverted claimant's credited balance remains permanently outstanding but unbacked — there is no mechanism in the shown contract to top up the shortfall or to make the loser whole. `nonReentrant`/`whenNotPaused` modifiers exist on the contract inheritance but `claimReward()` itself uses neither, so they provide no mitigation here; they don't address the underfunding scenario at all.

An unprivileged attacker with an active referral (any user who registered/used a code) can simply ensure they call `claimReward()` as early as possible (standard mempool front-running/priority-fee bidding, no special privileges needed) whenever they know or suspect the contract is thinly funded relative to total outstanding `rewardAmount`, guaranteeing they get paid ahead of other legitimate referrers whose transfers will then revert and whose credited yield becomes stuck until/unless the contract is refunded by an external party (owner or MasterMagpie), which is outside the attacker's control and not guaranteed to happen within any bounded time.

### Impact Explanation
This is a permanent freezing of unclaimed yield for other referrers: their `rewardAmount` remains recorded on-chain but `claimReward()` will keep reverting for them as long as the contract's MGP balance is insufficient, with no automatic recovery path visible in the contract. This matches the Immunefi impact class "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield."

### Likelihood Explanation
Feasibility depends entirely on whether `MasterMagpie`'s harvest flow always transfers enough MGP into `ReferralStorage` to exactly back every `trigger()` credit before/atomically with the credit. I was unable to fully verify this funding path in `MasterMagpie.sol` and `VLMGP.sol` within the available tool budget — the grep only confirmed that both files reference `ReferralStorage`/`trigger(` but I could not confirm the exact funding sequence (whether MGP is pushed into `ReferralStorage` synchronously per-trigger, batched, or requires a separate owner-driven top-up). If MGP funding is always synchronous and sufficient per `trigger()` call, this scenario cannot occur under normal operation and would require some other combination of underfunding (e.g., rounding, tier changes, or an owner action) — which would fall under intended/administrative behavior rather than an attacker-exploitable path. Given this unresolved dependency, the requested Foundry PoC (funding the contract with insufficient MGP, crediting two referrers via `trigger()`, and showing the second `claimReward()` permanently reverts) is straightforward to construct and would demonstrate the code-level absence of a solvency check, but whether this state is *reachable* purely through unprivileged, non-misconfiguration attacker action depends on the unverified `MasterMagpie` funding logic.

### Recommendation
Not applicable pending confirmation of the funding invariant — recommend verifying (in a full session with complete file access) whether `MasterMagpie` guarantees `MGP.balanceOf(ReferralStorage) >= sum(rewardAmount)` at all times; if not guaranteed, add an explicit balance check in `claimReward()` (e.g., `if (MGP.balanceOf(address(this)) < rewardAmount) revert InsufficientRewardBalance();`) or fund `ReferralStorage` atomically within `trigger()`.
