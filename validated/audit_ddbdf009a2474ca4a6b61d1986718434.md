### Title
Reward sniping via front-run deposit / back-run withdraw around `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` in `BaseRewardPool` / `BaseRewardPoolV2` - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol], [File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol], [File: rewards/MasterMagpie.sol])

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` distribute newly injected reward tokens by immediately stamping the entire `_amountReward` into `rewardPerTokenStored` proportional to the *current, instantaneous* `totalStaked()` [1](#0-0) , exactly mirroring the root cause in the Teller report: a value that should reflect steady-state participation is instead computed from a snapshot that can be manipulated by depositing right before, and withdrawing right after, the reward-affecting event. Since `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` are permissionless, un-delayed, same-block operations [2](#0-1) , an ordinary wallet can front-run a pending `queueNewRewards` (or permissionless `donateRewards`) call with a large deposit, capture a share of the freshly queued reward proportional to its inflated stake, and withdraw immediately afterward, extracting yield that should have accrued to genuine long-term stakers.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` (called from `queueNewRewards`, restricted to reward `managers`, and from `donateRewards`, which is unrestricted/permissionless) computes:
```
rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored += (_amountReward * 10**stakingTokenDecimals) / totalStaked();
``` [3](#0-2) 

`totalStaked()` reads the live balance of the staking token held by `MasterMagpie` (or, for `BribeRewardPool`, the live `totalSupply` of vote-weight) at the exact moment the reward is provisioned [4](#0-3) . There is no time-weighting, no snapshotting prior to the transaction, and no minimum holding period. A depositor's share of `rewardPerToken` is fixed the instant they deposit (`userRewardPerTokenPaid` is set to the current `rewardPerToken` on stake) and is realized any time after via `_earned`/`getReward` [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) .

Because `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` (and their `_harvestBaseRewarder` hooks into `BaseRewardPool.stakeFor/withdrawFor` via `updateReward`) execute atomically with no delay or lockup [7](#0-6) , an attacker can:
1. Observe a pending transaction that will call `queueNewRewards` (bribe harvest via `WombatBribeManager.castVotes`/`harvestSinglePool`, which forwards rewards and calls `queueNewRewards` on the pool's rewarder [8](#0-7) ) or directly call the unrestricted `donateRewards` themselves [9](#0-8) .
2. Front-run it with a large `deposit`/`depositFor` into `MasterMagpie` for that staking token, inflating `totalStaked()` right before `_provisionReward` runs.
3. Let the reward be minted into `rewardPerTokenStored` diluted by the attacker's own inflated stake, but the attacker still receives a share of the reward proportional to their (large, momentary) stake.
4. Immediately `withdraw` in a subsequent transaction (or with `donateRewards`, in the very next call), having captured reward yield with effectively zero holding-period risk, while diluting the entitlement that pre-existing long-term stakers should have received from that reward drop.

This is the same class of bug as the Teller `LenderCommitmentGroup_Smart::getPoolUtilizationRatio()` issue: an accounting ratio (`rewardPerTokenStored` here vs. utilization ratio there) is derived from an instantaneous, unprotected on-chain state variable (`totalStaked()` vs. `getPoolTotalEstimatedValue()`), which lets any wallet sandwich a beneficial event (a reward injection here vs. a favorable interest rate there) using ordinary deposit/withdraw calls with no cooldown.

### Impact Explanation
Every time a reward is queued (bribe harvests, `donateRewards`, or any manager pushing yield into a `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`), an attacker can extract a disproportionate share of that reward for themselves while diluting genuine long-term stakers' entitlement. This is a theft/permanent-loss-of-unclaimed-yield analog: legitimate stakers permanently lose the portion of reward that is redirected to the attacker's momentary inflated stake, and this can be repeated on every reward injection cycle (bribe harvests occur regularly via `castVotes`).

### Likelihood Explanation
`donateRewards` is fully permissionless and can be called by anyone at any time once the reward token is registered [9](#0-8) , so the attacker can even self-trigger the exploit deterministically (deposit, call `donateRewards`, withdraw) without needing to front-run anyone. For manager-only `queueNewRewards` paths (bribe harvest via `WombatBribeManager.castVotes`, which is also a public, callable-by-anyone function [10](#0-9) ), the attacker can trigger the harvest itself in the same block sequence, guaranteeing the sandwich. `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` have no delay, cooldown, or minimum staking duration [2](#0-1) , making this trivially and repeatably exploitable by any unprivileged wallet with capital to flash-inflate stake for one block.

### Recommendation
Introduce a withdrawal delay or a minimum staking duration before a user's stake becomes eligible for newly queued rewards (matching the Teller team's fix of adding a delay on `burnSharesToWithdrawEarnings`), or checkpoint `totalStaked()`/eligible balances prior to the block in which `_provisionReward` executes (e.g., via a snapshot mechanism) so that same-block deposit-then-reward-then-withdraw sequences cannot dilute/capture rewards. Alternatively, restrict `donateRewards` and gate `queueNewRewards` triggers so they cannot be atomically composed with deposit/withdraw calls from the same address in the same transaction or block.

### Proof of Concept
Conceptual sequence (mirroring the Teller POC pattern) against `BaseRewardPoolV2`/`MasterMagpie`:
1. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.deposit(stakingToken, largeAmount)`, which triggers `BaseRewardPool(V2).stakeFor` and inflates `totalStaked()` [11](#0-10) .
2. In the same or immediately following transaction, attacker calls `donateRewards(rewardAmount, rewardToken)` on the pool (or triggers/front-runs `WombatBribeManager.castVotes` → `queueNewRewards`), causing `rewardPerTokenStored` to increase based on the now-inflated `totalStaked()` [3](#0-2) .
3. Attacker immediately calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw(stakingToken, largeAmount)`, which calls `_harvestBaseRewarder`/`getReward` before decrementing stake, crediting the attacker their share of `rewardPerToken` accrued from step 2 [12](#0-11) , [6](#0-5) .
4. Attacker has captured yield with no meaningful holding period/risk, at the expense of pre-existing stakers whose share of the reward was diluted by the attacker's temporary inflated stake.
