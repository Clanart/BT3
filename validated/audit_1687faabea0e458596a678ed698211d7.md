### Title
No per-user balance-based cap on `claimReward` allows accrued referral rewards to be first-come-first-served, freezing unclaimed yield for other referrers when MGP balance is insufficient - (File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol)

### Summary
`ReferralStorage.trigger()` credits `rewardAmount` to referrers/referees purely as an internal accounting entry, with no check against the contract's actual MGP token balance [1](#0-0) . `claimReward()` blindly transfers a user's full `rewardAmount` via `MGP.safeTransfer`, so whichever eligible referrer submits their claim first drains the admin-funded balance, while other referrers with equally-valid, earlier-accrued `rewardAmount` values are left unable to claim until admin tops up the contract again [2](#0-1) .

### Finding Description
- `MasterMagpie._harvestMGP`/claim flow invokes `IReferralStorage(referral).trigger(_user, totalReward)` whenever a user harvests, and any unprivileged user can trigger this arbitrarily often by calling `harvest()` for their own stake [3](#0-2) .
- `trigger()` increments `refererInfo.rewardAmount` and `refereeInfo.rewardAmount` in storage only; it performs no transfer and does not verify that `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))` covers the sum of all outstanding `rewardAmount` entries across all referrers [1](#0-0) .
- `claimReward()` reads `userInfo.rewardAmount` and calls `MGP.safeTransfer(msg.sender, userInfo.rewardAmount)` with no check comparing the requested amount to the contract's current balance before or bounding total claims by supply (unlike the `mgpleft`-style balance-aware cap pattern used in other contracts in this repo, e.g. `wombat/ArbWomUp3.sol`) [2](#0-1) .
- Because MGP is funded into `ReferralStorage` periodically and in limited amounts by admin, the sum of all users' `rewardAmount` can legitimately exceed the actual token balance at any point in time. Any referrer (attacker or not) who calls `claimReward()` first receives their full amount via `safeTransfer`, while a referrer whose reward accrued earlier — but who claims later — has their `safeTransfer` revert due to insufficient balance, leaving their `rewardAmount` unpaid until the admin sends more MGP.

### Impact Explanation
This causes freezing (and potential effective loss, since there's no on-chain guarantee admin will ever fully refund) of unclaimed yield for legitimate referrers who accrued rewards earlier but did not race to claim first. This matches the "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" impact class. The magnitude is bounded by however much MGP the admin funds relative to total outstanding referral rewards at any snapshot.

### Likelihood Explanation
No special privileges are required — any two or more accounts accruing `rewardAmount` via normal `harvest()` activity, combined with a contract balance that is less than the sum of all `rewardAmount` entries (which the admin controls only by periodic top-ups, not by protocol-enforced invariant), is enough to trigger this. Since Ethereum transaction ordering is not guaranteed FIFO by call time, any user (attacker) can pay higher gas / front-run to be included first and drain the limited balance, which is both feasible and repeatable every funding cycle where the balance is insufficient relative to outstanding claims.

### Recommendation
Enforce a global invariant in `ReferralStorage`, e.g., track `totalOutstandingRewards` incremented in `trigger()` and decremented in `claimReward()`, and have `trigger()` (or a separate accrual-safety mechanism) revert/cap accrual, or have `claimReward()` pro-rate/queue payouts, so that `totalOutstandingRewards` never exceeds `MGP.balanceOf(address(this))`, or alternatively adopt the same balance-aware cap pattern (`mgpleft`) used in `wombat/ArbWomUp3.sol` so that no single claimant can capture more than a fair, balance-proportional share and any shortfall is spread rather than allocated purely by claim order.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `ReferralStorage`, `MasterMagpie` (or a mock that calls `trigger` directly, since only `masterMagpie` may call it), and an MGP ERC20 mock.
2. Register a code for referrer `R`, have referee `A` and referee `B` both use `R`'s code via `useCode()`.
3. Fund `ReferralStorage` with a limited amount of MGP (e.g., 100 MGP) via a direct `MGP.transfer`.
4. As `masterMagpie`, call `trigger(A, amountA)` and `trigger(B, amountB)` such that `R.rewardAmount` after both calls exceeds the 100 MGP funded (e.g., 60 + 60 = 120 total credited to `R`, or split across two separate referrers each expecting 60 with only 100 available).
5. Have an "attacker" referrer call `claimReward()` first, successfully draining up to the available balance via `MGP.safeTransfer` at `rewards/ReferralStorage.sol:164`.
6. Assert that the second legitimate referrer's `claimReward()` reverts (`InsufficientRewardBalance`/ERC20 transfer revert) despite `userInfo.rewardAmount` being nonzero and legitimately accrued, demonstrating funds frozen with no recourse until admin re-funds the contract.

        refererInfo.rewardAmount += refererAmount;
        refereeInfo.rewardAmount += refereeAmount;

    }
