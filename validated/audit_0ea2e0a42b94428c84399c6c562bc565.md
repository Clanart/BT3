### Title
Instantaneous, un-timeweighted `factor`/`totalBoostFactor` calculation in `ReferralStorage.updateTotalFactor` allows flash-locking MGP to steal inflated referral yield via `trigger()` - (File: rewards/ReferralStorage.sol)

### Summary
`ReferralStorage.updateTotalFactor` recomputes a referrer's boost `factor` as `DSMath.sqrt(getUserTotalLocked(_account))` purely from the *current* vlMGP balance, with no time-weighting, minimum holding period, or snapshotting. Because `VLMGP.lock()`/`_lock()` and `VLMGP.startUnlock()` both call `updateTotalFactor` synchronously and instantly, an attacker can spike their locked amount immediately before a referee's `trigger()` call and unwind it immediately after, capturing a boosted share of `refererAmount` that does not reflect any sustained stake.

### Finding Description
`_lock()` transfers MGP and immediately calls `updateTotalFactor(_for)` [1](#0-0) , which sets `userInfo.factor = DSMath.sqrt(vlMGPLockedAmount)` and updates `totalBoostFactor` based solely on the instantaneous locked balance, with no delay or time-weighting [2](#0-1) .

When a referee's MGP reward claim flows through `MasterMagpie` into `ReferralStorage.trigger()`, the referer's boosted percentage is computed live via `_calBoosted(_referer)`, which is `BoostPoint * userInfo.factor / totalBoostFactor` [3](#0-2) , and directly used to size `refererAmount` minted into `refererInfo.rewardAmount` in the same call [4](#0-3) .

Because the factor is recalculated on every `lock`/`_lock` and `startUnlock` call (not time-averaged or checkpointed against duration held), an attacker who: (1) registers a code via `registerCode`, (2) has a second address `useCode()` and later claims MGP reward through `MasterMagpie` (invoking `trigger`), and (3) times a large `lock()` immediately before that claim and a `startUnlock()` immediately after, can transiently dominate `totalBoostFactor` for exactly the block in which `trigger()` executes — capturing an inflated `refererAmount` disproportionate to any real, sustained qualifying stake. `startUnlock` itself calls `updateTotalFactor` again right after reducing the counted locked balance [5](#0-4) , letting the attacker revert their factor immediately, minimizing the exposure window while retaining the already-credited inflated `rewardAmount`.

No mechanism (time-weighted average lock, minimum lock duration, or snapshotting factor prior to the block) prevents this instantaneous manipulation.

### Impact Explanation
This allows theft of unclaimed referral yield: the attacker extracts a disproportionately large `refererAmount` from `trigger()` at the expense of other genuine, sustained-stake referrers whose share of `totalBoostFactor` is diluted only for the single block of the attack, while the attacker's real capital exposure is limited to the value transiently locked (their capital remains committed only through the unlock cooldown, not permanently). This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" Immunefi impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are all attacker-controlled and unprivileged: registering a referral code, having a second address use that code, and controlling/timing when that second address claims its MGP reward (which triggers `trigger()`). The only capital requirement is temporarily owning enough MGP to spike `factor` for one block, which is committed to `VLMGP` cooldown afterward but not permanently lost. This is repeatable each time the attacker's referee (or colluding accounts) claim rewards, making it a systematic yield-siphoning strategy rather than a one-off exploit.

### Recommendation
Compute referral boost factor from a time-weighted average lock (e.g., checkpointed balance over a minimum holding window) rather than the instantaneous `getUserTotalLocked` value, or snapshot `totalBoostFactor`/`userInfo.factor` at the start of the reward-accrual period being claimed in `trigger()`, so a single-block lock spike cannot affect an already-accrued reward distribution.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `VLMGP`, `ReferralStorage`, `MasterMagpie` with attacker's `refererAcct` and `refereeAcct`.
2. `refererAcct` calls `registerCode(code)`; `refereeAcct` calls `useCode(code)`.
3. Have a second, honest referrer (`honestAcct`) lock a modest, sustained MGP amount into `VLMGP` beforehand and let time pass (simulating genuine stake).
4. In one block: `refererAcct` calls `VLMGP.lock(largeAmount)` (spiking `factor`/`totalBoostFactor`), then trigger `refereeAcct`'s MGP reward claim through `MasterMagpie` (invoking `ReferralStorage.trigger`), then `refererAcct` immediately calls `VLMGP.startUnlock(largeAmount)`.
5. Assert `userInfos[refererAcct].rewardAmount` after `trigger()` significantly exceeds what `honestAcct`'s proportional, time-weighted stake would justify, demonstrating the instantaneous flash-lock captured an outsized share of `refererAmount`.
