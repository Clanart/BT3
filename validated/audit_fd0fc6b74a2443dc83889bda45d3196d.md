### Title
Reward-skimming via front-run vote/harvest/unvote sandwich in `WombatBribeManager.harvestSinglePool` and `BribeRewardPool` reward accounting - ([File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol], [File: rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol], [File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol])

### Summary
An unprivileged holder of vlMGP can vote a large delta into a pool, immediately trigger `harvestSinglePool` (or ride on anyone else's harvest/`castVotes` in the same block), claim a full pro-rata share of the freshly harvested bribe, and then unvote/withdraw — all within one block. Because reward accrual is based purely on instantaneous `totalStaked()`/`balanceOf()` at the moment a reward is queued rather than time-weighted exposure, the attacker captures yield that should have accrued to voters who held their stake for the entire bribe-earning period.

### Finding Description
`vote()` lets any vlMGP holder increase `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp` and calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` [1](#0-0) . `harvestSinglePool` casts a zero-delta vote to `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, whose documented purpose is exactly "harvest the bribes of selected pools" [2](#0-1) .

`stakeFor` in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` updates the caller's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` to the *current* `rewardPerToken` value **before** increasing `totalSupply`/`_balances`, and only afterwards increments the attacker's staked balance [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) .

When new bribe rewards are queued (via `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`), the entire reward amount is distributed pro-rata to `totalStaked()` **at that instant**: `rewardPerTokenStored += (_amountReward * 1e_dec) / totalStaked()` [5](#0-4) . Since the attacker's freshly staked `X` is already included in `totalStaked()` at the moment the harvested bribe is queued, the attacker earns `X / totalStaked() * newBribe` despite having contributed zero blocks of actual voting exposure that earned that bribe from Wombat. `_earned()` computes rewards purely from the delta of `rewardPerTokenStored` since the user's last checkpoint, with no time-weighting or vesting [6](#0-5) .

The attacker can then call `claimBribe` → `_claimBribeFor` → `IBribeRewardPool.getReward` to collect the reward [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) , and finally exit via `vote()` with a negative delta (or `unvote()`), calling `withdrawFor` to remove the stake [9](#0-8) [10](#0-9) .

No modifier, cooldown, vesting, or minimum-holding-period check exists to prevent a same-block/same-transaction vote→harvest→claim→unvote sequence. `getReward` is gated only by `onlyMasterMagpie` (i.e., callable by the manager contract on behalf of any user, not restricted by holding duration) [11](#0-10) .

### Impact Explanation
This is theft of unclaimed yield: bribes harvested from Wombat that economically belong to voters who held their position throughout the epoch are diluted and partially redirected to a last-second voter with zero real exposure. This maps to the Immunefi "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class. The magnitude scales with the size of the delta the attacker can vote (bounded by their vlMGP voting power) relative to the pool's total votes and the freshly harvested bribe amount — larger relative stakes and infrequent harvests amplify the theft.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are minimal: the attacker only needs a vlMGP lock (obtainable permissionlessly by locking MGP) and knowledge of an impending `harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes` call (harvesting is a public, permissionless, gas-incentivized action anyone can trigger, and is also visible in the mempool/predictable by cadence). No special privileges are required, and the whole sequence (`vote` → `harvestSinglePool` → `claimBribe` → `vote`/`unvote`) can be executed atomically in one transaction/block, making it fully repeatable each time a bribe harvest occurs. The only capital needed is enough vlMGP to secure a meaningful vote share relative to existing votes on the target pool.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighting or a minimum holding/warm-up period before newly staked votes become eligible for pending/queued rewards — e.g., snapshot eligible voters prior to processing a harvest (checkpoint `rewardPerToken` for a pool immediately before allowing new `stakeFor` calls to affect that pool's next reward distribution), or require that `stakeFor` votes only become reward-eligible starting from the *next* epoch/harvest rather than the currently-in-flight one. Alternatively, decouple `harvestSinglePool`'s zero-vote harvest trigger from allowing same-transaction stake changes by adding a cooldown between `vote` and `harvestSinglePool`/`claimBribe` for the same account/pool.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `BribeRewardPool` (for a test pool), mock `WombatStaking`, mock vlMGP with lockable balances, and a mock bribe reward token.
2. Simulate a "long-term voter" (Alice) who calls `vote(pool, +Y)` several blocks/epochs before a harvest, establishing genuine exposure.
3. In the harvest block: attacker (Bob) calls `vote(pool, +X)` (front-run), then immediately calls `harvestSinglePool([pool])`, which triggers the mock `wombatStaking.vote` to call `queueNewRewards(bribeAmount, token)` on the pool's `BribeRewardPool`.
4. Bob calls `claimBribe([pool])` and records `bobReward`.
5. Bob calls `vote(pool, -X)` (or `unvote(pool)`) to exit in the same block.
6. Assert `bobReward > 0` and `bobReward ≈ X/(X+Y) * bribeAmount`, i.e., comparable in magnitude to Alice's eventual `claimBribe` payout (`aliceReward ≈ Y/(X+Y) * bribeAmount`), despite Bob's stake duration being effectively zero blocks versus Alice's multi-block exposure — demonstrating that reward accrual is not time-weighted and permits single-block yield extraction.

    /// @notice this  function has a lesser importance than casting votes, hence no rewards will be given to the caller.
    function harvestSinglePool(address[] calldata _lps) public {
        uint256 length = _lps.length;
        int256[] memory votes = new int256[](length);
        address[] memory rewarders = new address[](length);
        for (uint256 i; i < length; i++) {
            address lp = _lps[i];
            Pool storage pool = poolInfos[lp];
            rewarders[i] = pool.rewarder;
            votes[i] = 0;
        }
        wombatStaking.vote(_lps, votes, rewarders, address(0));
    }
