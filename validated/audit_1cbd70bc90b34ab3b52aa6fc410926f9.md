### Title
Instant, non-time-weighted reward crediting in `BaseRewardPool` allows flash-deposit theft of unclaimed yield - (File: `rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol`)

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool` (and its variants `BaseRewardPoolV2`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, `vlMGPBaseRewarder`) credit newly queued rewards instantly and entirely to whatever `totalStaked()` balance exists at the moment `queueNewRewards()`/`_provisionReward()` is called, with no linear vesting or minimum holding period. Because `WombatPoolHelper.harvest()` is a permissionless external function that triggers this reward crediting, an ordinary user can deposit a large stake into `MasterMagpie` immediately before calling `harvest()`, capture a pro-rata share of yield that accrued while they held no position, and withdraw right after — diluting the rewards legitimately earned by long-term stakers. This is the same root cause described in the Frax report: reward accounting is not time-weighted to the staking period during which the yield was actually generated, letting opportunistic deposits siphon yield belonging to other users.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward()` updates `rewardPerTokenStored` in one shot, using the current `totalStaked()`: [1](#0-0) 

`earned()` then attributes reward strictly by current `balanceOf(_account)` multiplied by the delta in `rewardPerTokenStored` since the user's last checkpoint, with no consideration of how long that balance existed: [2](#0-1) 

The same instant, undiluted-by-time accounting exists in `BaseRewardPoolV2` and the Wombat-specific rewarders: [3](#0-2) 

Reward queuing for the Wombat LP pools is triggered by `harvest()`, which is externally callable by anyone with no access control: [4](#0-3) 

Deposits into `MasterMagpie` immediately increase `user.amount`/`user.available` and therefore `totalStaked()` for the reward pool as read by `BaseRewardPool.totalStaked()`: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

Because `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward` divides the newly harvested reward by `totalStaked()` at the instant it is called (not by a time-weighted accumulation over the period the reward was generated), a user who deposits immediately before triggering (or witnessing) a harvest, and withdraws immediately after, receives a share of rewards proportional to their balance at that single snapshot — despite having staked for effectively zero duration. Existing long-term stakers are diluted by exactly that amount, i.e., their yield is being redirected to the flash depositor. Withdrawal is available immediately since `MasterMagpie._withdraw`/`_harvestAndUnstake` impose no lock-up beyond a simple balance check: [7](#0-6) 

This mirrors the Frax root cause: reward release is not delayed/vested relative to when a staker's position actually existed, so value generated by long-term holders' capital can be captured by someone entering and exiting the pool around the reward-crediting event.

### Impact Explanation
This results in direct theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate long-term stakers in a `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`/Wombat-rewarder pool. An attacker with sufficient capital (even flash-loaned, since the staking token itself is an LP/receipt token that must be transferred in — not necessarily requiring a flash loan if the attacker already holds capital) can repeatedly extract yield with no staking-duration risk, at the expense of real depositors. This satisfies "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield."

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is moderate: it requires the attacker to (a) hold or acquire the underlying staking/receipt token, (b) deposit into `MasterMagpie` for the target pool, and (c) call the permissionless `WombatPoolHelper.harvest()` (or observe/front-run another party's harvest/queueNewRewards trigger), then withdraw. No privileged role is needed — `harvest()` has no access control and `deposit`/`withdraw` are ordinary user-facing functions.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted reward accrual so that a staker's share of newly queued rewards is proportional to their balance integrated over the period the reward was generated, not their balance at the single moment `queueNewRewards` executes. Options include:
- Adopting a linear-release/streaming model similar to `xERC4626`'s `rewardsCycleEnd`, so freshly queued rewards vest over a cycle rather than being instantly claimable.
- Requiring a minimum staking duration before a user becomes eligible for rewards queued during their holding period (checkpoint-based accrual keyed to entry time).
- Restricting/rate-limiting `harvest()` calls or requiring a cooldown between deposit and reward eligibility.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool P has stakers with `totalStaked() = S` and accumulated pending WOM yield of `R` sitting unharvested in the underlying Wombat position.
2. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`depositFor` (via `WombatPoolHelper._stake`) with amount `D`, so `totalStaked()` becomes `S + D`. [6](#0-5) 
3. Attacker immediately calls the permissionless `WombatPoolHelper.harvest()`, which flows to `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`, crediting `rewardPerTokenStored += R * 10**decimals / (S + D)`. [4](#0-3) [1](#0-0) 
4. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw`, which internally calls `_harvestBaseRewarder` to claim `earned()`, awarding the attacker `D/(S+D) * R` in reward tokens for zero staking duration. [7](#0-6) [2](#0-1) 
5. Legitimate long-term stakers receive only `S/(S+D) * R` instead of the full `R`, effectively having their yield diluted/stolen by the attacker's flash deposit-withdraw sequence.

    function _harvestAndUnstake(address _stakingToken, address _account, uint256 _amount, bool _isVlMgp) internal {
        updatePool(_stakingToken);

        UserInfo storage user = userInfo[_stakingToken][_account];

        if (!_isVlMgp && user.available < _amount)
            revert WithdrawAmountExceedsStaked();
        else if(user.amount < _amount && _isVlMgp)
            revert UnlockAmountExceedsLocked();
        
        _harvestMGP(_stakingToken, _account);
        _harvestBaseRewarder(_stakingToken, _account);

            user.available = user.available - _amount;
