### Title
Reward-donation sandwich lets a staker front-run `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` to steal yield from genuine stakers - (File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol)

### Summary
The reported bug lets a Flip staker observe an imminent, value-changing on-chain event (`completeGame`) and choose, in the same block, whether to finalize a pending stake/unstake request to capture the upside or dodge the downside. The same "observe pending value-changing tx and time your own tx around it" bug class is reachable in this codebase's reward-distribution logic: `BaseRewardPool._provisionReward` (and its `BaseRewardPoolV2`/`mWOMSVBaseRewarder`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder` counterparts) immediately folds any incoming reward amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` at call time, with no vesting, streaming, or lockup. A user who sees a pending `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` transaction (called by `WombatStaking` after `harvest()`, or by any manager/`donateRewards` caller) can deposit into `MasterMagpie` immediately before it lands, instantly acquire a proportional share of `rewardPerTokenStored`, then withdraw right after, capturing yield he never had time-weighted exposure to and diluting the rewards long-term stakers should have received.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` in `rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol` computes: [1](#0-0) 

`rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored` is bumped by `_amountReward * 10**decimals / totalStaked()` at the instant the reward is provisioned, with `totalStaked()` read live. There is no minimum holding period, no linear/streaming reward release, and deposits into `MasterMagpie` (and correspondingly into the rewarder via `_stakingToken`) are permitted with no delay right up until the block that calls `queueNewRewards`. This mirrors the pattern in the external report where `finalizeStake`/`finalizeUnstake` let a user react to already-known information (the flip result) instead of being locked in before the outcome is determined — here, a user can react to a pending, already-broadcast reward-injection transaction (e.g. `WombatStaking._toMasterWomAndSendReward` → `queueNewRewards`, seen in [2](#0-1) , or any `donateRewards(...)` call) by depositing immediately before it and withdrawing immediately after.

The identical `_provisionReward` logic (no time-weighting of new deposits vs. existing stakers) recurs across `rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol` and `rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol`: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

### Impact Explanation
Any unprivileged wallet monitoring the mempool can sandwich a reward-funding transaction: deposit right before `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` executes, then withdraw in the very next transaction. Because `rewardPerTokenStored` is updated using an instantaneous `totalStaked()` snapshot with no streaming/vesting, the attacker captures a share of freshly injected rewards proportional to their (temporary) stake, without bearing any real economic exposure or time commitment to the pool. This directly and permanently reduces (steals) the yield that would otherwise accrue to genuine long-term stakers, matching the "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" impact bar.

### Likelihood Explanation
`queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` calls are triggered by routine, externally visible operations (harvest cycles in `WombatStaking`, bribe distribution, manual reward donations), making the timing of reward injections predictable/observable in the mempool. Since deposits and withdrawals in `MasterMagpie`/pool helpers have no cooldown preventing an instant deposit-then-withdraw around such a transaction, the attack is executable by any ordinary wallet with capital and requires no privileged access, satisfying medium-to-high likelihood.

### Recommendation
- Stream newly queued rewards over time (e.g., a `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` model as in Synthetix-style reward pools) instead of instantly folding them into `rewardPerTokenStored`, so a flash deposit cannot capture a lump-sum share.
- Alternatively, require a minimum staking duration before a user's balance is eligible for a given reward-per-token checkpoint, or snapshot `totalStaked()`/user balances prior to the block in which rewards are provisioned.
- Consider applying a deposit/withdraw cooldown or fee for very short holding periods to disincentivize reward sniping.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker monitors the mempool and observes a pending transaction that will call `WombatStaking._toMasterWomAndSendReward` → `IBaseRewardPool(_rewarders[i]).queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, rewardToken)` (see [5](#0-4) ), or a manager/anyone calling `donateRewards` directly.
2. Attacker front-runs it with `MasterMagpie.deposit(...)`/pool-helper deposit for the corresponding `stakingToken`, increasing `totalStaked()` right before the reward call executes.
3. `_provisionReward` executes, computing `rewardPerTokenStored += (_amountReward * 10**decimals) / totalStaked()` using the now-inflated `totalStaked()` that includes the attacker's fresh deposit — the attacker instantly becomes eligible for a proportional share of `_amountReward`.
4. Attacker immediately calls `getReward`/`multiclaim` and withdraws their stake, realizing the reward share with near-zero holding time and no market exposure, at the expense of the pool's genuine stakers.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
