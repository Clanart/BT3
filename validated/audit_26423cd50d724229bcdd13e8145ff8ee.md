### Title
Last-block vote staking before permissionless `castVotes` allows theft of accrued bribe rewards - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol)

### Summary
`castVotes(bool swapForBnb)` is callable by any address at any time with no cooldown or minimum holding period since the caller's last `vote()` call. [1](#0-0)  Because the underlying `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` reward-accounting model injects harvested bribes into `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` at the moment of harvest, and checkpoints a staker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at stake time (before the increment is applied), a user can call `vote()` to add vlMGP weight to a pool immediately before triggering `castVotes()` and receive a pro-rata share of that pool's entire accrued epoch bribe despite having contributed zero vote-weight during the actual accrual period.

### Finding Description
`vote()` updates `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp[msg.sender][lp]` and calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)`. [2](#0-1)  `stakeFor` is guarded by the `updateRewards` modifier, which checkpoints the caller's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at the **current** `rewardPerTokenStored` value using the **old** balance, and only afterwards increases `totalSupply`/`_balances`. [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

`castVotes()` is `public` with no access control, no `nonReentrant`, and no minimum time since the last `vote()`/`castVotes()`; it computes vote deltas and calls `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, which harvests bribes from Wombat and forwards them to each pool's rewarder. [5](#0-4)  When new rewards are queued into a rewarder, `_provisionReward` computes the `rewardPerTokenStored` increment as `_amountReward * 10**decimals / totalStaked()`, using `totalStaked()` **at the time of the call**. [6](#0-5) 

Exploit sequence:
1. Attacker calls `vote()` to add vlMGP weight to pool `lp`, immediately before `castVotes()` is called (by the attacker themselves or anyone else, since it's permissionless). `stakeFor` checkpoints the attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at the pre-harvest `rewardPerTokenStored`, and increases `totalSupply` to include the attacker's new stake.
2. `castVotes()` executes, harvesting the full epoch's accrued bribe for `lp` and pushing it into the rewarder, computing the `rewardPerTokenStored` increment using the now-inflated `totalStaked()` (which includes the attacker's just-added stake).
3. Because the attacker's checkpoint was taken *before* this increment, `earned = balance * (newRewardPerTokenStored - oldRewardPerTokenPaid)` credits the attacker a full share of the increment proportional to their newly added balance — despite having held that vote weight for zero time during the actual accrual window on Wombat.
4. The attacker can then call `unvote()`/`vote()` again to withdraw their weight (claim=true triggers `_getReward`), realizing the stolen share, while long-term voters' realized share of the same epoch's bribe is diluted by the enlarged `totalStaked()` denominator used in step 2.

No mechanism (cooldown, minimum staking duration, or time-weighted average balance) exists in `vote()`, `castVotes()`, `stakeFor`, or `_provisionReward` to prevent this. This breaks the intended reconciliation between `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp[user][lp]` (which reflects only current-block vote weight) and the assumption that `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).balanceOf(user)` represents weight held for the full epoch being harvested.

### Impact Explanation
An attacker can capture a portion of an entire epoch's bribe rewards for a pool by staking vote weight one block before a permissionless `castVotes()` call, and immediately withdrawing after claiming. This directly reallocates real economic value (bribe reward tokens) away from voters who held their position for the whole accrual period to the attacker, constituting direct theft of user funds (bribe yield) — matching Critical: Direct theft of user funds / theft of unclaimed yield.

### Likelihood Explanation
- `castVotes()` has no access control and no cooldown, so any address can trigger the harvest at will. [1](#0-0) 
- The attacker only needs vlMGP voting weight (locked MGP) available to allocate via `vote()`; no flash-loan of vlMGP is required since the exploit works with weight added in the block(s) immediately preceding the cast, not funds borrowed intra-transaction.
- This is repeatable every epoch/cast cycle and scales with the size of accrued bribes and the fraction of `totalStaked()` the attacker can quickly add.
- The precondition (bribe contract registering ≥1 reward token, standard case) is met by design since `BaseRewardPoolV2`/`BribeRewardPool` always track `rewardTokens`.

### Recommendation
Introduce a minimum holding/cooldown period between `vote()` and eligibility for a given `castVotes()` harvest (e.g., checkpoint vote weight only for votes cast before `lastCastTime`, or require votes to be locked for at least one full epoch before they count toward the next harvest distribution). Alternatively, switch the bribe reward distribution to a time-weighted / streaming model (reward rate over a duration, à la Synthetix `StakingRewards`) rather than instantaneous `rewardPerTokenStored` injection based on `totalStaked()` at harvest time, so late stakers cannot retroactively capture rewards accrued before their stake existed.

### Proof of Concept
Hardhat/Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, a `BribeRewardPool` for pool `lp` with one reward token, `vlMGP`, and mock `WombatStaking`/`IWombatVoter` that accrues a known bribe amount `R` over an epoch.
2. Voter A locks MGP and calls `vote()` allocating weight `W_A` to `lp` at epoch start; let time pass (simulate `R` accruing on Wombat's bribe contract).
3. Just before calling `castVotes()`, Attacker locks MGP and calls `vote()` allocating weight `W_atk` to `lp` in the same/adjacent block.
4. Call `castVotes(false)` (attacker or third party) to harvest `R` into the rewarder.
5. Assert: `IBribeRewardPool(rewarder).earned(attacker, rewardToken) > 0` and proportional to `W_atk / (W_A + W_atk) * R`, while `earned(voterA, rewardToken) < R` (diluted below what Voter A would receive absent the attacker's last-block stake).
6. Differential run: repeat step 3-5 without the attacker's late `vote()` call and confirm Voter A's `earned` amount is strictly higher, and `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp[attacker][lp]` / `balanceOf(attacker)` show the attacker captured yield disproportionate to any time-weighted contribution — confirming the invariant violation.
