## Title
Front-running `harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes` with `vote()` allows an attacker to steal a disproportionate share of just-harvested bribe rewards from long-term voters - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol, rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol, rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol)

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager.vote()` is a fully permissionless function that immediately increases a user's stake in the corresponding `BribeRewardPool` via `stakeFor`, with no cooldown or time-weighting. Because `BribeRewardPool` distributes newly harvested bribes pro-rata by the staker's balance *at the moment `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward` runs* (not by time-weighted contribution during the accrual period), an attacker can front-run a pending `harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes` transaction with `vote()` to inflate their share right before the reward-per-token bump, then immediately claim an unearned portion of the harvest via `claimBribeFor`.

### Finding Description
`vote()` calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` unconditionally for any user with unused vlMGP voting power [1](#0-0) . `stakeFor` in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` runs the `updateRewards` modifier (which settles the account's *pre-existing* balance against the *current* `rewardPerTokenStored`) and only afterward increases both the user's balance and `totalSupply` [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .

`harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes` subsequently call `wombatStaking.vote()`, which forwards the externally-accrued Wombat bribe into `IBaseRewardPool(_rewarders[i]).queueNewRewards(...)` [4](#0-3) , invoking `_provisionReward`, which bumps `rewardPerTokenStored` by `amountReward * 10**decimals / totalStaked()` [5](#0-4) . The harvested `amountReward` reflects bribes accrued externally on Wombat for votes locked in during a prior `castVotes` cycle — it does **not** depend on the attacker's new stake. However, the denominator `totalStaked()` and the attacker's own balance used in `_earned()` are both evaluated using state *as of the harvest transaction*, so:

1. Attacker front-runs the pending harvest with `vote([lp],[maxDelta])`, instantly increasing `_balances[attacker]` and `totalSupply` in `BribeRewardPool` at no cost beyond gas (their vlMGP stays locked/unspent, only their votable allowance is consumed).
2. `harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes` executes next, calling `_provisionReward`, which computes the `rewardPerToken` increase using the now-inflated `totalStaked()`, diluting the per-token reward gained by long-term stakers.
3. Attacker calls `claimBribeFor`/`claimBribe`, which invokes `getReward(_for,_for)` [6](#0-5) , paying out `userShare * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)` using the attacker's just-inflated balance against the freshly bumped `rewardPerTokenStored` [7](#0-6)  — capturing a slice of a bribe that accrued entirely before the attacker's vote existed.

No modifier, cooldown, nonReentrant guard, or time-weighted accounting prevents `vote()` from being placed immediately before a harvest in the same block; `unvote` restrictions only apply to inactive pools and are irrelevant here since the attacker need not unstake to realize the gain.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft of unclaimed yield: rewards meant to accrue to voters who held their positions through the bribe-accrual period are partially redirected to a last-block staker. This maps to the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class. The loss is proportional to `attackerDelta / totalStaked_after` fraction of every harvested bribe, extracted from the pool of existing voters, and is repeatable every time a `harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes` transaction is visible in the mempool.

### Likelihood Explanation
The only precondition is holding any nonzero vlMGP (`getUserVotable(attacker) > 0`), which any user can obtain permissionlessly by locking MGP. The attack requires no special capital beyond normal gas/priority fees to front-run, is fully repeatable for every harvest cycle, and does not require reentrancy or flash loans — a simple higher-gas transaction submitted ahead of the harvest suffices.

### Recommendation
Decouple bribe distribution from instantaneous balance snapshots: introduce a time-weighted/checkpointed staking model (e.g., snapshot balances used in `stakeFor` only become "active" for reward accrual after the next epoch), or require `vote()` deltas to be locked for a minimum duration (cooldown) before they count toward `BribeRewardPool` balance used in reward distribution, or perform reward provisioning against balances captured at the start of the harvested period rather than the balance present when `_provisionReward` executes.

### Proof of Concept
Hardhat plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `BribeRewardPool`, and mock `wombatStaking`/`voter` returning a fixed bribe amount for a pool `lp`.
2. Long-term voter `A` locks vlMGP and calls `vote([lp],[100])`, then advance time so an external bribe of `R` tokens is pending for `lp`.
3. Baseline run: call `harvestSinglePool([lp])` directly, then `A.claimBribe([lp])`; record `earnedA_baseline`.
4. Attack run (reset state): attacker `B` locks vlMGP (`getUserVotable(B) >= 100`); in the same block, `B.vote([lp],[100])` executes immediately before `harvestSinglePool([lp])` (simulate via same-block ordering); then both `A.claimBribe([lp])` and `B.claimBribeFor([lp],B)` are called.
5. Assert `earnedA_attack < earnedA_baseline` and `earnedB_attack > 0` despite `B` having zero prior stake/time contributing to the harvested bribe `R`, demonstrating a wealth transfer from `A` to `B` purely from front-running `vote()`.
