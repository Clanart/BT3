### Title
Cached FROST preprocess reused across re-executed `share`/`complete` calls can sign distinct messages with identical nonces, leaking the validator MuSig secret key - (File: coordinator/src/tributary/signing_protocol.rs)

### Summary
Analogous to the highlight.run bug — where a password input's obfuscation was bound to its *initial* `type` attribute and silently lost when the DOM later mutated it to `type="text"` — the coordinator's DKG-confirmation signer derives its FROST nonces deterministically from a seed cached under only the `(b"DkgConfirmer", attempt)` context. The "protected" quantity (a fresh, single-use nonce) is decided once at `preprocess` time and never re-validated when `share`/`complete` are later executed over a potentially different message/preprocess set. The file's own header admits that re-running `sign` over distinct messages or nonce commitments causes nonce reuse and that the guard against this ("check the commitments generated from the decided nonces are in fact its commitments on-chain") is an unimplemented TODO.

### Finding Description
`SigningProtocol::preprocess_internal` encrypts and stores a 32-byte `CachedPreprocess` seed keyed only by `context` (`CachedPreprocesses`, created at lines 86–90; cache write at lines 123–135). Every later call to `share_internal` re-executes `preprocess_internal`, decrypts the same seed, and rebuilds identical nonces via `AlgorithmSignMachine::from_cache` → `seeded_preprocess` (`crypto/frost/src/sign.rs`, lines 121–144, 268–274).

The message signed is `set_keys_message(set, removed, key_pair)` (lines 296–300). Two separate entry points produce shares under the same context:

- `generated_key_pair` (handle.rs:47–60) calls `.share(preprocesses, key_pair)` with the `key_pair` reported by the processor, after unconditionally overwriting `DkgKeyPair::set`.
- `DkgConfirmed` handling (handle.rs:508–560) calls `confirmer.complete(preprocesses, &key_pair, shares)`, which internally calls `share_internal` *again* (signing_protocol.rs:312–326, 150–180), reconstructing `msg` from `DkgKeyPair::get` — i.e., whatever `key_pair` was most recently written, not necessarily the one signed in `share`.

If `generated_key_pair` is invoked more than once for an attempt (each invocation rewrites `DkgKeyPair` and `KeyToDkgAttempt` with no idempotency check), or the stored `key_pair` at `complete` time differs from the one used in `share`, the same FROST nonce pair signs two different messages. Similarly, the header comment (lines 34–55) explicitly states safety depends on the received preprocess set and message being identical across re-executions, and marks the on-chain-commitment-consistency check as TODO (lines 50–54) — meaning a coordinator that re-executes `share_internal` after state divergence (partial DB rebuild, rewritten `ConfirmationNonces`/`DkgKeyPair`) will emit a second share under an already-used nonce.

A Schnorr/FROST signature share has the form `s = d + e·ρ + c·λ·x`. Two shares with the same nonce commitment `(D, E)` and different challenges `c` yield `x` (and hence the validator's root-of-trust MuSig secret `self.key`) by linear algebra — the exact failure the `CachedPreprocess` docs warn about (`crypto/frost/src/sign.rs:83–92, 209–219`) and the FROST spec reiterates (`spec/cryptography/FROST.md:51–62`).

### Impact Explanation
Recovery of a validator's private key — the key used as the root of trust for confirming DKG results on Substrate and for all tributary `Signed` transactions — enables forging `set_keys` confirmations and impersonating the validator in the BFT layer. This is key-share/private-key recovery, the highest-impact class for this code.

### Likelihood Explanation
Triggering requires the coordinator to run `share`-producing paths twice under one `(b"DkgConfirmer", attempt)` context with differing inputs. `share_internal` is already invoked twice per confirmation (`share` then again inside `complete`), so any divergence between the stored `key_pair`/`preprocesses` across those invocations — a second `generated_key_pair` event, a DB write reordering (acknowledged as possible in the comment at handle.rs:527–532), or the missing on-chain-vs-local preprocess check after a partial rebuild — produces nonce reuse without requiring malformed peer input. The authors themselves flag the missing invariant (TODO at signing_protocol.rs:50–54). Reachability is conditional on that divergence, which is why this is not Critical.

### Recommendation
Before emitting a share, verify that the locally derived `preprocess` equals the preprocess previously published on-chain for this context (the TODO at lines 50–54), and refuse to sign if the reconstructed `(preprocesses, msg)` differ from those used in the first `share` call. Persist the signed `(msg_hash, preprocess_set_hash)` alongside `CachedPreprocesses` and hard-fail on mismatch rather than re-executing deterministically. Also make `generated_key_pair` idempotent per attempt (reject a second `DkgKeyPair::set` with a different value).

### Proof of Concept
1. Coordinator publishes confirmation nonces for `attempt = k` via `dkg_confirmation_nonces`; `CachedPreprocesses` now holds seed `S` under `(b"DkgConfirmer", k)`.
2. `generated_key_pair` fires with `key_pair_A`: `share()` derives nonces `(d, e)` from `S` and publishes share `s_A = d + e·ρ + c_A·λ·x` over `msg_A = set_keys_message(set, removed, key_pair_A)`.
3. A second `generated_key_pair` (or `complete()` after `DkgKeyPair` was overwritten) runs `share_internal` again: `from_cache` regenerates the identical `(d, e)` from `S`, and signs `msg_B ≠ msg_A`, publishing `s_B = d + e·ρ + c_B·λ·x`.
4. Any observer of the two tributary `DkgConfirmed`/share publications computes `λ·x = (s_A − s_B)/(c_A − c_B)` and recovers the validator's secret key.
