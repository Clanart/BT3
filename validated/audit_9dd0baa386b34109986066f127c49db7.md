### Title
Flash-loan deposit immediately before `WombatStaking.vote`/`queueNewRewards` lets an attacker capture a disproportionate share of freshly-harvested bribes at the expense of genuine long-term stakers - (File: `wombat/WombatStaking.sol`, `rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol`, `rewards/MasterMagpie.sol`)

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool._provisionReward` distributes newly queued rewards proportionally to `totalStaked()`/`balanceOf()` read live at the moment `queueNewRewards` executes, with no time-weighting or reward-eligibility lock. Because `WombatBribeManager.castVotes()` is a public, unpermissioned function that triggers `WombatStaking.vote()` → `IBaseRewardPool.queueNewRewards()` in the same transaction, an attacker can flash-loan LP, deposit into `MasterMagpie` right before calling `castVotes()`, capture an outsized share of the bribe reward being queued for that pool, then withdraw/claim and repay the loan atomically.

### Finding Description
`BaseRewardPool._provisionReward` computes: [1](#0-0) 
`rewardPerTokenStored` is bumped using `this.totalStaked()` at the exact instant the reward is queued, with no snapshot of who held stake *before* the reward accrued.

`earned()`/`_updateFor` compute a user's owed reward using **live** `balanceOf(_account)` multiplied by the delta between the current `rewardPerTokenStored` and the user's last-paid checkpoint: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

In `MasterMagpie._deposit`, the harvest/checkpoint call (`_harvestBaseRewarder`, which triggers the reward pool's `updateReward` modifier and snapshots `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at the account's balance *before* the deposit) happens **before** `user.amount` is incremented: [4](#0-3) 

This means: if an attacker deposits LP right before a reward is queued, their checkpoint is set on their old (small/zero) balance, but once the reward is queued and `rewardPerTokenStored` increases, `earned()` is calculated against their new, large post-deposit balance — granting them credit as if they had been staked the whole time the reward accrued.

The trigger for queuing rewards, `WombatBribeManager.castVotes()`, is **unpermissioned** (any EOA can call it, and the caller even receives a `bribeCallerFee`): [5](#0-4) 
which calls `WombatStaking.vote()`, which loops over pools calling `IBaseRewardPool(_rewarders[i]).queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, ...)`: [6](#0-5) 

Attack flow, all atomic in one transaction, one EOA/contract, unprivileged:
1. Flash-loan a large amount of pool LP token.
2. `deposit`/`depositFor` into `MasterMagpie` for the target pool via `WombatPoolHelper`/`AnkrBNBPoolHelper` (`IMasterMagpie(masterMagpie).depositFor`) — this sets the attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` checkpoint at the pre-reward `rewardPerTokenStored`. [7](#0-6) 
3. Call `castVotes()` (public, no access control) which harvests bribes and calls `queueNewRewards`, bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` that now includes the attacker's flash-loaned deposit.
4. `withdraw`/`withdrawFor` from `MasterMagpie` — this calls `_harvestBaseRewarder` → `getReward`, transferring the attacker's inflated `earned()` share, then returns the principal LP.
5. Repay the flash loan, keeping the extracted reward as profit.

None of `nonReentrant`, `whenNotPaused`, or the receipt-token accounting prevent this because it is not reentrancy — it is a sequence of independent, permitted calls by the same caller within one transaction, and the reward-index model itself has no cooldown or time-weighting to exclude same-block/flash deposits from freshly queued rewards.

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct theft-of-yield vector: freshly harvested bribe rewards intended for genuine long-term voters/stakers of a pool are partially or wholly redirected to an attacker who held capital in the pool for essentially zero real time, financed entirely by a flash loan. This matches the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield" and, if repeated across every `castVotes()` call, causes a continuous drain of bribe income from legitimate stakers to whichever address front-runs each vote/harvest cycle with the largest flash-loaned deposit.

### Likelihood Explanation
- No privileged role required: `castVotes()` is public and even pays the caller a fee, so the attacker fully controls call ordering and timing.
- No flash-loan-specific protection, deposit lockup, or reward-vesting delay exists in `BaseRewardPool`/`MasterMagpie` to prevent a same-block deposit from capturing an immediately-queued reward.
- Capital requirement is only a flash loan of the relevant LP token size relative to existing `totalStaked()`, which is broadly available for major LP tokens.
- The attack is fully repeatable on every `castVotes()`/`harvestSinglePool()` call and scales with the size of the queued bribe reward relative to the flash-loanable pool liquidity.

### Recommendation
Redesign reward accrual so that only stakers who held their position through the period the reward was earned are eligible for it, e.g.:
- Snapshot `totalStaked()`/user balances at the start of the epoch/vote cycle rather than using the live value at `queueNewRewards` time.
- Introduce a minimum staking duration (or a reward-eligibility delay) before newly deposited stake begins accruing a share of newly queued rewards.
- Alternatively, stream new rewards linearly over time (as in standard `StakingRewards`/`Synthetix` reward-rate models) instead of instantaneously bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` for the full amount in a single block, which removes the incentive to flash-deposit immediately before a harvest.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, `BaseRewardPool`, `WombatStaking`, `WombatBribeManager` (or minimal mocks matching their interfaces) with a mock LP token and mock bribe/reward token.
2. Seed the pool with a "genuine" staker who deposits LP and waits (simulating normal usage) so bribes are expected to accrue to them proportionally over time.
3. Set up a flash-loan mock (e.g., an ERC-3156-style mock vault holding a large LP balance).
4. Attacker contract, in a single external call:
   - Borrows LP from the flash-loan mock.
   - Calls `MasterMagpie.depositFor`/`WombatPoolHelper.deposit` to stake the borrowed LP.
   - Calls `WombatBribeManager.castVotes(false)` (or directly `WombatStaking.vote(...)`) to trigger `queueNewRewards` on the pool's `BaseRewardPool` with a nonzero bribe amount.
   - Calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw`/`withdrawFor` to unstake and trigger `getReward`, claiming the reward token.
   - Repays the flash loan.
5. Assertions:
   - `rewardToken.balanceOf(attacker) > 0` after loan repayment (net profit extracted with zero starting capital).
   - The genuine staker's `earned()` (computed from `BaseRewardPool.earned`) after the same `castVotes()` call is measurably lower than it would have been had the attacker's flash-loaned stake not diluted the pro-rata share at reward-queue time.
   - Confirm the full sequence executes in one transaction (`vm.startPrank`/single external call from attacker contract) with no reverts from `nonReentrant`/`whenNotPaused` guards, proving no existing protection stops the attack.
