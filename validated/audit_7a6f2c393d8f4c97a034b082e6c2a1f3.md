### Title
Bribe Reward Sniping via Unrestricted `vote()`/`castVotes()` Sequencing Bypasses the Voting Accrual Period - ([File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol])

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager.vote()`, `castVotes()`, `voteAndCast()` and `harvestSinglePool()` are all callable by any wallet with no lock, cooldown, or snapshot mechanism between changing vote weight and triggering a bribe harvest. This lets a holder of already-locked `vlMGP` instantly redirect voting weight into a pool immediately before triggering (or front-running) its bribe harvest, capture a pro-rata share of the freshly distributed bribe rewards accrued by other voters, and then immediately withdraw ("unvote") — extracting yield that should belong only to voters who held their position throughout the accrual period. This mirrors the Oval bug class where a mechanism meant to guarantee that only the party who "committed" earlier can claim newly available value is bypassed due to the absence of any timing constraint.

### Finding Description
`vote()` lets a caller instantly increase or decrease `pool.totalVoteInVlmgp` and calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` with no delay: [1](#0-0) 

`stakeFor` in `BribeRewardPool` immediately increases `totalSupply` and the caller's `_balances`, snapshotting `userRewardPerTokenPaid` to the current `rewardPerToken()` via the `updateRewards` modifier — before any new rewards are queued: [2](#0-1) 

`castVotes()` / `harvestSinglePool()` are public, unrestricted functions that trigger `wombatStaking.vote()`, which harvests pending Wombat bribes and forwards them to the pool's `BribeRewardPool` via reward queuing, increasing `rewardPerTokenStored` proportionally to `totalStaked()` at that instant: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because `earned = balance * (rewardPerToken() - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`, a wallet that calls `vote()` to add weight immediately before `castVotes()`/`harvestSinglePool()` executes receives a full pro-rata share of the just-harvested bribes, despite having contributed zero time to the voting/accrual period that generated those bribes. `unvote()` similarly has no cooldown, letting the attacker withdraw the weight (and claim the reward) right after: [5](#0-4) 

This bypasses the implicit invariant that bribe rewards accrue to voters who supported a gauge over the period the bribes were earned — analogous to the Oval report where a missing timing safeguard (`maxAge` vs `lockWindow`) let a party capture value that was supposed to be reserved for whoever committed earlier.

### Impact Explanation
Any wallet already holding locked `vlMGP` (a normal, unprivileged position) can dynamically redirect its voting weight to whichever pool is about to be harvested, repeatedly sniping bribe rewards across all bribe pools. This constitutes theft of unclaimed yield from genuine long-term voters, since the sniper's added `totalStaked()` dilutes `rewardPerTokenStored` allocated to honest voters while granting the sniper an equal per-token share for effectively zero holding time.

### Likelihood Explanation
`vote()`, `unvote()`, `castVotes()`, and `harvestSinglePool()` are all public/external functions with no `onlyOwner` or role restriction, reachable directly from an ordinary wallet in the same transaction or adjacent blocks (including via `voteAndCast()` in a single call). No minimum lock duration or snapshot delay protects the reward distribution, making this trivially and repeatedly executable by anyone holding vlMGP.

### Recommendation
Introduce a minimum holding/lock period (or a snapshot mechanism) before a voter's weight counts toward newly harvested/queued rewards — e.g., only count vote weight that existed prior to the block in which `castVotes()`/`harvestSinglePool()` is called, or require votes to remain in place for a minimum duration before they are eligible for the next reward queuing event.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker holds vlMGP locked (already-votable balance) with active votes on Pool A.
2. Attacker observes (or triggers) that Pool B is about to receive a large bribe harvest.
3. Attacker calls `unvote(PoolA)` then `vote([PoolB], [+delta])` in the block immediately before `castVotes()`/`harvestSinglePool([PoolB])` executes (or bundles it in the same transaction via `voteAndCast`).
4. `castVotes()` harvests PoolB's bribes and calls `queueNewRewards`, increasing `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` which now includes the attacker's freshly added weight.
5. Attacker calls `claimBribe([PoolB])` to receive a full pro-rata share of the harvested bribes.
6. Attacker calls `unvote(PoolB)` immediately, freeing the vlMGP weight to repeat the process on the next pool about to be harvested — extracting yield without ever contributing voting weight during the actual accrual period, at the expense of genuine long-term voters.
