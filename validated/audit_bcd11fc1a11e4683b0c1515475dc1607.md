### Title
Reward donation/harvest can be instantly sniped via flash-stake due to unweighted, single-snapshot `rewardPerTokenStored` update - (File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol, rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol)

### Summary
The external report's root cause is that a critical distributed value (the auto-roll price) is derived from a single, unweighted, unverified snapshot (the last order/last block price) instead of a time/volume-weighted value, allowing a cheap transaction to skew it. The same structural weakness exists in Magpie's `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` reward accounting: incoming rewards are injected into `rewardPerTokenStored` **instantly and in full** based on the `totalStaked()` snapshot at the exact block of injection, rather than being streamed over time. Any wallet can inflate its share of `totalStaked()` immediately before a reward-injecting call and immediately after capture a share of the reward disproportionate to its actual staking duration.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` (used by both `queueNewRewards` and the permissionless `donateRewards`) updates the pool's `rewardPerTokenStored` in one shot: [1](#0-0) 

The increment is `(_amountReward * 10**decimals) / totalStaked()` computed against the **instantaneous** `totalStaked()`: [2](#0-1) 

`donateRewards` is callable by *any* address (not just `onlyManager`), so an attacker can trigger this injection path directly: [3](#0-2) 

The same one-shot, non-time-weighted pattern exists in `BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward`: [4](#0-3) 

Because a user's earned amount is `balance * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`, and `userRewardPerTokenPaid` is only synced to the current `rewardPerTokenStored` at the moment of deposit/withdraw: [5](#0-4) 

...a wallet that deposits a large stake into `MasterMagpie` immediately before a reward-injection event, and withdraws immediately after, captures a share of the newly injected reward as if it had been staked the entire accrual period, at the expense of genuine long-term stakers whose share of `rewardPerTokenStored` is diluted by the attacker's temporary presence in `totalStaked()`. This mirrors the report's core flaw: a value used for a critical distribution calculation is taken from a single, easily-manipulated instant rather than being smoothed/weighted, and a low-cost action (here, a flash deposit rather than a "few wei" order) can skew the outcome.

The reward-injection trigger itself is also reachable by an ordinary wallet without any privileged role: `WombatStaking.deposit`/`depositLP` call `_toMasterWomAndSendReward`, which harvests from the Wombat exchange and forwards to `queueNewRewards` on every call: [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 

This means any unprivileged wallet can (1) deposit a large stake into the target pool via `MasterMagpie`, (2) call `depositLP`/`deposit` with a trivial amount on `WombatStaking` to force a harvest-and-queue event while holding the inflated stake, and (3) immediately withdraw, capturing a disproportionate cut of the harvested WOM/bribe rewards that should have accrued to long-term stakers.

### Impact Explanation
This results in theft of unclaimed yield from genuine long-term stakers, whose expected share of harvested rewards is diluted and redirected to the flash-staking attacker — one of the accepted impact categories (theft of unclaimed yield).

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood depends on: (a) the size of capital an attacker can temporarily commit relative to existing `totalStaked()` (the smaller the existing pool, the higher the attacker's captured share), and (b) whether `MasterMagpie`'s deposit/withdraw functions impose any lock, cooldown, or exit fee that would negate the profit of an immediate withdraw. I was unable to confirm the latter within my remaining tool budget — `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw` internals were not retrieved before the iteration limit. If no lock/fee exists (as is typical for non-`vlMGP`/non-locked pools such as generic Wombat LP receipt-token pools), the attack is executable end-to-end by a single unprivileged EOA within one or two transactions, making likelihood high for smaller/thinner pools and moderate for larger ones.

### Recommendation
Stream newly injected rewards over a fixed duration (Synthetix-style `rewardRate`/`periodFinish`) rather than adding the full amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single instantaneous update, so that a transient increase in `totalStaked()` cannot capture a disproportionate share of a lump-sum reward. Alternatively, require a minimum holding period (time-weighted average balance) before a staker's balance counts toward reward-share calculations, analogous to using a "last reliable block" weighted price rather than a single unverified snapshot in the original report.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` for a Wombat pool with relatively low `totalStaked()`.
2. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`depositFor` (via the pool's helper) with a large amount of the staking/receipt token, inflating `totalStaked()` several-fold.
3. Attacker calls `WombatStaking.depositLP`/`deposit` with a trivial amount, which internally invokes `_toMasterWomAndSendReward` → harvest + `queueNewRewards`, injecting the full pending WOM/bribe reward into `rewardPerTokenStored` divided by the now-inflated `totalStaked()` [8](#0-7) .
4. Attacker immediately calls `getReward`/`getRewards` to claim their pro-rata share (computed via `_earned`, using their large balance against the full `rewardPerToken` delta) [5](#0-4) , then withdraws their staked principal via `MasterMagpie.withdraw`.
5. Net effect: attacker earns reward proportional to a large balance held for a near-zero duration, diluting the share that should have accrued to genuine long-term stakers.

**Caveat:** Full confirmation of exploitability requires verifying `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw` for any lock/cooldown/fee that could block step 5 — this was not completed due to tool-call exhaustion and should be validated in a follow-up session with full repository access.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
    }
