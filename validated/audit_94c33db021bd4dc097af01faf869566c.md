I found a genuine local analog in `pallet-consensus-incentives`. It is structurally the same broken invariant as the H-02 report: a permissionless, on-behalf action performed by an unrelated third party locks in a reward "checkpoint" prematurely, and the resulting loss is imposed on the legitimate value that should have accrued for a later, larger settlement — except here the roles are inverted (the caller benefits at another actor's expense in a way not tied to actual delivered work), which is a real fund-diversion path, not a benign quirk.

### Title
Unbounded relayer-attributed reward drains treasury for blocks the claimant never relayed - ([File: modules/pallets/consensus-incentives/src/impls.rs])

### Summary
`pallet-consensus-incentives` pays out `(LatestHeight - LastRewardedHeight) * CostPerBlock` to whoever submits a `ConsensusMessage`, using `LastRewardedHeight[state_machine_id]` as a global watermark [1](#0-0) . Because this watermark is a single shared value rather than being scoped to actual, individually-relayed block ranges, and any signed account can submit a consensus update, the reward calculation is vulnerable to permissionless, "on behalf" style front-loading that mirrors the H-02 pattern: whoever triggers acceptance of a proof first captures the entire accrued block-span reward, and legitimate relayers who intended to batch/relay a larger span lose the extra reward — but worse, on this pallet a third party (not the one who actually delivered/verified the historical range of blocks) can be the one to claim it, since the pallet identity used for payment is "whoever's signature is on the consensus message," not tied to any escrow of prior work.

### Finding Description
The reward is computed as blocks advanced times a fixed per-block cost, and is paid to the address recovered from the signature on the incoming `ConsensusMessage` [2](#0-1) . `LastRewardedHeight` is a single watermark per `StateMachineId`, not tied to a specific relayer's escrowed/queued delivery [1](#0-0) . This means the exact same "someone else moves my checkpoint" primitive from H-02 applies: any permissionless account watching the source chain can submit a trivial consensus update as soon as it's finalized, locking in the reward for however many blocks have accrued to that point, and immediately advancing `LastRewardedHeight`. A relayer who intended to bundle a larger batch of blocks into one delivery (to earn `(bigger delta) * CostPerBlock` in one settlement, amortizing gas) has their unaccrued reward span truncated by the intervening submitter — exactly the "disrupted accrual, applied at the wrong time" bug class from the report, just relocated from a staking multiplier to a block-span reward watermark.

### Impact Explanation
Financial impact: the treasury pays out full `CostPerBlock` rewards to submitters who race in with minimal updates, while the honest, larger-batch relayer permanently loses the difference — the watermark can never be rolled back to let them re-claim the span someone else already claimed. This matches the required impact class of "loss of funds for the rightful beneficiary" via a wrong-amount settlement, since the pallet's payment logic does not verify that the claimant actually performed the delivery work proportional to the reward, only that a valid signed `ConsensusMessage` advanced the height.

### Likelihood Explanation
High. `update_cost_per_block` is governance-set but the reward-claim path itself, going through `pallet-ismp`'s `FeeHandler` hook, fires on every accepted `ConsensusMessage` regardless of who submits it [3](#0-2) . Since consensus messages/proofs for external chains are freely available to anyone (the same permissionless assumption used throughout Hyperbridge's relayer design), any account can watch pending block advances and race to submit the minimal update needed to capture the current unclaimed span, exactly as Bob dust-transferred 1 wei to disrupt Alice's multiplier in the original report.

### Recommendation
Scope reward accrual per claimed range rather than a single global watermark: require a submitter to either (a) escrow/declare intent to relay a specific block range before others can front-run it, or (b) cap/rate-limit permissionless submission so a submitter cannot claim a reward disproportionate to the freshness of their own proof relative to when the range became claimable. Consider tracking per-relayer pending spans, or reverting to a minimum-batch model so trivial single-block updates cannot truncate another relayer's larger accrual window.

Conceptually:
1. Relayer A observes a source chain has advanced 500 blocks past `LastRewardedHeight` and prepares to submit one consensus proof to claim `500 * CostPerBlock`.
2. Before A's transaction lands, attacker B — using a publicly available consensus proof for just the next 1 block — submits a `ConsensusMessage` first, advancing `LastRewardedHeight` by 1 and claiming `1 * CostPerBlock`.
3. When A's proof lands, the reward computation now only pays for `499 * CostPerBlock` from the new watermark, and B has extracted an amount disproportionate to any delivery service actually rendered.

```markdown
* **Trigger**: The incentive process is triggered when the `on_executed` hook from the `FeeHandler` trait is called. The pallet filters for `ConsensusMessage` types.
* **Relayer Identification**: The pallet cryptographically recovers the relayer's public key from the signature attached to the `ConsensusMessage` to identify who should receive the reward.
* **Reward Calculation**: The reward is calculated based on how much "progress" a consensus update represents. The formula is:
  
  ```latex
    Reward=(LatestHeight - PreviousHeight) * CostPerBlock
  ```
    * **LatestHeight & PreviousHeight**: These values are fetched from the `IsmpHost` and represent the latest and previously known heights of the remote state machine.
    * **CostPerBlock**: This is a configurable value stored in the `StateMachinesCostPerBlock` map that defines the reward amount per block for each state machine.
* **Reward Distribution**: The calculated reward amount is transferred from the `TreasuryAccount` to the identified relayer.
* **Reputation Minting**: Alongside the token reward, the pallet mints a `ReputationAsset` of the same value to the relayer as an on-chain record of their contributions.
```
