Looking at the external bug's class — **authorization bypass enabling unauthorized access to bridge-controlled resources** — I searched Clementine's tx-sender, gRPC interceptors, operator/verifier RPC handlers, and deposit/withdrawal flows for a native analog.

The gRPC layer is well-protected: `Interceptors::OnlyAggregatorAndSelf` enforces mTLS leaf-certificate pinning, and `internal_*` methods are further restricted to the entity's own certificate.

However, the **TxSender JSON-RPC server** is a separate service with **no authentication whatsoever**.
