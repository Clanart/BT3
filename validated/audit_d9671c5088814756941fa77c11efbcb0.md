## Analysis

This confirms the classic instant reward-per-token accounting model used by `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`/`_provisionReward`: whenever bribes are harvested, `rewardPerTokenStored` is bumped immediately in proportion to `1/totalSupply` at that instant [1](#0-0) , and any account whose `userRewardPerTokenPaid` is stale (i.e., just staked) is entitled to the *entire* new increment times its current balance via `_earned` [2](#0-1) . There is no vesting, cooldown, streaming (`rewardRate`/`periodFinish`), or minimum holding period anywhere in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`/`DelegateVoteRewardPool`.

However, to reach `IDelegateVoteRewardPool(delegatedPool).getReward(attacker)` via `claimAllBribes`, the precondition is `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp[attacker][delegatedPool] > 0` [3](#0-2) . That mapping is only incremented in `WombatBribeManager.vote()`, which requires the caller to actually possess unused vlMGP voting capacity — `getUserVotable(msg.sender)` is checked at the end of `vote()` and reverts with `NotEnoughVote()` if exceeded [4](#0-3) . vlMGP voting power comes from locked MGP (a real, non-flash-loanable position established via governance locking, separate from this codebase's flash-loan-able assets), so the attacker cannot manufacture the vote weight in the same transaction from zero — they need pre-existing locked MGP. This is a genuine capital/precondition constraint, not a full mitigation of the underlying accounting flaw.

Given that constraint is satisfiable (any vlMGP holder with unused voting capacity can reallocate votes to the `delegatedPool` "pool" via `vote()`, which internally calls `stakeFor` on the `DelegateVoteRewardPool` and immediately updates `_balances` and vote weights [5](#0-4)  and [6](#0-5) ), an attacker who already holds spare vlMGP capacity can front-run a pending `castVotes()`/`harvestAll()` transaction: vote into `delegatedPool` right before harvest, letting `_updateVote()` register their vote weight, then have `claimAllBribes(attacker)` triggered (by anyone) right after `harvestAll()` distributes the newly harvested bribes, entitling the attacker to a full pro-rata share of rewards that were earned over the entire preceding period by other depositors, not just the brief window the attacker was staked.

### Title
Reward sniping via flash-vote into delegatedPool before harvestAll dilutes/steals unclaimed yield from genuine depositors - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol / rewards/DelegateVoteRewardPool.sol)

### Summary
`DelegateVoteRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` distribute bribe rewards using an instantaneous `rewardPerTokenStored += amount/totalSupply` update with no time-weighting, streaming, or lock-up. An attacker holding unused vlMGP voting capacity can vote into `delegatedPool` immediately before another user's `castVotes()` triggers `harvestAll()`, then call `claimAllBribes(attacker)` to receive a pro-rata share of rewards accrued over the entire prior period, diluting genuine long-term depositors.

### Finding Description
`WombatBribeManager.vote()` lets any vlMGP holder allocate votes to `delegatedPool` as a "pool", routing to `IBribeRewardPool(delegatedPool).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` [5](#0-4) , which increments `_balances[attacker]` and `totalSupply` in `DelegateVoteRewardPool` instantly, with no vesting period [6](#0-5) .

When any user later calls `castVotes()`, it calls `IDelegateVoteRewardPool(delegatedPool).harvestAll()` [7](#0-6) , which in turn calls `claimAllBribes(address(this))` to pull the accumulated bribes for the delegated pool from all underlying pools it voted into, then calls `_manageRewards` [8](#0-7) . `_manageRewards` immediately folds the entire harvested amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` via `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`, using the *current* `totalSupply` at that instant [1](#0-0) .

Because `_earned` computes `userShare * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid) / 1e_decimals + userRewards`, an account whose `userRewardPerTokenPaid` was just set to the pre-harvest value (by having just staked) is entitled to `userShare * fullNewIncrement`, exactly the same as an account that held that share for the entire accrual period [2](#0-1) . There is no `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` streaming mechanism to time-weight rewards, unlike typical Synthetix-style staking rewards contracts.

The attacker then calls `WombatBribeManager.claimAllBribes(attacker)` (public, callable by anyone for any `_for`), which checks `userVotedForPoolInVlmgp[attacker][delegatedPool] > 0` and forwards to `IDelegateVoteRewardPool(delegatedPool).getReward(attacker)` [9](#0-8) , paying out the attacker's inflated `earned()` share immediately.

Existing checks that limit but don't eliminate the issue: `vote()` requires the caller to have unused vlMGP votable capacity (`getUserVotable`) [4](#0-3) , so the attacker needs pre-existing locked MGP — this is not flash-loanable capital, but it can be pre-staged well in advance and reused indefinitely (vote in, harvest, unvote/withdraw, repeat) since unvoting simply calls `withdrawFor` with no lock time [10](#0-9) .

### Impact Explanation
This causes theft of unclaimed yield: an attacker who briefly reallocates existing vote weight into `delegatedPool` right before a harvest captures a full pro-rata share of bribes accrued over the entire preceding period, at the expense of the pool's genuine longer-term depositors whose effective share of the same harvested rewards is diluted. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class scoped in this audit.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions: the attacker must already hold locked MGP with unused vlMGP votable capacity (or must lock MGP in advance — locking is not flash-loanable but capital can be reused repeatedly across many harvest cycles since `unvote`/`vote` have no cooldown). The attacker must front-run a pending `castVotes()` transaction (public mempool, feasible via higher gas price) to land their `vote()` call in the same or an earlier block right before `harvestAll()` executes. This is repeatable indefinitely as long as harvests keep occurring, and capital efficiency improves with each repetition since the same vlMGP allocation can be cycled in and out around every harvest.

### Recommendation
Introduce a minimum staking duration / cooldown before a voter's stake in `delegatedPool` is eligible to receive newly harvested rewards (e.g., snapshot balances used for distribution based on balance held before the harvest was initiated, or require a lock period between `stakeFor`/`vote` and reward eligibility). Alternatively, switch to a time-weighted / streamed reward distribution (rewardRate over a duration) instead of instantaneously folding the full harvested amount into `rewardPerTokenStored`, so that new stakers cannot retroactively capture rewards earned before their deposit.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `DelegateVoteRewardPool` as `delegatedPool`, and an underlying pool with a bribe rewarder; register `delegatedPool` as a poolInfos entry whose `rewarder == delegatedPool`.
2. Have "Victim" lock MGP, obtain vlMGP, and call `vote()` to allocate votes into `delegatedPool` at time T0; wait N blocks/days while bribes accrue on the underlying pool.
3. Immediately before calling `castVotes()` (which triggers `harvestAll`), have "Attacker" (who already holds unused vlMGP capacity from a pre-existing lock) call `vote()` to allocate votes into `delegatedPool` in the same block/transaction ordering (front-run).
4. Call `castVotes()` (any caller) to trigger `IDelegateVoteRewardPool(delegatedPool).harvestAll()`, which pulls the accrued bribes and updates `rewardPerTokenStored`.
5. Call `claimAllBribes(attacker)` and `claimAllBribes(victim)`; record `earnedRewards` for both.
6. Assert: `attacker.earnedRewards / attacker.stakeDuration` (near 0 duration) is orders of magnitude larger than a time-weighted fair share baseline computed as `victim.earnedRewards * (attacker.balance/victim.balance) * (attacker.stakeDuration/victim.stakeDuration)`, demonstrating the attacker captured rewards disproportionate to their actual contribution period.
