### Title
Front-Running Reward Distributions to Steal Yield from Other Stakers - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol](rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol))

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool` (and its variants `BaseRewardPoolV2`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, `vlMGPBaseRewarder`) distribute rewards by instantly bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` in proportion to the pool's *current* `totalStaked()` at the moment the reward is provisioned, rather than streaming it over time. Because deposits/withdrawals to the underlying `MasterMagpie` pools have no cooldown, lock, or minimum holding period, an ordinary unprivileged wallet can front-run an incoming reward-funding transaction with a large `deposit`, capture a disproportionate share of the newly added rewards, and immediately withdraw — diluting and stealing yield from legitimate long-term stakers. This is analogous to the reported bug class: an actor exploits the lack of any delay/anti-manipulation mechanism around a value-affecting event (there, fee changes; here, reward funding) to capture value that should have accrued to other users.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` in `BaseRewardPool.sol` immediately converts the incoming reward amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` using the pool's `totalStaked()` at that exact block: [1](#0-0) 

`rewardPerToken()` simply returns this stored value with no time-weighting or vesting: [2](#0-1) 

`earned()` computes a user's reward strictly as `balanceOf(user) * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`, so anyone holding a balance at the moment `rewardPerTokenStored` increases immediately becomes entitled to a proportional share, regardless of how long they have staked: [3](#0-2) 

Deposits/withdrawals into the pools tracked by `BaseRewardPool` (via `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw`) have no cooldown, minimum-duration requirement, or fee that would penalize instant in-and-out staking: [4](#0-3) 

`queueNewRewards` (manager-triggered, e.g. by `WombatStaking` after harvesting) and `donateRewards` (fully permissionless) both funnel into the same instant-distribution `_provisionReward` path: [5](#0-4) 

An unprivileged attacker who observes a pending reward-funding transaction in the mempool (or simply times deposits around predictable harvest/vote-cast cadences in `WombatStaking`/`WombatBribeManager`) can:
1. Deposit a large amount of `stakingToken` into the pool immediately before the reward-funding transaction lands, inflating `totalStaked()` and their own `balanceOf`.
2. Let the reward transaction land, which increases `rewardPerTokenStored` based on the now-inflated stake, awarding the attacker's newly deposited balance a full share of the reward with zero holding duration.
3. Immediately withdraw the principal and claim the reward, having contributed no meaningful time-weighted stake to the pool.

This works identically across `BaseRewardPool`, `BaseRewardPoolV2`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, and `vlMGPBaseRewarder`, all of which share this instant/undiluted-by-time reward math.

### Impact Explanation
This results in direct theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate stakers: rewards that would otherwise have accrued proportionally to genuine long-term depositors are instead captured by an attacker who briefly and artificially inflates their share right before a reward injection. Because `queueNewRewards`/harvest events in `WombatStaking` and `WombatBribeManager` occur on a fairly predictable cadence (vote casting, harvesting), and `donateRewards` is fully public and can even be triggered/observed by the attacker themselves in combination with a deposit, this is a repeatable, reachable pattern for any wallet with sufficient capital, not requiring any privileged role.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high: no special permissions are needed, the attack requires only capital and either mempool visibility of `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` calls or knowledge of the routine harvest/vote-casting schedule in `WombatStaking.vote`/`WombatBribeManager.castVotes`. No lock, cooldown, or vesting mechanism exists anywhere in `MasterMagpie` or the reward pools to prevent instant deposit-then-withdraw around a reward event.

### Recommendation
Introduce reward streaming (vesting rewards linearly over a period, as in standard `StakingRewards`-style contracts) instead of instantly reflecting the full reward amount in `rewardPerTokenStored`, or require a minimum staking duration/cooldown before newly deposited balances become eligible for freshly queued rewards.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker monitors mempool for an upcoming `queueNewRewards` (or calls/observes `donateRewards`) call on a `BaseRewardPool` instance.
2. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`depositFor` (via the pool's `stakingToken`) with a large amount just before that transaction is mined, inflating `totalStaked()`. [6](#0-5) 
3. The reward transaction executes `_provisionReward`, increasing `rewardPerTokenStored` proportionally to the now-inflated `totalStaked()`. [1](#0-0) 
4. Attacker calls `withdrawFor`/`withdraw` to remove their staked principal, then calls `getReward` to claim their share of `earned()`, computed with zero effective holding time. [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) 
5. Net effect: attacker extracts yield proportional to their capital at the time of the reward event, diluting the rewards legitimate long-term stakers would have earned.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
