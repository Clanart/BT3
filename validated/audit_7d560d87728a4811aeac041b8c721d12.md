### Title
Front-running `harvest()` with a deposit lets an attacker steal a disproportionate share of newly queued WOM/bonus rewards - (File: [wombat/WombatStaking.sol](), [rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol]())

### Summary
`BaseRewardPoolV2` distributes newly received reward tokens instantly and proportionally to whatever `totalStaked()` (live receipt-token balance held by `MasterMagpie`) happens to be at the moment `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` is called [1](#0-0) . `WombatStaking.harvest()` is an unrestricted external function that anyone can call to pull pending WOM (and bonus token) rewards from Wombat and push them into the corresponding rewarder via `queueNewRewards` [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) . Because a user's share of freshly queued rewards is based purely on their current staked balance and not on how long they were staked while the reward accrued, an attacker can deposit immediately before `harvest()` and withdraw immediately after, capturing a share of rewards that accrued entirely before their deposit — exactly the "new deposits instantly getting a share of undistributed rewards" bug class from the report.

### Finding Description
`BaseRewardPoolV2._updateFor`/`updateReward` snapshots `userRewardPerTokenPaid` to the *current* `rewardPerToken` value at deposit time [4](#0-3) . `MasterMagpie._deposit` calls `_harvestBaseRewarder` (which triggers this checkpoint) **before** increasing `user.amount`, and `totalStaked()` in the rewarder is simply the live `IERC20(stakingToken).balanceOf(operator)` [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) . So immediately after depositing, the attacker's balance is already counted in `totalStaked()` with a reward-per-token baseline equal to the pre-harvest value.

`WombatStaking.harvest(_lpToken)` can be called by any address (only gated by `_onlyActivePool`, not an owner/whitelist/EOA-only check) and triggers `_toMasterWomAndSendReward`, which withdraws 0 liquidity from MasterWombat (this itself triggers Wombat's internal harvest of WOM/bonus rewards accrued since the last interaction) and forwards the harvested amount into the rewarder via `_sendRewards -> queueNewRewards` [7](#0-6) [8](#0-7) . `_provisionReward`/`queueNewRewards` immediately bumps `rewardPerTokenStored` by `_amountReward * 10^decimals / totalStaked()` using the *current* total, with no time-weighting or streaming [9](#0-8) .

Consequently, an attacker can:
1. Deposit a large amount of LP tokens right before calling (or in the same transaction as) `WombatStaking.harvest(lpToken)` via `WombatPoolHelper.deposit()`/`depositLP()` [10](#0-9) .
2. Call/trigger `harvest()`, which queues the accumulated WOM rewards proportionally across the now-inflated `totalStaked()`, including the attacker's fresh stake [2](#0-1) .
3. Immediately withdraw via `WombatPoolHelper.withdraw()`, which calls `MasterMagpie.withdrawFor` → `_harvestBaseRewarder` → `getReward`, paying out the attacker's share of the newly queued rewards [11](#0-10) [12](#0-11) .

This dilutes and steals yield from honest long-term stakers who were staked while the WOM rewards actually accrued in Wombat.

### Impact Explanation
This is direct theft of unclaimed yield from existing stakers: an attacker with a large enough temporary deposit (regular loan or flash-loan-funded LP acquisition, or simply large capital) can capture a disproportionate share of harvested rewards without having contributed to the staking period during which those rewards accrued. No price manipulation is required, matching the described attack pattern, and the attack directly reduces payouts to legitimate long-term LPs.

### Likelihood Explanation
`harvest()` is callable by anyone and is expected to be called periodically to realize WOM emissions; deposit/withdraw are also permissionless for any pool helper user. The only friction is a possible withdrawal fee, if configured (`isPoolFeeFree`), plus normal LP acquisition, but the report explicitly notes no flash loan is even required, only a normal loan, and the risk of price movement during the short window is low. Given harvest is a normal, frequently-triggered operation, the opportunity window recurs regularly.

### Recommendation
Do not let a fresh deposit take part in rewards accrued before the deposit. Options include: (1) applying reward accrual to `totalStaked()` computed *before* the current transaction's stake/unstake delta (i.e., snapshot old totalStaked prior to updating balances), (2) streaming newly queued rewards over time (rate-based distribution, à la Synthetix `rewardRate`/`periodFinish`) instead of instantly bumping `rewardPerTokenStored`, or (3) requiring `harvest()`/`queueNewRewards` to run and checkpoint balances before any deposit's shares become eligible for the reward that was already pending at call time, combined with a minimum staking duration before a deposit's balance counts toward `totalStaked()` used in `_provisionReward`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes (or triggers) that `WombatStaking.harvest(lpToken)` is about to be called (it is unrestricted and can be called by the attacker directly).
2. In the same transaction (or immediately before), attacker calls `WombatPoolHelper.deposit()`/`depositLP()` with a large LP amount, which flows through `WombatStaking.depositLP` and `MasterMagpie.depositFor`, increasing the rewarder's `totalStaked()` [13](#0-12) [14](#0-13) .
3. Attacker (or anyone) calls `WombatStaking.harvest(lpToken)`, which harvests accumulated WOM from MasterWombat and calls `queueNewRewards` on the rewarder, bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` based on the now-inflated `totalStaked()` [2](#0-1) [9](#0-8) .
4. Attacker calls `WombatPoolHelper.withdraw()`, which withdraws the LP and, via `_harvestBaseRewarder`/`getReward`, transfers the attacker's proportional share of the just-queued WOM rewards [11](#0-10) .
5. Attacker has captured yield proportional to the deposit size despite having staked for effectively zero time during the actual reward-accrual period, at the expense of pre-existing stakers.

Note: I could not fully verify the exact wording/permissions of the `_onlyActivePool` modifier body (index search only located references, not the modifier definition) or whether a withdrawal fee/cooldown exists that could reduce attacker profit; a Devin session with full repo access would be needed to confirm these exact guard conditions if further validation is required.

    function _harvestAndUnstake(address _stakingToken, address _account, uint256 _amount, bool _isVlMgp) internal {
        updatePool(_stakingToken);

        UserInfo storage user = userInfo[_stakingToken][_account];

        if (!_isVlMgp && user.available < _amount)
            revert WithdrawAmountExceedsStaked();
        else if(user.amount < _amount && _isVlMgp)
            revert UnlockAmountExceedsLocked();
        
        _harvestMGP(_stakingToken, _account);
        _harvestBaseRewarder(_stakingToken, _account);

        user.amount = user.amount - _amount;
        
        if(!_isVlMgp)
            user.available = user.available - _amount;
        user.rewardDebt = (user.amount * tokenToPoolInfo[_stakingToken].accMGPPerShare) / 1e12;
    }
