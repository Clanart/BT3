### Title
Forged `ReceivedOutput` prevout values pass `multisig` validation and produce signatures over nonexistent funds - ([File: networks/bitcoin/src/wallet/send.rs](networks/bitcoin/src/wallet/send.rs))

### Summary
The analog of the Wormhole "recorded amount vs actual balance" mismatch lives in `ReceivedOutput`/`SignableTransaction`. `ReceivedOutput::read` accepts a fully attacker-supplied `TxOut` (value + script_pubkey) and `OutPoint`. `SignableTransaction::new` then trusts `input.output.value` for the fee/change math, and `SignableTransaction::multisig` validates only the `script_pubkey` against the offset-derived key — never checking that the claimed `value`/`outpoint` corresponds to a real on-chain output. The result mirrors the report: a recorded amount is used instead of the actual balance, and the downstream operation (here, producing a valid broadcastable spend) fails because reality doesn't match.

### Finding Description
`ReceivedOutput` is constructed either by `Scanner::scan_transaction` (trusted, from chain data) or by `ReceivedOutput::read` (untrusted bytes): [1](#0-0) . `SignableTransaction::new` sums `input.output.value` as `input_sat` for the funds-sufficiency check and stores the TxOuts verbatim as `prevouts` [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) . `multisig` only verifies `p2tr_script_buf(offset.group_key()) == prevouts[i].script_pubkey` — the *value* and *outpoint* are never validated [4](#0-3) . Signing then commits to `Prevouts::All(&self.tx.prevouts)`, i.e., the attacker-controlled amounts [5](#0-4) .

### Impact Explanation
An unprivileged party who can feed bytes to `ReceivedOutput::read` (explicitly in-scope attack surface) can craft a `ReceivedOutput` with the real `script_pubkey` (publicly derivable) but a fabricated `value` and/or nonexistent `outpoint`. Every honest participant's `TransactionMachine` produces a signature share committing — via BIP-341 `Prevouts::All` — to the forged prevout amount. The completed transaction is unbroadcastable: either the outpoint doesn't exist, or the committed prevout value doesn't match the UTXO, making the Schnorr signature invalid under consensus. This is a permanent DoS on the spend attempt (the `signature_share` was already consumed/broadcast), exactly analogous to `close_account` failing because actual balance ≠ recorded `amount_in`. Additionally, `fee()` reports a fabricated figure (`sum(prevouts) - sum(outputs)`), so fee accounting is corrupted by attacker input [6](#0-5) .

### Likelihood Explanation
Requires an integrator path where `ReceivedOutput`s are deserialized from untrusted input rather than obtained from `Scanner` over verified chain data. Within the declared threat model (untrusted bytes into `ReceivedOutput::read`), it is directly reachable; no collusion, no malicious validator, no leaked keys needed. Severity: Medium — DoS of spends and false balance reporting, no direct theft (the forged script check prevents redirection, and consensus prevents value inflation being paid out).

### Recommendation
Either mark `ReceivedOutput` deserialization as trusted-only and provide a `verify`-style API, or make `SignableTransaction::multisig`/spending verify each `prevout` against chain data (e.g., re-derive the output from the referenced transaction or require outputs only sourced from `Scanner`). At minimum, document that `ReceivedOutput::read` must never be applied to unauthenticated bytes, and have the signer reject inputs whose outpoint/value cannot be confirmed.
