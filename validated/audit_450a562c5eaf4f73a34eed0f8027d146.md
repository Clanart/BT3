### Title
Instant-stake front-running of `queueNewRewards` dilutes and steals time-weighted reward accrual - ([File: rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol])

### Summary
`_provisionReward` (invoked by `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards`) applies an entire reward donation to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single step using the instantaneous `totalStaked()` value, with no time-weighting or streaming. An attacker who observes a pending `queueNewRewards` transaction in the mempool can front-run it by increasing their `mWOMSV`-backing stake, immediately capture a full pro-rata share of the reward once it lands, and later unwind their position, diluting the payout that accrues to genuine long-term stakers.

### Finding Description
`rewardPerToken` is a simple stored accumulator: `rewards[_rewardToken].rewardPerTokenStored` [1](#0-0) . `_provisionReward` updates it in one shot as `rewardPerTokenStored += (_amountReward * 10**decimals) / totalStaked()`, where `totalStaked()` simply reads the current `mWOMSV` total supply at call time [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) .

A user's earned reward is computed purely from the delta between the current global accumulator and the value stored at their last update, multiplied by their *current* balance — with no minimum holding duration or time-integral: `_earned = (balance * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)) / 1e18 + userRewards` [4](#0-3) .

Balance is fetched live from `MasterMagpie.stakingInfo` [5](#0-4) , which is increased via `depositMWomSVFor` → `_deposit`, and `_deposit` harvests-and-snapshots the *pre-deposit* balance/reward-per-token before adding the new stake to `user.amount` [6](#0-5) . This means: any tokens staked immediately before a `queueNewRewards` call are fully eligible for the entire upcoming reward increment, with zero time cost.

Exploit flow:
1. Attacker watches mempool for `rewardManager.queueNewRewards(amount, token)`.
2. Attacker front-runs with `mWomSV.lock(D)` (real `mWOM` transfer, not flash-loanable) followed by `MasterMagpie.depositMWomSVFor`, inflating `totalStaked()` from `T` to `T+D`.
3. `queueNewRewards` executes: `rewardPerTokenStored += R / (T+D)` instead of the `R/T` that pre-existing stakers "should" have received.
4. Attacker back-runs with a claim (`getReward`/`getRewards`, reachable via `MasterMagpie`'s harvest/claim path — permissionless for self), receiving `D * R / (T+D)` of the reward despite zero holding time.
5. All pre-existing stakers who had not yet harvested receive a diluted `balance * R/(T+D)` instead of `balance * R/T` for this donation — a direct, quantifiable transfer of yield to the attacker.
6. Attacker can later call `mWomSV.startUnlock`/`unlock` after the `coolDownInSecs` period to reclaim `D`.

Existing protections do not stop this: `onlyManager` gates who can *call* `queueNewRewards`, but does not gate who benefits from it; `nonReentrant` is irrelevant here; `updateReward`/`updateRewards` modifiers correctly snapshot balances but do not incorporate any time-weighting; there is no minimum-stake-duration or streamed-emission (`rewardRate` over a `periodFinish`) design as used in Synthetix-style reward pools to prevent exactly this kind of donation sniping.

Note: withdrawal of the attacker's principal (`D`) is not instantaneous — `mWomSV.startUnlock`/`unlock` requires `coolDownInSecs` [7](#0-6) . This bounds attack repeatability/capital efficiency but does not block the attack itself, since the reward is captured immediately upon `getReward`, independent of when `D` is later withdrawn.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate long-term stakers: any lump-sum reward donation via `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` can be partially or mostly captured by capital that was staked for a single block, at the expense of stakers who held their position for the accrual period. This matches the Immunefi "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class. The magnitude scales with `D/(T+D)`, i.e. an attacker with capital comparable to or larger than existing `totalStaked()` can capture a large fraction of any single reward drop.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions: attacker needs (a) visibility into the mempool for `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` calls (public, predictable — `rewardManager` and reward amounts are often known or estimable in advance), (b) capital in `mWOM` sufficient to `lock()` a meaningful stake relative to `totalStaked()`, and (c) willingness to have that capital locked for `coolDownInSecs` to eventually reclaim it. No privileged role is required — `lock`, `depositMWomSVFor` (via the `mWomSV` contract's `_onlyMWomSV` gate, which any mWomSV lock holder can trigger through normal locking flow), and reward claiming are all permissionless user actions. This is fully repeatable for every future `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` call, making it a systemic, standing risk rather than a one-off. Feasibility is high on any chain with a public mempool.

### Recommendation
Replace the instantaneous lump-sum donation accounting with a time-weighted/streamed emission model (e.g., Synthetix-style `rewardRate` distributed linearly over a fixed `rewardsDuration`/`periodFinish`), so that newly staked capital only accrues rewards proportional to the time it is actually staked. Alternatively, introduce a minimum staking duration or a snapshot/checkpoint mechanism (e.g., freeze eligibility for stake added within N blocks of a reward injection) before new stake becomes eligible for pending reward distributions.

### Proof of Concept
Hardhat test plan:
1. Deploy `mWomSV`, `MasterMagpie`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, and a mock reward ERC20; register the reward token via `queueNewRewards` initialization.
2. Seed two long-term stakers, Alice and Bob, each `lock()`-ing `X` mWOM and depositing into `MasterMagpie` for `mWOMSV`, well before any reward donation; advance time.
3. Simulate mempool front-run: have Attacker call `mWomSV.lock(D)` + `depositMWomSVFor` in the same or immediately preceding block as `rewardManager.queueNewRewards(R, rewardToken)` (mine both in same block via Hardhat `evm_mine` batching, or send both txs with Attacker's tx submitted with higher gas price ahead of the pending `queueNewRewards` tx).
4. Immediately after, have Attacker call `getReward`/claim path via `MasterMagpie`.
5. Assert: `attackerReceived == D * R / (T + D)` (or approx, minus rounding) is greater than what a proportional, time-weighted allocation based on staking duration (near zero, since Attacker staked for ~0 time) would justify — i.e., Attacker receives a nontrivial reward despite having staked for a negligible duration compared to Alice/Bob.
6. Assert Alice's and Bob's `earned()` for that reward round is strictly less than it would have been (`balance * R / T`) had Attacker not front-run — computed by running a control scenario without the Attacker's stake and diffing the `rewardPerTokenStored` delta.
7. Optionally show Attacker later calls `startUnlock`/`unlock` after `coolDownInSecs` to recover `D`, closing the loop with net profit equal to the diluted portion of `R`.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20Metadata(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;

        if (totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**mWOMSVDecimal) / totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
    }
