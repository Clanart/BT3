### Title
Reward-pool accounting rewards instantaneous balance instead of time-weighted stake, allowing a same-block deposit→queueNewRewards→withdraw sandwich to capture a disproportionate share of newly queued rewards - (File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol)

### Summary
`balanceOf(_account)` in `BaseRewardPoolV2` reads the live staked amount from `MasterMagpie.stakingInfo`, and `_provisionReward`/`queueNewRewards` distributes newly queued rewards by dividing the reward amount by `totalStaked()` at the instant the call executes. Because neither function is time-weighted or cooldown-gated, and `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw` harvest rewards using the account's *current* balance right before/after mutating it, an attacker can inflate their share of `totalStaked()` for the single block in which `queueNewRewards` executes and capture a reward amount that tends toward the entire queued reward as their deposit size grows.

### Finding Description
`balanceOf()` has no snapshot or time component: [1](#0-0) 

Reward distribution mutates `rewardPerTokenStored` using the live `totalStaked()` denominator: [2](#0-1) 

`earned()`/`_earned()` multiply the user's *current* `balanceOf()` by the delta of `rewardPerToken` since the account was last synced: [3](#0-2) 

On the `MasterMagpie` side, `_deposit` harvests the base rewarder using the OLD (pre-increment) balance, then increases `user.amount`: [4](#0-3) 

`_withdraw`/`_harvestAndUnstake` harvests the base rewarder using the CURRENT (pre-decrement, i.e. still-full) balance before subtracting `_amount`: [5](#0-4) 

**Exploit flow:**
1. Attacker flash-loans a large amount `A` of `stakingToken` and calls `MasterMagpie.deposit(stakingToken, A)`. `_harvestBaseRewarder` runs on the attacker's old (near-zero) balance, so `userRewardPerTokenPaid` is synced to the reward-per-token value *before* the upcoming reward injection, and `user.amount` becomes `A` (plus any prior stake `S`).
2. The reward manager calls `queueNewRewards(R, rewardToken)` (a routine, legitimate operation, not requiring any special trust from the attacker to trigger — the attacker only needs to time their tx around this normal, periodic call). `rewardPerTokenStored` increases by `delta = R * 10**decimals / (S + A)`, since `totalStaked()` now includes the attacker's flash-loaned deposit.
3. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw(stakingToken, A)`. `_harvestBaseRewarder`/`getReward` computes `earned = balanceOf(attacker) * delta / 10**decimals`, using `balanceOf` = `S + A` (still full, since the harvest happens before `user.amount` is decremented). The attacker's harvested share is `(S+A) * R / (S+A) ≈ R` as `A → ∞` (bounded by the fraction of the pool they control at that instant), i.e. the attacker can claim close to the entire newly queued reward `R` despite holding the stake for a single block.
4. Attacker repays the flash loan, keeping the harvested reward tokens as pure profit, and this is repeatable every time rewards are queued.

Existing protections do not stop this: `nonReentrant`/`whenNotPaused` only guard against reentrancy/pausing, not sequential same-block sandwiching; there is no minimum staking duration, cooldown, or time-weighted-average balance used in `rewardPerToken` accrual or `balanceOf`; and the harvest-before-mutate ordering in both `_deposit` and `_withdraw` means the momentarily inflated balance is exactly what gets rewarded.

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate long-term stakers: each `queueNewRewards` call's reward pool can be siphoned almost entirely by a flash-loan-funded, single-block staker, diluting or eliminating the rewards long-term stakers should have received for that distribution. This matches the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield" / "direct theft of user funds."

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker needs no privileged role — only the ability to flash-loan or otherwise momentarily acquire a large amount of `stakingToken`, call the public `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw`, and time their transactions around an observable, periodic `queueNewRewards` call (a routine operation, visible in the mempool or predictable from off-chain reward schedules). This is feasible for any LP-style `stakingToken` with available liquidity/flash-loan sources, and the attack is fully repeatable for every future reward injection.

### Recommendation
Base reward accrual on a time-weighted/checkpointed balance rather than the instantaneous live balance: track locally staked balances and timestamps within the rewarder (or require a minimum holding/cooldown period before a deposit counts toward `rewardPerToken` accrual), and/or update reward checkpoints for all accounts based on stake duration rather than point-in-time balance queries to `MasterMagpie.stakingInfo`. Alternatively, disallow harvesting rewards accrued from a `queueNewRewards` event for stakes that were deposited after the previous reward checkpoint until at least one full accrual cycle has elapsed.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test outline:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, `BaseRewardPoolV2`, and a mintable LP `stakingToken`; set up a long-term staker LS with stake `S` held for many blocks with no interaction.
2. Simulate attacker: mint/flashloan large amount `A >> S` of `stakingToken` to attacker.
3. Attacker calls `masterMagpie.deposit(stakingToken, A)`.
4. Reward manager calls `rewarder.queueNewRewards(R, rewardToken)` in the same block.
5. Attacker calls `masterMagpie.withdraw(stakingToken, A)` in the same block, triggering `_harvestBaseRewarder` (or subsequently `multiclaim`) to transfer rewards.
6. Assert: `rewardToken.balanceOf(attacker)` after withdrawal is close to `R` (e.g. `>= 0.9 * R` for `A = 100*S`), while `rewarder.earned(LS, rewardToken)` for the long-term staker is far below their time-weighted proportional share `R * S/(S+A)` they should be entitled to had accrual been time-weighted, demonstrating disproportionate reward capture relative to holding period.
