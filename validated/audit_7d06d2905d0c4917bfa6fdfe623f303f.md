This confirms the vulnerability: `mWomSV.getRewardablePercentWAD()` exists and correctly computes a partial-forfeiture percentage based on cooldown state [1](#0-0) , exactly mirroring the mechanism used by `vlMGP.getRewardablePercentWAD` in `vlMGPBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` [2](#0-1) . However, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` never calls this function — it hardcodes `rewardableAmount = _amount`, making the forfeit check dead code and `forfeitAmount` always zero [3](#0-2) .

### Title
mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit never applies cooldown-based reward forfeiture, letting unlocking users claim 100% of pending rewards - (File: rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol)

### Summary
`mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` (lines 385-398) sets `rewardableAmount = _amount` unconditionally instead of scaling it by the user's rewardable percentage (as computed by `mWomSV.getRewardablePercentWAD`), so `forfeitAmount` is always `0`. Any mWomSV holder can call `startUnlock()` right after rewards accrue and then `getReward()`/`getRewards()` via MasterMagpie to receive their full pro-rata share with zero forfeiture, even though the sibling `vlMGPBaseRewarder` implementation intentionally forfeits a portion of rewards for users mid-unlock to redistribute to fully-locked stakers.

### Finding Description
`mWomSV` exposes `getRewardablePercentWAD(address _user)` which returns a percentage `<100%` for accounts with tokens in cooldown or partially unlocked, precisely to support proportional reward forfeiture [1](#0-0) . The analogous `vlMGPBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` correctly consumes this signal: `rewardableAmount = _amount * vlMGP.getRewardablePercentWAD(_account) / 1e18` [4](#0-3) .

In contrast, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder._calExpireForfeit` never calls `mWOMSV.getRewardablePercentWAD(_account)` at all; it just sets `rewardableAmount = _amount`, making the subsequent `if (rewardableAmount > _amount) revert` and `forfeitAmount = _amount - rewardableAmount` dead/no-op code that always yields `forfeitAmount = 0` [3](#0-2) . This function is reached from `_sendReward`, which is invoked by `getReward`/`getRewards`, both callable by any account through `MasterMagpie` on behalf of the user [5](#0-4) . Nothing else in `getReward`, `updateReward`, or `_updateFor` recomputes or enforces forfeiture — the reward-index/accounting machinery is otherwise identical to `vlMGPBaseRewarder` and does not compensate for the missing scaling.

An attacker (or any normal user) can therefore: lock mWOM into `mWomSV` via `lock()`, wait for/trigger a reward accrual (`queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` from a manager, or organically from protocol operations), call `mWomSV.startUnlock()` for the full locked amount, and immediately call `getReward()`/`getRewards()` through MasterMagpie to receive 100% of the accrued reward with `forfeitAmount == 0`, despite being fully in cooldown. Reward funds that should have been redistributed to still-locked stakers (via `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`) are instead paid out to the unlocking user, permanently misallocating that portion of yield.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft/misallocation of yield: reward tokens intended (per protocol design as implemented for vlMGP) to be partially forfeited by unlocking users and redistributed to remaining lockers are instead paid out in full. This constitutes theft of unclaimed yield from other stakers and breaks the reward conservation invariant, matching the Immunefi "theft of unclaimed yield" / "protocol insolvency (partial)" impact category. The loss is proportional to reward volume flowing through mWomSV rewarders and repeats every reward cycle for any user willing to unlock.

### Likelihood Explanation
No privileged role is required — any holder of mWOM (freely purchasable) can lock, then call the public `startUnlock()` and `getReward()`/`getRewards()` paths. The exploit requires no flash loans, no reentrancy, and is fully repeatable on every reward distribution cycle, making it highly likely to be exploited (or simply to silently degrade the intended reward-forfeiture mechanism) once discovered.

### Recommendation
Fix `_calExpireForfeit` in `mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol` to mirror `vlMGPBaseRewarder`'s logic: `uint256 rewardableAmount = _amount * mWOMSV.getRewardablePercentWAD(_account) / 1e18;` before the `if (rewardableAmount > _amount) revert` check, so forfeiture is actually computed based on the user's cooldown/unlock status returned by `mWomSV.getRewardablePercentWAD`.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `mWomSV`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, `MasterMagpie`, and a reward token; register the rewarder in MasterMagpie.
2. Two users, Alice and Bob, each `lock()` equal amounts of mWOM into `mWomSV`.
3. Manager calls `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` to accrue a fixed reward amount `R` to the rewarder.
4. Bob immediately calls `mWomSV.startUnlock(fullAmount)`, putting 100% of his stake into cooldown.
5. Both Alice and Bob call `getReward()` (via MasterMagpie) to claim.
6. Assert: `calExpireForfeit(bob, rewardToken) == 0` despite `getRewardablePercentWAD(bob) < 1e18`; Bob's `RewardPaid` amount equals the full pro-rata share (same as Alice's), and `forfeitAmount` emitted is `0`.
7. Assert the sum of amounts paid to Alice + Bob equals the entire `R` distributed, i.e., no reward was queued back via `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`/`ForfeitRewardAdded`, showing full extraction of yield that should have been partially forfeited and redistributed, confirming the conservation violation.

        uint256 fullyInLock = getUserTotalLocked(_user);

                }

        }
