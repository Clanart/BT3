### Title
Reward Sniping via Front-Running `queueNewRewards` Lets Attackers Steal Freshly Distributed Rewards From Long-Term Stakers - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool` distributes newly queued rewards instantly and entirely into `rewardPerTokenStored` (no time-vesting/streaming), and reward entitlement is based purely on the staker's balance at the moment of accrual, with no minimum holding period or withdrawal cooldown. An unprivileged attacker can front-run a manager's `queueNewRewards()` call by depositing a large stake immediately before it, then withdraw immediately after claiming, capturing a share of the reward proportional to their balance despite holding it for a negligible amount of time, diluting the rewards genuine long-term stakers should have received.

### Finding Description
The premise in the question that `_updateFor` "loses" prior accrual by overwriting `userRewards` is incorrect — `earned()` explicitly adds the prior `userRewards[_rewardToken][_account]` to the new delta [1](#0-0) , so previously accrued rewards are preserved, not lost. Additionally, `MasterMagpie._deposit` calls `_harvestBaseRewarder` (which triggers `rewarder.updateFor(_account)` → `_updateFor`) *before* `user.amount` is increased [2](#0-1) , so a deposit correctly checkpoints earned rewards using the pre-deposit balance for the delta since the last checkpoint — an attacker cannot retroactively apply their new, larger balance to reward-per-token deltas that occurred *before* their deposit.

However, a distinct, real vulnerability exists: `_provisionReward` applies the entire newly queued reward amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` instantly, in a single step, with no time-based streaming/vesting [3](#0-2) . Because `earned()` computes reward purely as `balanceOf(_account) * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)` [1](#0-0) , and there is no minimum staking duration or withdrawal cooldown in `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` [4](#0-3) , an attacker can:
1. Monitor the mempool for the manager's `queueNewRewards()` transaction (an `onlyManager`-gated call, but visible pre-confirmation to anyone).
2. Front-run it with a large `deposit()` into the same staking pool, immediately increasing their `balanceOf` in `MasterMagpie`.
3. Let `queueNewRewards()` execute, which bumps `rewardPerTokenStored` for the *entire pool* based on total staked (now inflated by the attacker's front-run deposit), instantly making a large `earned()` amount available to anyone holding balance at that moment, proportionally.
4. Call `getReward()` to claim their disproportionate share of the newly queued reward.
5. Immediately `withdraw()` their stake, having held it for only 1-2 blocks.

This dilutes the share that genuine long-term stakers, who held their balance through the entire prior accrual period, receive from that reward drop, since the attacker's newly added balance is factored into `rewardPerTokenStored`'s per-token denominator (`this.totalStaked()`), lowering the per-token amount, while also directly siphoning off reward proportional to their inflated balance at claim time.

### Impact Explanation
This falls under theft of unclaimed yield / direct economic loss to long-term stakers (Immunefi "Theft of unclaimed yield" / "Direct theft of user funds"): every `queueNewRewards()` call is an opportunity for a capitalized, unprivileged attacker to extract value proportional to a large, momentarily-held balance rather than genuine time-weighted stake, permanently reducing the reward pool available to long-term stakers.

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitability requires: (1) capital to acquire a large stake in the relevant `stakingToken` (MGP, mWOM, vlMGP, or LP tokens depending on pool) temporarily, (2) ability to front-run the manager's `queueNewRewards()` transaction, which is realistic on public mempools (or via searcher/MEV infrastructure), and (3) the reward pool must have relatively low total staked so a moderate deposit meaningfully shifts the reward share. This is repeatable on every `queueNewRewards()` call and does not require any privileged role, matching the "unprivileged attacker + front-running" threat model allowed by the rules.

### Recommendation
Stream/vest newly queued rewards over a fixed duration (e.g., Synthetix-style `rewardRate` over `rewardsDuration`) instead of applying the full amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single block, and/or introduce a minimum staking duration or withdrawal cooldown so a stake must be held for a non-trivial period before it can earn/claim a share of newly queued rewards.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry/Hardhat test plan:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, a `BaseRewardPool` for a staking token, and register a manager.
2. Have "long-term staker" Alice deposit `100` staking tokens and let time pass.
3. Have attacker Bob prepare a `deposit(stakingToken, 10000)` transaction (unconfirmed).
4. Simulate manager calling `queueNewRewards(reward, rewardToken)` — but have Bob's deposit mined in the block immediately prior (front-run).
5. After `queueNewRewards` executes, have Bob call `getReward(bob, bob)` and then `withdraw(stakingToken, 10000)`.
6. Assert Bob receives a reward amount `≈ reward * 10000/(100+10000)`, despite holding the stake for 1 block, while Alice's realizable share of that same `reward` amount (checked via her subsequent `getReward`) is reduced compared to a control scenario where Bob never deposited, demonstrating dilution of Alice's rightful reward.

```text
    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
```
