### Title
Attacker Can Front-Run Reward Distribution to Dilute Other Stakers' Rewards - ([File: rewards/MasterMagpie.sol], [File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol], [File: rewards/mWOMSVBaseRewarder.sol], [File: rewards/vlMGPBaseRewarder.sol])

### Summary
The reward accounting used by `BaseRewardPoolV2`, `mWOMSVBaseRewarder`, `vlMGPBaseRewarder` and `DelegateVoteRewardPool` distributes incoming rewards as an instantaneous lump-sum increase to `rewardPerTokenStored`, proportional to the pool's *current* `totalStaked()` at the moment `queueNewRewards()`/`_provisionReward()`/`_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer()` runs. Because `MasterMagpie.deposit()`/`withdraw()` (and pool-helper `depositFor`/`withdrawFor`) have no lock, cooldown, or entry/exit penalty, an attacker can mempool-watch for a reward-injecting transaction (e.g. `WombatStaking.harvest()` → `_toMasterWomAndSendReward()` → rewarder `queueNewRewards`, or a direct `donateRewards`/manager call), front-run it with a large `deposit()`, and immediately `withdraw()` right after the reward lands — capturing a disproportionate share of the reward while diluting genuine long-term stakers, at only gas cost.

### Finding Description
`BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward()` updates `rewardPerTokenStored` using the pool's `totalStaked()` at call time: [1](#0-0) 

The same unconditioned, instantaneous-lump-sum pattern exists in `mWOMSVBaseRewarder._provisionReward`/`_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`: [2](#0-1) 

and in `vlMGPBaseRewarder`: [3](#0-2) 

Deposits and withdrawals into the pools that back these rewarders' `totalStaked()`/`balanceOf()` are unrestricted — `MasterMagpie.deposit()`/`withdraw()` have no lock period, minimum staking duration, or exit fee: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

The reward injection itself is externally triggerable/observable: `WombatStaking.harvest()`/`_toMasterWomAndSendReward()` sends newly harvested rewards to the rewarder's `queueNewRewards`, and this call (or any `donateRewards`/manager call) is visible in the mempool before it lands: [6](#0-5) 

Because reward accrual is not streamed over time (no `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` vesting as in a standard Synthetix `StakingRewards`), an attacker's stake immediately prior to the reward-injection transaction is worth exactly as much per unit of stake as tokens that have been staked for a long time, letting the attacker walk away with a slice of the reward for effectively zero holding period.

### Impact Explanation
Existing long-term stakers of the affected pools receive a permanently smaller share of distributed rewards each time this is exploited, since the attacker's flash-stake inflates `totalStaked()` at the exact moment `rewardPerTokenStored` is bumped, then exits before contributing to future emissions. This is a direct theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate depositors, redirected to the attacker, repeatable for every future reward distribution and requiring only capital for the duration of one transaction bundle plus gas.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires no privileged role — only an ordinary wallet capable of monitoring the mempool and executing `deposit()`/`withdraw()` (or the pool-helper equivalents) around a `harvest()`/`queueNewRewards()`/`donateRewards()` call. Since reward-injection transactions are periodic and mempool-visible, and MasterMagpie deposits/withdrawals have no lock, the attack is straightforward to script and repeat whenever reward batches are sufficiently large relative to gas costs.

### Recommendation
Stream rewards over time (e.g., a `rewardRate` distributed linearly over a fixed duration, Synthetix-style) instead of applying them as an instantaneous lump sum against `totalStaked()` at the injection block, and/or introduce a minimum staking duration or withdrawal cooldown in `MasterMagpie` so that stake weight used for reward-per-token calculations reflects time-weighted participation rather than instantaneous balance at the moment of distribution.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker monitors the mempool for a `WombatStaking.harvest()` (or manager `queueNewRewards()`/`donateRewards()`) call that will inject a large reward batch into a `BaseRewardPoolV2`/`mWOMSVBaseRewarder`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder` instance.
2. Attacker front-runs it with `MasterMagpie.deposit(_stakingToken, largeAmount)`, instantly maximizing their share of `totalStaked()`.
3. The reward transaction executes, calling `_provisionReward`/`queueNewRewards`, which increases `rewardPerTokenStored` proportional to the (now attacker-inflated) `totalStaked()`.
4. Attacker immediately calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw(_stakingToken, largeAmount)` — since there is no lock or exit penalty, they fully exit and harvest their disproportionate share of the just-added reward via `getReward`/`getRewards`, at the expense of pre-existing stakers.

        if (!_isVlMgp)

    }

    function _harvestAndUnstake(address _stakingToken, address _account, uint256 _amount, bool _isVlMgp) internal {
        updatePool(_stakingToken);

        UserInfo storage user = userInfo[_stakingToken][_account];

        if (!_isVlMgp && user.available < _amount)
            revert WithdrawAmountExceedsStaked();
        else if(user.amount < _amount && _isVlMgp)
            revert UnlockAmountExceedsLocked();
        
        _harvestMGP(_stakingToken, _account);
        _harvestBaseRewarder(_stakingToken, _account);

        user.amount = user.amount - _amount;
        
        if(!_isVlMgp)
            user.available = user.available - _amount;
        user.rewardDebt = (user.amount * tokenToPoolInfo[_stakingToken].accMGPPerShare) / 1e12;
    }
