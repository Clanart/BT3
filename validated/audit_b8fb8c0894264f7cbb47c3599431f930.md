### Title
Front-running `castVotes()` with a last-second `vote()` lets an attacker steal a disproportionate share of a just-harvested WOM bribe from `BribeRewardPool`, diluting long-term voters - (File: rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol)

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager.vote()` calls `BribeRewardPool.stakeFor()` synchronously, immediately crediting the caller's vlMGP balance and `totalSupply` before any bribe has been harvested. Because `BribeRewardPool`'s reward accounting (`BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward`) distributes newly queued rewards purely as `amount / totalStaked()` at the instant `queueNewRewards()` is called (via `WombatStaking.vote()` inside `castVotes()`), an attacker can front-run a pending `castVotes()` transaction with a large `vote()` to inflate `totalStaked()` just before the bribe is queued, capture a share of that reward proportional to their instantaneous (not time-weighted) balance, and immediately `unvote()` to withdraw the stake plus the inflated reward.

### Finding Description
The exploit chain:

1. `WombatBribeManager.vote()` immediately calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` for any positive delta: [1](#0-0) 
2. `BribeRewardPool.stakeFor` runs the `updateRewards` modifier (checkpointing the caller's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at the *current* `rewardPerTokenStored`, i.e. before any new reward is added) and then instantly increases `totalSupply` and `_balances[_for]`: [2](#0-1) 
3. When `castVotes()` runs (triggerable by anyone, public mempool-visible), it calls `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, which harvests the pool's pending bribe and forwards it via `queueNewRewards`: [3](#0-2) 
4. `_provisionReward` distributes the harvested amount strictly by current `totalStaked()`, with no time-weighting of who contributed to earning it: [4](#0-3) 
5. Because the attacker's balance was inflated in step 2 (checkpoint already reset to the pre-reward `rewardPerTokenStored`), the attacker earns `(attackerBalance / totalStaked) * newReward` for a bribe amount that was actually accrued on Wombat's side based on the *previous* vote weight, not the attacker's just-submitted delta.
6. The attacker calls `WombatBribeManager.unvote()`, which calls `withdrawFor(msg.sender, currentVote, true)`, immediately claiming the disproportionate reward and withdrawing the stake: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

No mechanism prevents this: there is no cooldown between `vote()`/`unvote()`, no minimum holding period before a newly staked balance can earn a queued reward, and no snapshot/checkpoint of "eligible" balance taken prior to the bribe harvest. The `updateRewards` modifier only protects against double-counting existing accrued rewards; it does not protect other stakers from dilution by a balance added in the same block as (or transaction immediately preceding) a `queueNewRewards()` call.

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct theft of unclaimed yield from other, genuine long-term voters of the pool: their pro-rata share of the harvested bribe is diluted by the attacker's flash-added balance, and the attacker withdraws immediately without having contributed time-weighted voting power that earned the bribe. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" / "direct theft of user funds" Immunefi impact classes and violates the reward-conservation invariant (rewards should be split according to time-weighted contribution, not instantaneous balance at harvest time).

### Likelihood Explanation
The attack requires only: (1) holding/locking vlMGP so the attacker is entitled to call `vote()`/`unvote()` — a normal, unprivileged user action; (2) monitoring the mempool for a pending `castVotes()` call, which is a public, permissionless function anyone can call (bots regularly call it for the caller fee); (3) submitting a `vote()` with higher gas/priority to land immediately before it, and an `unvote()` immediately after — both standard front-running/back-running techniques requiring no special privileges. This is fully repeatable every time a bribe is about to be harvested for a pool with meaningful pending rewards, and profitability scales with the size of the harvested bribe relative to the pool's existing `totalStaked()`, which is maximized exactly in the "sole/near-sole active voter" scenario described.

### Recommendation
Decouple "voting power" (used for `castVotes` targeting) from "reward-eligible balance" (used for `rewardPerToken` distribution). Options:
- Snapshot/checkpoint each user's reward-eligible balance and have `queueNewRewards` use a balance snapshot taken before the current transaction's `vote()` calls, or require newly staked balance to only become reward-eligible starting from the next `castVotes()` epoch (i.e., delay stakeFor's effect for the current harvest).
- Alternatively, enforce a minimum lock/holding duration between `vote()` and `unvote()` (e.g., must remain staked at least one full cast-vote cycle) so that a single-block flash vote/unvote cannot capture freshly queued rewards.
- Consider moving the `stakeFor` effect for reward-accounting purposes so it only starts influencing `rewardPerToken` after the reward that's currently in-flight in `castVotes()` has been queued (do harvest before applying new deltas rather than in the same external call sequence).

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `BaseRewardPoolV2`/`BribeRewardPool` setup mirroring `WombatBribeManager` + `WombatStaking` + mock `voter`/`veWom`/Wombat bribe contract.
2. Set up two voters: `victim` who votes early and holds their vote for many blocks (simulating long-term contribution), and `attacker` who holds vlMGP but has not voted yet.
3. Simulate a pending bribe amount accrued in the mock Wombat bribe contract for the target pool (representing rewards earned by `victim`'s long-standing vote weight).
4. Have a third party submit `castVotes()`. Before it lands, have `attacker` call `vote([lp], [largeDelta])`, front-running it (assert `BribeRewardPool.balanceOf(attacker)` increases and `totalSupply` inflates).
5. Execute `castVotes()`, which triggers `queueNewRewards` on the pool's `BribeRewardPool`; assert `rewardPerTokenStored` update splits the newly queued bribe using the post-front-run `totalStaked()`.
6. Have `attacker` immediately call `unvote(lp)`; assert `attacker` receives `earned(attacker, WOM) > 0` despite having voted for 0 blocks/epochs prior to the bribe being generated.
7. Compute `victim`'s time-weighted share of the vote vs. actual `earned(victim, WOM)` after the same bribe distribution, and assert `attacker`'s realized reward share vastly exceeds their time-weighted vlMGP contribution (e.g., attacker holds vote for 1 block but claims >50% of the bribe that accrued over many blocks of `victim`'s exclusive voting).
