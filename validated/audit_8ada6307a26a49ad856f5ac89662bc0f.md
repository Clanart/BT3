### Title
Rewards accrued while self-custody become unclaimable by the owner and are instead forwarded to a joined pool - ([File: chia/pools/plotnft_drivers.py])

### Summary
`PlotNFT.claim_pool_rewards()` gates on the *current* pooling state of the singleton (`self.pooling`), not on whether a given unclaimed reward coin was earned before or after the pool was joined. Because the p2-singleton reward address (`reward_puzhash()`) is fixed for the lifetime of the launcher ID and does not change when the owner transitions from self-custody to pooling, any reward coin that was farmed while self-custodying but left unclaimed at the time `join_pool` is executed becomes permanently routed through `forward_pool_reward()` instead of `claim_pool_rewards()` — sending the full reward amount to the pool's `pool_puzzle_hash` rather than to the owner.

### Finding Description
`PlotNFTPuzzle.reward_puzhash()` derives the p2-singleton reward address purely from `launcher_id`, independent of pooling state: [1](#0-0) 

`claim_pool_rewards()` unconditionally rejects any claim while `self.pooling` is true: [2](#0-1) 

and the only alternative path, `forward_pool_reward()`, sends the reward's full value to the currently-configured pool (`pool_puzzle_hash`), not to the owner: [3](#0-2) [4](#0-3) 

Because these two functions branch solely on the singleton's *current* `pooling` flag rather than on the height/timing of the reward relative to the join-pool transition, a reward coin sent to the fixed `reward_puzhash()` while self-custodying, but left unspent when the owner subsequently calls `join_pool`, can no longer be claimed by the owner. This is directly confirmed by the test suite, which shows `claim_pool_rewards` raising `"Cannot claim rewards while pooling"` for any unclaimed reward once pooling is active, and the wallet-level test that explicitly exercises this exact "LOSE REWARDS while leaving/pooling" scenario: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 

At the wallet level, `PlotNFT2Wallet.claim_rewards()` simply pulls whatever reward coins are tracked in the store and calls `plotnft.claim_pool_rewards()`, which will throw if the PlotNFT singleton is currently pooling — with no distinction for rewards that predate the pool-join spend: [7](#0-6) 

This is the same root-cause class as the reported issue: a state transition (enrolling as courier / joining a pool) is checked only against present-tense state, silently reclassifying and redirecting value that was already earned by the user under the prior state, with no automatic claim/sweep performed as part of the transition.

### Impact Explanation
Rewards that were legitimately earned by a plot-NFT owner while self-custodying — but not yet swept before the owner decides to join a pool — are converted from "owner-claimable" to "must be forwarded to the pool." The user has no way to invoke `claim_pool_rewards()` on that coin once pooling is active; the only spend path (`forward_pool_reward`) sends the entire reward amount to the pool's `pool_puzzle_hash`, not back to the user. This is a direct, unauthorized loss/redirection of previously-earned XCH block rewards to a third party (the pool) with no user consent for that specific coin. This satisfies "concrete unsigned or unauthorized coin movement" / "reward redirection."

### Likelihood Explanation
This requires only ordinary user behavior reachable by any plot-NFT owner: farm at least one self-custody block reward, delay claiming it, then call the standard `join_pool` wallet RPC flow before sweeping. No malicious peer or privileged actor is needed — it is a normal wallet-user action sequence (`join_pool` then attempt `claim_rewards`), and the failure mode is deterministically reproduced by the existing test suite itself, indicating it is a structural (not edge-case) property of the current claim/forward gating logic.

### Recommendation
Track reward coins by the block height/tx at which they were farmed (self-custody vs. after pool-join), or automatically sweep/claim all outstanding self-custody reward coins as part of the `join_pool` transaction before the singleton transitions into the pooling state. Alternatively, allow `claim_pool_rewards()` to accept rewards whose farming height precedes the most recent join-pool transition height, independent of the singleton's current `pooling` flag.

### Proof of Concept
1. Create a plot-NFT and leave it self-custodying; farm at least one block reward to `reward_puzhash()` (owner-controlled p2-singleton address) — do not claim it.
2. Call `join_pool` to transition the singleton into `FARMING_TO_POOL` state.
3. Attempt `plotnft.claim_pool_rewards(rewards_to_claim=[<the pre-join reward>], ...)` — this raises `ValueError("Cannot claim rewards while pooling. If you're a pool, try 'forward_pool_rewards'")` per [8](#0-7) .
4. The only remaining spend path, `forward_pool_reward(reward)`, sends the full reward amount to `guaranteed_pool_config.pool_puzzle_hash` (the pool), not the owner, per [3](#0-2) . This is exercised verbatim in the "LOSE REWARDS" section of `test_plotnft_lifecycle`: [9](#0-8) , where the plotnft's balance is decreased by `POOL_REWARD_AMOUNT` with no corresponding credit to the owner.
