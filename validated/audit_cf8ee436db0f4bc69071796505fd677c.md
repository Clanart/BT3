### Title
Just-in-time vote-staking allows attackers to snipe bribe rewards accrued over the entire epoch, diluting long-term voters - ([File: rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol], [File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol])

### Summary
`BribeRewardPool` (via `BaseRewardPoolV2`) distributes bribe rewards as an instant lump-sum bump to `rewardPerTokenStored` inside `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`, rather than streaming rewards over time. Because `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` only snapshot `userRewardPerTokenPaid` at call time (standard Synthetix-style accounting) but `totalStaked()` is read live at the moment the lump sum is queued, any vlMGP holder can call `vote()` (or `voteAndCast()`) to inflate their staked balance in the pool's `_balances`/`totalSupply` an instant before `castVotes()` forwards the harvested bribe, capturing a pro-rata share of a bribe that accrued over the entire prior epoch despite having zero time-weighted exposure, then immediately `unvote()` to reclaim their vlMGP.

### Finding Description
`WombatBribeManager.vote()` calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` [1](#0-0)  which increases `totalSupply` and `_balances[_for]` in `BribeRewardPool` immediately, after only snapshotting `userRewardPerTokenPaid` to the pre-stake `rewardPerTokenStored` via the `updateRewards` modifier [2](#0-1) .

`WombatBribeManager.castVotes()` (or the atomic `voteAndCast()` which calls `vote()` then `castVotes()` in one transaction [3](#0-2) ) then triggers `wombatStaking.vote()`, which harvests Wombat-side bribes accumulated since `lastCastTime` and calls `IBaseRewardPool(_rewarders[i]).queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, ...)` [4](#0-3) .

Inside `_provisionReward`, the reward is divided by `totalStaked()` at that exact moment to bump `rewardPerTokenStored`: [5](#0-4) 

Since the attacker's `stakeFor` call already inflated `totalSupply` (i.e., `totalStaked()`) just before this queuing happens, and the attacker's `userRewardPerTokenPaid` was pinned to the pre-bump value, `earned()` immediately credits them: [6](#0-5) 

The attacker then calls `claimBribe()` → `_claimBribeFor` → `IBribeRewardPool(...).getReward(_for, _for)` [7](#0-6)  to withdraw the sniped reward, and `unvote()` to withdraw their full vlMGP stake back [8](#0-7) . No cooldown, minimum holding period, or time-weighting exists in `vote`/`unvote`/`stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` to prevent this — the reward-per-token model only prevents claiming rewards accrued strictly *before* the stake snapshot, but does not prevent capturing a share of a reward event that occurs at the instant of staking, because `totalStaked()` for the distribution divisor is read live rather than time-weighted.

### Impact Explanation
This results in theft of unclaimed yield from legitimate long-term voters: the bribe reward pool for an LP accrues real bribes over the entire period between `castVotes()` calls (potentially many days), but an attacker who stakes only for one block/transaction can claim a share proportional to `bigDelta / totalStaked()` of that whole accrued reward, diluting the share legitimately owed to voters who held their vote weight the entire period. This matches the "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" Immunefi impact category, since honest long-term voters permanently lose the diluted portion of the epoch's reward.

### Likelihood Explanation
- The attacker needs only vlMGP (locked MGP), obtainable permissionlessly by anyone who locks MGP — no protocol privilege required.
- The attack is atomic and repeatable every epoch via `voteAndCast()` in a single transaction (vote → castVotes), or by front-running a third party's `castVotes()` call since `lastCastTime`/pool state is fully observable on-chain.
- Capital is only needed transiently (borrowed vlMGP voting power is not flash-loanable since vlMGP requires locking, but an attacker who already holds a large vlMGP balance can repeat this against every pool/epoch at negligible marginal cost beyond gas).
- The magnitude of profit scales with the size of the bribe queued and the attacker's vlMGP relative to total staked in that specific `BribeRewardPool`, making it highly practical against pools with concentrated voting or large periodic bribes.

### Recommendation
Do not allow newly staked/voted balances to participate in a reward distribution that was accrued before the stake occurred. Options:
- Introduce a minimum vote-lock duration (e.g., require votes to remain for at least one full cast cycle before being eligible for that cycle's reward) before `stakeFor`'s balance counts toward `totalStaked()` for the purpose of the imminent `queueNewRewards` call.
- Snapshot `totalStaked()` at the start of the epoch (before any `vote()` calls in the same block) rather than reading it live at `queueNewRewards` time, so vote deltas submitted in the same transaction/block as `castVotes()` do not dilute the current distribution.
- Alternatively, stream rewards over time (rewardRate/duration model) instead of an instantaneous lump-sum bump to `rewardPerTokenStored`, which is the standard mitigation against this JIT-staking class of exploit.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager`, `WombatStaking`, `BribeRewardPool` for a pool `lp`, with a mock Wombat `voter`/bribe contract that returns a fixed large bribe amount on `vote()`.
2. Set up two vlMGP holders: `longTermVoter` votes `1000e18` for `lp` well before any cast, and lets one full epoch elapse with no further staking (simulate a bribe accrued via the mock bribe contract returning e.g. `100e18` reward tokens on the next `castVotes()` call).
3. Right before calling `castVotes()`, have `attacker` (who separately locked MGP to get `1000e18` vlMGP) call `vote(lp, +1000e18)` (or use `voteAndCast` to combine into a single tx) then call `castVotes()`.
4. Assert `attacker.earned(lp_rewardToken) == 50e18` (half of the 100e18 bribe, since attacker's balance now equals `longTermVoter`'s in `totalStaked()`), despite `attacker` having held the position for 0 seconds versus `longTermVoter`'s full epoch.
5. Have `attacker` call `claimBribe([lp])` then `unvote(lp)`, confirming they receive `50e18` reward tokens and fully withdraw their `1000e18` vlMGP.
6. Assert Conservation violation: `longTermVoter.earned(...)` is only `50e18` instead of the full `100e18` they would have received absent the attacker's JIT stake, proving dilution/theft of yield.
