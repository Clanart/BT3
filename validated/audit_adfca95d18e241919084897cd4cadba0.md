### Title
Just-in-time vote/unvote around bribe harvest allows theft of disproportionate bribe share from BribeRewardPool - (File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol, rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol, wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol)

### Summary
`BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` distributes new bribe rewards as an instantaneous lump-sum increment to `rewardPerTokenStored` proportional to `totalStaked()` at the exact moment `_provisionReward` runs, rather than accruing over time (unlike `WomUp.sol`'s Synthetix-style `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` model). Because `WombatBribeManager.vote()`/`unvote()` call `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` on the rewarder with no cooldown, minimum holding period, or epoch lock, a voter can inflate their staked vlMGP balance immediately before a bribe harvest/claim and withdraw immediately after, capturing a share of the newly injected reward proportional to their inflated balance instead of their actual time-weighted accrual.

### Finding Description
`BaseRewardPoolV2._provisionReward` (rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol:290-314) updates `rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored += _amountReward * 10**decimals / totalStaked()` at the moment new rewards are queued — a single snapshot based on `totalStaked()` at that instant, not a time-weighted rate. `_earned`/`earned` (rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol:177-184, 316-321) then compute a user's reward purely from the delta between the current `rewardPerToken` and the user's last-paid checkpoint multiplied by their current `balanceOf`, with no minimum-duration requirement.

`WombatBribeManager.vote()` (wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol:182-220) calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` synchronously, and `unvote()`/negative-delta `vote()` calls `withdrawFor(...)` synchronously (wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol:223-237). Neither function imposes any cooldown, epoch restriction, or minimum staking duration before a vote can be reversed. `BribeRewardPool.stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` (rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol:57-85) immediately mutate `totalSupply`/`_balances` and run `updateRewards`, which crystallizes `userRewards` using the current `rewardPerToken` before applying the balance change.

`castVotes()` and `harvestSinglePool()` (wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol:241-311) are both fully permissionless and trigger `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, which harvests pending bribes from Wombat's bribe contracts and forwards them into the pool's rewarder (feeding `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward`). `claimBribe`/`claimAllBribes` (wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol:326-375) call `IBribeRewardPool(rewarder).getReward(_for, _for)` to pay out `earned()` at the current `rewardPerToken`.

Since none of `vote`, `harvestSinglePool`/`castVotes`, `claimBribe`, and `unvote` are `nonReentrant`-guarded against each other or rate-limited, an attacker contract can chain, within a single atomic transaction: `vote(lp, +hugeDelta)` → `harvestSinglePool([lp])`/`castVotes(...)` (forces the pending bribe to be provisioned into `rewardPerTokenStored` while `totalStaked()` includes the attacker's inflated stake) → `claimBribe([lp])` (crystallizes and pays out a share proportional to the inflated `_balances[attacker]`) → `unvote(lp)` (removes the stake). This captures a share of the harvested bribe proportional to `hugeDelta` relative to the honest long-term voters' balances, despite having contributed that stake for zero meaningful duration.

### Impact Explanation
This is a theft of unclaimed bribe yield from honest long-term voters: the attacker's transient stake dilutes `totalStaked()` at the exact reward-injection moment, capturing bribe proceeds that should have accrued to voters who kept their vlMGP committed to that pool for the full period. This matches the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield."

### Likelihood Explanation
The precondition is that the attacker must actually hold real, previously-locked vlMGP (via `VLMGP.lock`/`lockFor`) equal to `hugeDelta` — vlMGP itself is not flash-loanable because unlocking requires `startUnlock` plus a cooldown period with an early-exit penalty (VLMGP.sol:275-300), so a true "flash-vote" using borrowed principal that must be returned same-block is not possible. However, unvoting a pool via `WombatBribeManager.unvote`/negative-delta `vote` does not require unlocking MGP at all — it only withdraws the vlMGP's vote allocation from the rewarder, which is immediate and reversible within the same transaction. Therefore any voter who already holds a sizeable locked vlMGP position (real capital, not flash capital) can repeatedly reallocate that same capital across pools right around each harvest/claim event to disproportionately capture newly harvested bribes, at the expense of continuously-committed voters — a repeatable strategy requiring only ordinary capital and gas, with no special privileges.

### Recommendation
Change `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` reward accounting to a time-weighted accrual model (e.g., Synthetix-style `rewardRate` over a fixed `duration`, as already used in `WomUp.sol`) instead of an instantaneous lump-sum `rewardPerTokenStored` bump based on `totalStaked()` at provisioning time. Alternatively, enforce a minimum staking duration or epoch-locked snapshot for vote weight (e.g., snapshot balances at the start of an epoch and only allow `claimBribe` against that pre-epoch snapshot) so that a vote/unvote occurring within the same epoch as a harvest cannot participate in that epoch's distribution.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `VLMGP`, `WombatBribeManager`, `BribeRewardPool` (via `BaseRewardPoolV2`), and mock Wombat voter/bribe contracts.
2. Add a pool with an active `BribeRewardPool` rewarder; have "Honest" user lock a moderate amount of MGP and `vote(lp, +amount)` well before any bribe harvest, staying staked throughout.
3. Fund the mock bribe contract with a bribe reward token to be harvested.
4. Have "Attacker" lock a large amount of MGP (`hugeDelta` >> Honest's stake) and, in one transaction via a helper contract, call: `vote(lp, +hugeDelta)` → `harvestSinglePool([lp])` (triggers `queueNewRewards`/`_provisionReward` on the rewarder) → `claimBribe([lp])` → `unvote(lp)`.
5. Assert: `attackerClaimedReward / hugeDelta` (per-unit reward captured) is comparable to or greater than `honestClaimedShare / honestStake`, despite the attacker having zero actual holding duration versus Honest's full-period stake — i.e., `attackerClaimedReward` exceeds what a time-weighted (duration-proportional) accrual model would have awarded, demonstrating the conservation-invariant violation.
