### Title
Cached deterministic FROST preprocess is re-served across divergent signing sets/messages, leaking the secret share - (File: coordinator/src/tributary/signing_protocol.rs)

### Summary
The RFQ bug class is "a committed/signed artifact cannot be invalidated and remains executable later under changed conditions." The Serai analog lives in `SigningProtocol::preprocess_internal`, which stores a deterministic `CachedPreprocess` seed keyed only by `context` and reuses it for every later call in that context — there is no mechanism to invalidate the cached preprocess once the surrounding signing conditions (participant set or signed message) change. `DkgConfirmer` calls `share_internal` once to produce a share and then again inside `complete`, each time re-deriving identical nonces from the cached seed; if the preprocess map or `key_pair` differs between the two derivations, the same nonces are signed under different FROST binding challenges, allowing third-party recovery of the validator's private key share.

### Finding Description
`AlgorithmSignMachine` nonces are fully deterministic: `seeded_preprocess` seeds `ChaCha20Rng` from `CachedPreprocess` and derives `Commitments::new` nonces plus the addendum from it (`crypto/frost/src/sign.rs:121-144`). `preprocess_internal` writes that seed to `CachedPreprocesses` on first use and reloads it on every subsequent call keyed by `context` (`coordinator/src/tributary/signing_protocol.rs:123-145`). `share_internal` calls `preprocess_internal` fresh each time (`signing_protocol.rs:156`), so every invocation of `share()` — and the implicit second invocation inside `DkgConfirmer::complete` (`signing_protocol.rs:321-324`) — reuses the exact same `(d, e)` nonce pair. The FROST signature share is `d + e·rho·lambda·c`, where the challenge `c` and binding factor `rho` bind the message and the entire included-preprocess set. `sign()` rebuilds the `included` set and `ThresholdView` from whatever `preprocesses` map is passed (`crypto/frost/src/sign.rs:290-313`). If `share()` is executed for one `(preprocesses, msg)` pair and `share()`/`complete()` is later executed for a different `(preprocesses, key_pair)` pair — e.g., a later accumulation of preprocesses yields a different `included` set, or `complete` is fed a superset or a different `key_pair` than `share` — two shares are published that use identical nonces but different `rho`/`c`. Solving the two linear equations recovers `d` and `e`, and hence the signer's FROST secret share. The cached seed is never invalidated between the two uses — exactly the "no revocation of a stale signed artifact" class.

### Impact Explanation
Recovery of a validator's MuSig/FROST private key share under the Ristretto group key used for `set_keys` confirmation signatures. A recovered key share lets the holder impersonate that share in all future sessions for the lifetime of the key, undermining the `t`-of-`n` confirmation of validator-set keys (`set_keys_message` is signed by `musig_key(set, signers)` per `substrate/validator-sets/pallet/src/lib.rs:1138-1141`).

### Likelihood Explanation
The trigger does not require key compromise: `share()` returns only the share while `complete()` re-runs `share_internal` with caller-supplied `preprocesses` and `key_pair`. Any divergence between the two calls' `included` sets or message — additional preprocesses arriving after the first `Ready` accumulation, a retried share with a different subset, or a distinct `key_pair` — deterministically reuses the nonces because the seed is cached by `context` alone and is never rotated or invalidated after a successful sign. The shares are broadcast publicly (`ProcessorMessage::SubstrateShare` / `DkgShares` on the Tributary), so any observer can perform the two-equation nonce recovery.

### Recommendation
Bind the cached preprocess to the full signing context it will be used for (e.g., key `CachedPreprocesses` by `(context, included_set, msg_hash)`) and refuse to reuse a seed whose stored binding differs, or burn/delete the cached seed the first time `share_internal` succeeds so `complete` must reconstruct the machine from a serialized state rather than re-deriving nonces. Alternatively, persist the `AlgorithmSignMachine` output of `share_internal` and reuse that single machine for `complete` instead of regenerating it from the seed.

### Proof of Concept
1. For a fixed `attempt`, `DkgConfirmer::share(preprocesses_A, key_pair)` is called; `preprocess_internal` loads the cached seed and `sign` emits share `s_1 = d + e·rho_A·lambda·c(msg_A, PP_A)` (`signing_protocol.rs:288-309`, `crypto/frost/src/sign.rs:121-141`).
2. Later, `DkgConfirmer::complete(preprocesses_B, key_pair', shares)` calls `share_internal` again with `preprocesses_B != preprocesses_A` (or a different `key_pair`); the same seed regenerates identical `d, e`, and a second share `s_2 = d + e·rho_B·lambda·c'` is effectively derivable/published (`signing_protocol.rs:312-327`).
3. An observer of the two public shares solves for `d, e` from the two equations (all coefficients `rho`, `lambda`, `c` are publicly recomputable), then recovers the signer's secret share from `s - (d + e·rho·c) = lambda·share_i·c`-style relation per FROST share structure — third-party recovery of the private key share that `CachedPreprocess` documentation explicitly warns reuse enables (`crypto/frost/src/sign.rs:84-92`).
