### Title
Instant lump-sum reward distribution in `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` combined with unlocked deposit/withdraw enables reward-sandwiching by short-term stakers - (File: `rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol`, `rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol`, `rewards/MasterMagpie.sol`)

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` distribute newly queued rewards to stakers all-at-once, in the same transaction they are provisioned, exactly like `Furnace#melt()` distributes accrued melt in one shot. Because `MasterMagpie` deposit/withdraw has no cooldown or lock, an unprivileged wallet can stake immediately before a reward-provisioning transaction and withdraw immediately after, capturing a share of the reward proportional only to its instantaneous balance rather than to time actually staked — diluting the yield genuine long-term stakers should have received. This mirrors report [M-15]'s pattern A2 (front-run the payout, hold briefly, redeem for outsized yield).

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` in `BaseRewardPool.sol` (and the identical logic in `BaseRewardPoolV2.sol`) updates `rewardPerTokenStored` for the *entire* incoming reward amount in a single step, based on whatever `totalStaked()` happens to be at that instant: [1](#0-0) 

This is called from `queueNewRewards` (restricted to `onlyManager`, e.g. `WombatStaking`/pool helpers relaying harvested WOM/MGP rewards) and from the fully permissionless `donateRewards`: [2](#0-1) 

`earned()` computes a user's share purely from `balanceOf(_account) * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid)`, i.e. instantaneous balance times the accumulated-per-token delta since the account last interacted — there is no time-weighting of how long the balance was actually held: [3](#0-2) 

Crucially, `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw` impose no lock-up, cooldown, or minimum holding period on the LP-side pools that route through these rewarders: [4](#0-3) 

An attacker can therefore: (1) call `MasterMagpie.deposit()` with a large stake right before a `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` call lands (either by front-running a predictable/mempool-visible harvest, or by using `donateRewards` themselves against a pool they just entered), (2) let the full reward amount be baked into `rewardPerTokenStored` against the now-inflated `totalStaked()` in their favor, and (3) call `MasterMagpie.withdraw()` in the same or next transaction to claim a disproportionate share and exit — exactly the "get a higher yield by holding for a short period" pattern described in the Furnace report, since Magpie's BaseRewardPool has no analog to a `rewardPeriod`/streaming rate at all (it is worse than the pre-fix Furnace: 100% of the reward vests instantly rather than over even a long period).

### Impact Explanation
Every legitimate staker who was in the pool before the attacker's flash-deposit has their pro-rata share of the newly queued reward permanently diluted, because `rewardPerTokenStored` is computed against `totalStaked()` at donation time, which the attacker artificially inflated with capital they intend to remove seconds later. This is a direct, repeatable transfer of yield from long-term LPs to short-term flash stakers — theft of unclaimed yield reachable from an ordinary wallet with no privileged role required.

### Likelihood Explanation
`queueNewRewards`/reward funding calls from `WombatStaking`/pool helpers are regular, recurring operations (harvest cycles), making timing predictable/front-runnable via mempool observation. `donateRewards` requires no permission at all, so an attacker can self-trigger the exploit deterministically without needing to front-run anyone, simply by depositing right before donating rewards to a pool they've just entered heavily, then withdrawing after `_updateFor` credits them. No cooldown, minimum stake duration, or streaming mechanism exists to prevent this in `MasterMagpie.sol` or `BaseRewardPool.sol`/`BaseRewardPoolV2.sol`.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted or streamed reward distribution (e.g., a `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` model, as used by Synthetix-style `StakingRewards`, or a short `rewardPeriod` similar to the Reserve fix) instead of crediting the entire donated amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single block. Alternatively, add a minimum staking duration / withdrawal cooldown in `MasterMagpie._withdraw` so capital cannot enter and exit within the same reward-provisioning window, and consider restricting or rate-limiting `donateRewards`.

### Proof of Concept
1. Attacker observes (or self-triggers via `donateRewards`) that a `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` call is about to add `R` reward tokens to a `BaseRewardPool` whose current `totalStaked()` is `S` (held by long-term LPs).
2. Attacker calls `MasterMagpie.deposit(stakingToken, X)` with a large `X`, becoming a staker with balance `X` and `userRewardPerTokenPaid == rewardPerTokenStored` (current, pre-reward value), per `_deposit` → `_harvestBaseRewarder` → `updateFor`. [5](#0-4) 
3. `queueNewRewards(R, rewardToken)`/`donateRewards(R, rewardToken)` executes, calling `_provisionReward`, which bumps `rewardPerTokenStored` by `R * 10**decimals / (S + X)` — instantly, for the whole `R`. [1](#0-0) 
4. Attacker immediately calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw(stakingToken, X)`; `_harvestAndUnstake` calls `getReward`, crediting the attacker `X * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid) / 10**decimals` — a full share of `R` proportional to `X/(S+X)` despite having staked for effectively zero time — then returns the principal `X`. [6](#0-5) [7](#0-6) 
5. Long-term stakers who held balance `S` throughout receive a smaller share of `R` than they would have absent the attacker's transient deposit, since the denominator `totalStaked()` was inflated only during the provisioning step.

    function _provisionReward(uint256 _amountReward, address _rewardToken) internal {
        IERC20(_rewardToken).safeTransferFrom(
            msg.sender,
            address(this),
            _amountReward
        );
        Reward storage rewardInfo = rewards[_rewardToken];
        rewardInfo.historicalRewards =
            rewardInfo.historicalRewards +
            _amountReward;
        if (this.totalStaked() == 0) {
            rewardInfo.queuedRewards += _amountReward;
        } else {
            if (rewardInfo.queuedRewards > 0) {
                _amountReward += rewardInfo.queuedRewards;
                rewardInfo.queuedRewards = 0;
            }
            rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored =
                rewardInfo.rewardPerTokenStored +
                (_amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals()) /
                this.totalStaked();
        }
        emit RewardAdded(_amountReward, _rewardToken);
    }
