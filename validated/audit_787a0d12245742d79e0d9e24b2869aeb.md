### Title
Instant stake-then-claim allows an attacker to capture a pro‑rata share of externally‑accrued bribes they never earned - (File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol, rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol)

### Summary
`WombatBribeManager.vote()` calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, delta)` which increases the caller's `_balances`/`totalSupply` in `BribeRewardPool` *before* any new bribe reward is queued for that pool. Because `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` uses an instantaneous `rewardPerTokenStored` bump model (no time‑weighted streaming/vesting like `periodFinish`/`rewardRate`), a user who stakes vote weight into a pool immediately before `castVotes()` harvests and queues that pool's bribe reward will receive a full pro‑rata share of a reward that accrued entirely before they held any vote weight.

### Finding Description
- `vote()` [1](#0-0)  calls `stakeFor`, which in `BribeRewardPool` snapshots the caller's rewards under `updateRewards` *using the pre‑increase balance* and then increases `_balances`/`totalSupply`: [2](#0-1) . This correctly zeroes out any historical reward for the new stake, but it also means the freshly added balance is now part of `totalSupply` for any bribe reward queued afterward.
- `castVotes()` [3](#0-2)  calls `wombatStaking.vote(...)`, which harvests Wombat bribes accrued since the previous cast and forwards/queues them into each pool's `BribeRewardPool`, bumping `rewardPerTokenStored` for the whole pool's current `totalSupply` — a lump‑sum, non‑time‑weighted update, since `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` has no per‑second reward rate or vesting period (`rewardPerToken` simply returns `rewards[_rewardToken].rewardPerTokenStored`) [4](#0-3) .
- `claimBribe()`/`_claimBribeFor()` then computes `earned()` as `balanceOf(account) * (rewardPerTokenStored - userRewardPerTokenPaid)` plus any prior unclaimed amount, and pays it out immediately via `getReward` [5](#0-4) .

Because the attacker's `balanceOf` at claim time is `X` (the freshly staked delta) and `rewardPerTokenStored` jumped due to a bribe that accrued during a period when the attacker held zero weight, the sequence `vote(lp,+X)` → `castVotesAndClaimBribes([lp], false)` → `vote(lp,-X)` lets the attacker collect a bribe share proportional to `X` for a period they were not present, diluting the fair share owed to voters who actually held weight during the whole accrual window. This is a "flash‑stake to skim a lump‑sum reward drop" pattern common to Synthetix‑style pools that lack a streaming/vesting reward rate; nothing in `stakeFor`, `withdrawFor`, or `castVotes`/`claimBribe` prevents staking and claiming within the same transaction, and there is no minimum holding period, snapshot, or `nonReentrant`/timelock guard blocking it.

Note: the "caster fee" portion forwarded by `_forwardRewards` in `castVotes()` is paid to `msg.sender` unconditionally as a gas incentive for calling `castVotes`, not based on vote stake, so that part is by design and not itself an exploitable theft; the exploit here is specifically the bribe-share dilution captured through `claimBribe`.

### Impact Explanation
This is theft of unclaimed yield: existing voters who held vote weight throughout the bribe-accrual epoch see their share of the harvested bribe diluted by an attacker who only held weight for a single block/transaction. The attacker extracts value (bribe tokens) with zero real economic exposure or time-weighted contribution, which matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class.

### Likelihood Explanation
The attacker only needs an existing vlMGP lock large enough to generate meaningful vote-weight delta (`getUserVotable` gate in `vote()`), and the ability to call three public functions (`vote`, `castVotesAndClaimBribes`, `vote`) atomically in one transaction — fully within reach of any unprivileged EOA/contract, and repeatable every time a pool has a non-trivial pending bribe to harvest. No special timing beyond normal transaction ordering/front-running is required since the whole exploit can be self-contained in one transaction the attacker submits.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted/streamed reward distribution in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` (e.g., a `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` vesting model as used elsewhere in the codebase for `vlMGPBaseRewarder`/`mWOMSVBaseRewarder`) instead of an instantaneous `rewardPerTokenStored` bump, so that newly staked balances cannot participate in rewards that accrued before the stake existed. Alternatively, snapshot eligible voters/balances at the start of each voting epoch (before `castVotes` harvests), and only allow `claimBribe` to pay out based on that pre-epoch snapshot, or enforce a minimum holding duration before a stake becomes eligible for the next bribe distribution.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `WombatBribeManager` with a mocked `wombatStaking.vote()` that simulates harvesting a fixed bribe amount `R` for pool `lp` on each call, and a `BribeRewardPool` for `lp`.
2. Have a "long-term" voter B stake `Y` vlMGP weight into `lp` well before the test's harvest window (simulate elapsed epochs with no claim).
3. In a single transaction from attacker A:
   - `vote([lp], [+X])` (stakes `X` into `BribeRewardPool`, `totalSupply` becomes `Y+X`)
   - `castVotesAndClaimBribes([lp], false)` (harvests `R`, bumps `rewardPerTokenStored` by `R*1e18/(Y+X)`, then `claimBribe` pays attacker `X * R / (Y+X)`)
   - `vote([lp], [-X])` (unstake back to zero)
4. Assert attacker A received `X*R/(Y+X) > 0` bribe tokens despite holding `lp` vote weight for zero blocks prior to the harvest.
5. Assert voter B's `earned()` for the same reward token is reduced to `Y*R/(Y+X)` instead of the full `R` they would have received absent the attacker's flash stake, demonstrating the diluted/stolen share.
