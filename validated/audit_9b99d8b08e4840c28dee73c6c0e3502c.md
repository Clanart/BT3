### Title
Owner (admin) can steal previously accrued `swapForGas` commission via `claimCommission()` after an ownership/config change - ([File: contracts/bindings/kip247/GaslessSwapRouter.go] — Solidity source referenced only via ABI/bytecode in this repo)

### Summary
The KIP-247 `GaslessSwapRouter` gasless-swap contract accrues a native-KAIA "commission" inside its own contract balance every time `swapForGas()` executes (see the `SwappedForGas` event emitting a `Commission` field), and exposes a single `claimCommission()` entrypoint that sweeps the *entire* contract balance to the current `owner()`. There is no bookkeeping that separates commission accrued before/after an ownership transfer or before/after a `updateCommissionRate()` change, and `transferOwnership()`/`renounceOwnership()` (OpenZeppelin `Ownable`) can move ownership to any address at any time. This is the same bug class as the reported `yieldDistributorAddress` issue: an admin-settable "fee/commission recipient" role can be reassigned, and the new holder of that role sweeps up *all* accumulated fees, including those that accrued while a different party held the role — with no delay, no snapshot, and no per-epoch accounting.

### Finding Description
`GaslessSwapRouter` is the KIP-247 gasless swap router used to let users pay gas in ERC-20 tokens: `swapForGas()` swaps the user's token for KAIA, repays the block proposer, and keeps a `commission` cut in the contract's own balance (as evidenced by the `SwappedForGas(proposer, amountRepaid, user, finalUserAmount, commission)` event) [1](#0-0) .

The only function that withdraws this accumulated commission is `claimCommission()`, which per the decompiled bytecode reads the contract's native balance and forwards it in full to the address stored in the owner slot, emitting `CommissionClaimed(amount)`: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

Ownership of the contract (and hence the right to call `claimCommission()` — it appears gated to `owner()` in the same way `updateCommissionRate`, `addToken`/`removeToken` are owner-only) can be reassigned at any time via the standard OpenZeppelin `Ownable` functions `transferOwnership()` and `renounceOwnership()`: [4](#0-3) 

Because `claimCommission()` sweeps the *whole* contract balance rather than a per-owner or per-epoch accrual, there is no mechanism preventing a newly appointed owner from claiming commission that accumulated entirely under a previous owner's tenure. This mirrors the reported `ExchangeProxy.setYieldDistributor` / `claimFees` issue: an admin-controlled "fee beneficiary" pointer (`owner` here, `yieldDistributorAddress` in the original report) can be repointed, and the new holder can immediately claim the entire fee pot with no protection for whoever should have received the fees that accrued under the old assignment.

### Impact Explanation
Any fee/commission accrued by `GaslessSwapRouter` from user gasless swaps is not durably attributed to the entity that earned/administered it during accrual. If ownership changes (intentionally via governance rotation, via a compromised owner key, or via `renounceOwnership` followed by a new deployment/owner scenario), the new owner can call `claimCommission()` and unilaterally redirect the entire accumulated commission balance to itself, effectively "stealing" fees that should have belonged to the protocol/treasury role active when the corresponding `swapForGas` transactions were executed. Since commission accrues continuously from ordinary user-submitted gasless transactions, the amount at risk grows unboundedly with usage before any claim, making this a direct value-movement/fee-theft vector consistent with unauthorized fee redirection in the gasless module.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is Medium: it requires an ownership change event (owner key rotation, planned governance handover, or a compromised owner key) to occur while unclaimed commission is outstanding — a plausible and even routine operational event for a system contract governed by an admin key, exactly the scenario called out in the original report. No unusual preconditions from unprivileged users are needed; the only trigger is a change of the `owner` pointer, a normal administrative action supported natively by the contract.

### Recommendation
- Snapshot/settle outstanding commission to the *current* owner before any `transferOwnership()`/`renounceOwnership()` call completes (e.g., override `_transferOwnership` to auto-claim pending commission to the outgoing owner first).
- Alternatively, maintain a per-owner claimable ledger (accrue commission to a mapping keyed by "owner at time of swap" rather than sweeping the live balance), so a new owner can only claim commission accrued during its own tenure.
- Add a timelock/delay on ownership transfer combined with a grace window during which the outgoing owner may still claim pre-existing commission.
- Emit a clear on-chain warning/require guarding `claimCommission()` if a pending ownership change is in flight, and document/enforce that owner rotation must be preceded by a commission claim.

### Proof of Concept
1. Owner A operates `GaslessSwapRouter`; over time, many users call `swapForGas()`, each accruing commission into the contract balance (visible via `SwappedForGas.Commission` events) — no claim is made yet, so the contract balance holds e.g. 100 KAIA of unclaimed commission.
2. Owner A calls `transferOwnership(B)` (or the owner key is compromised/rotated) — `Ownable` allows this with no check on outstanding balances [5](#0-4) .
3. New owner B immediately calls `claimCommission()`; the function sweeps the entire contract native balance (the full 100 KAIA, all accrued while A was owner) to B [6](#0-5) .
4. Owner A receives nothing for commission accrued during its own tenure — the fee is fully redirected to B with no ledger, delay, or protection.
