### Title
Unbounded sweep of contract's entire mWom balance into attacker's mWomSV lock in `_deposit` mode 2 - (File: wombat/ArbWomUp3.sol)

### Summary
`incentiveDeposit`'s mode-2 branch in `_deposit` locks `IERC20(mWom).balanceOf(address(this))` — the contract's *total* mWom balance — into the caller's `mWomSV` position instead of only the mWom amount produced by that caller's own deposit. Any mWom residing on the contract from a prior transaction (dust, rounding, or a non‑1:1 exchange on `IMWom.deposit`) is therefore swept into the next caller's own locked balance for free, and that inflated locked balance also feeds into the tiered MGP reward calculation used by `getRewardAmount`/`calDoubledCounted`.

### Finding Description
In `_deposit`, mode 2 does:
```
uint256 mWomBal = IERC20(mWom).balanceOf(address(this));
IERC20(mWom).safeApprove(address(mWomSV), mWomBal);
ILocker(mWomSV).lockFor(mWomBal, _account);
``` [1](#0-0) 

Instead of tracking only the mWom amount resulting from the current `_amount` (i.e., `toDeposit` plus whatever the `smartWomConvert.convert` call produced for this specific call), it queries the contract's whole token balance and locks all of it to `_account`. The comment on `bracketRewarded` — `mapping(address => uint) public bracketRewarded;   // not in use` — confirms that no per-call/per-account ledger is kept to reconcile what each caller actually contributed. `calDoubledCounted` separately reads `mWomSV.getUserTotalLocked(_account)` from the locker contract, so it will simply account for whatever ends up locked, including any swept residual, when computing tier-based rewards in `getRewardAmount`. [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

If a residual mWom balance is sitting on the contract from an earlier call (e.g., dust left by a previous mode 1/mode 3 deposit if `IMWom.deposit` is not perfectly 1:1, or any other reason the contract's mWom balance is nonzero before a new call), an attacker calling `incentiveDeposit(_amount, _convertRatio, false, 2)` with a small `_amount` gets the entire outstanding mWom balance — including funds not derived from their own deposit — locked under their own `mWomSV` position. This inflates their `mWomSV.getUserTotalLocked` balance, which both (a) hands them locked tokens they never paid for and (b) pushes them into higher reward tiers, causing `getRewardAmount` to mint them more vlMGP than their actual contribution warrants.

`nonReentrant` and `whenNotPaused` do not prevent this because the exploit does not require reentrancy or bypassing pause — it only requires a nonzero mWom balance to exist on the contract at the time of the call, which is a state, not an access-control, precondition.

### Impact Explanation
An attacker gains mWom tokens they did not pay for, locked to their own address via `mWomSV.lockFor`, and simultaneously gains additional vlMGP incentive rewards computed off the inflated locked balance. This is a direct theft of funds that belong to the protocol/other depositors, matching a Critical impact.

### Likelihood Explanation
Exploitability is gated entirely on the precondition that mWom is sitting on the contract balance rather than fully consumed by a prior call. Whether this reliably occurs depends on the exact behavior of the external `IMWom.deposit` and `IConverter.convert` implementations, which are not present in this repository (only interfaces are available), so it cannot be confirmed from this codebase alone whether such residual balances arise in practice under normal operation.

### Recommendation
Do not use `IERC20(mWom).balanceOf(address(this))` to determine the lock amount in mode 2. Instead, capture the mWom balance immediately before minting/converting for this specific call and compute the delta (`balanceAfter - balanceBefore`) to lock only the amount attributable to the current caller's `_amount`. Any pre-existing balance should be swept by a privileged admin function, not implicitly assigned to whichever user happens to call next.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan (requires mocking `IMWom` and `smartWomConvert` since only interfaces are available in-repo):
1. Deploy `ArbWomUp3` with mock `mWom`, `mWomSV` (mock `ILocker` recording `lockFor` calls and `getUserTotalLocked`), and mock `smartWomConvert`.
2. Simulate a residual by directly minting/transferring mWom tokens to the `ArbWomUp3` contract address (representing leftover from an earlier call) before any attacker interaction.
3. As attacker, call `incentiveDeposit(smallAmount, 0, false, 2)`.
4. Assert that `mWomSV.lockFor` was called with `mWomBal == residual + attacker's own converted/deposited mWom`, i.e., greater than what the attacker's `smallAmount` alone should produce.
5. Assert `mWomSV.getUserTotalLocked(attacker)` increased by more than the attacker's rightful share, and that the resulting `rewardToSend` (vlMGP minted) from `getRewardAmount` is correspondingly inflated versus a baseline run with zero residual.
