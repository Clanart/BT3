This confirms the analysis: `Airdrop2.sol` implements actual linear vesting math using `vestingPeriodCount` and `intervals` in `_getClaimable` [1](#0-0) , while `ArbitrumMWomAirdrop.sol` was clearly derived from it but its `_getClaimable` was reduced to `vested = totalAmount`, making the whole amount claimable at once and rendering `vestingPeriodCount`/`intervals` dead, unused state variables [2](#0-1) .

### Title
Full airdrop amount is claimable in a single transaction at `startVestingTime` instead of vesting linearly, allowing early claimants to drain the reward reserve and freeze funds for later claimants - (File: rewards/ArbitrumMWomAirdrop.sol)

### Summary
`_getClaimable` unconditionally sets `vested = totalAmount`, so any whitelisted address with a valid merkle proof can call `claim()` once at `t = startVestingTime` and immediately withdraw their entire allocation, even though `vestingPeriodCount` and `intervals` exist and imply the deployer intended gradual, linear vesting (as implemented correctly in the sibling contract `Airdrop2.sol`). If the contract is funded assuming a gradual release schedule (i.e., balance ≈ sum of allocations released over time, not all-at-once), early claimants can exhaust `reward.balanceOf(address(this))`, causing `safeTransfer`/`safeApprove` calls for later legitimate claimants to revert and their claims to be blocked until the owner tops up the contract.

### Finding Description
`claim()` requires only `block.timestamp >= startVestingTime` and a valid merkle proof [3](#0-2)  — there is no `nonReentrant`-adjacent per-period check, no `vestingPeriodCount` gating and no dependency on `intervals`. `_getClaimable` simply computes `vested = totalAmount` and returns `totalAmount - claimed` [2](#0-1) , unlike `Airdrop2.sol`'s `_getClaimable`, which computes `vested` as a function of elapsed time divided by `intervals` and `vestingPeriodCount` [4](#0-3) . Any unprivileged whitelisted claimant (an EOA with a valid merkle leaf `keccak256(account, totalAmount)`) can therefore call `claim(totalAmount, proof, false, false)` exactly once at `startVestingTime` and receive 100% of their allocation via `reward.safeTransfer(msg.sender, claimable)` [5](#0-4) . No existing modifier (`whenNotPaused`, `nonReentrant`) prevents this because they only guard against pausing/reentrancy, not against the missing time-based vesting math.

### Impact Explanation
If the contract's token balance is provisioned based on an assumption of gradual release (as `vestingPeriodCount`/`intervals` suggest was intended), first-claimants draining their full allocation immediately can leave insufficient `reward` balance for later legitimate claimants, causing their `claim()` calls to revert on `safeTransfer`/`safeApprove` due to insufficient balance — a freezing of unclaimed yield for those users until the owner performs a manual top-up. This matches Immunefi's "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" impact class for reward/vesting contracts.

### Likelihood Explanation
This requires no special capital, privileges, or timing tricks beyond being a member of the merkle tree (any of the addresses the airdrop was designed for) — it is 100% deterministic and repeatable by every claimant simply calling `claim()` at or after `startVestingTime`. This is not a griefing/edge-case bug; it is the contract's actual, deployed logic (as-is, without any admin misconfiguration), so likelihood is high for any deployment expecting linear vesting behavior.

### Recommendation
Fix `_getClaimable` in `ArbitrumMWomAirdrop.sol` to implement the same (or an equivalent) time-based vesting formula as `Airdrop2.sol`, using `vestingPeriodCount` and `intervals`, e.g.:
```solidity
uint256 vested = (totalAmount * 5 / 100) + (totalAmount * 95 / 100) * ((block.timestamp - startVestingTime) / intervals) / vestingPeriodCount;
```
and populate `vestingPeriodCount`/`intervals` in the constructor (they are currently never set, defaulting to 0, which would also cause a division-by-zero revert if the correct formula were naively copied without also fixing constructor initialization).

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `ArbitrumMWomAirdrop` with `reward` funded only with `totalAmountAlice + totalAmountBob` (simulating a reserve sized for gradual release, not simultaneous full payout, though in this case reserve happens to equal full amounts — instead fund only `totalAmountAlice` to demonstrate insufficiency).
2. Build merkle tree with two leaves: Alice (`totalAmountAlice`) and Bob (`totalAmountBob`); fund the contract with only `totalAmountAlice` worth of `reward` tokens (simulating partial/gradual funding expectation).
3. Warp to `startVestingTime`.
4. Call `claim(totalAmountAlice, aliceProof, false, false)` from Alice — assert `reward.balanceOf(alice) == totalAmountAlice` (100% received instantly, confirming no vesting).
5. Call `claim(totalAmountBob, bobProof, false, false)` from Bob — assert the call reverts (e.g., `ERC20: transfer amount exceeds balance`), demonstrating Bob's yield is frozen because Alice's early full claim drained the reserve.
