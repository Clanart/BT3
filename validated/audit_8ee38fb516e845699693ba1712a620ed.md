### Title
Instant, uncooldown reward crediting in `BaseRewardPool`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder` lets a flash-depositor front-run harvests to steal a disproportionate share of freshly-queued rewards - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol], [File: rewards/vlMGPBaseRewarder.sol], [File: rewards/MasterMagpie.sol], [File: wombat/WombatStaking.sol])

### Summary
The reported StRSR bug is a "front-run a lump-sum return of value into a pro-rata pool" issue: an attacker stakes right before a batch of value is credited to all current stakers, capturing a share of value he never contributed to earning, then exits. The same structural pattern exists in this protocol's reward-pool accounting: `_provisionReward` in `BaseRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2`/`vlMGPBaseRewarder` instantly bumps `rewardPerTokenStored` by `amount * 1e18 / totalStaked()` at the moment rewards (harvested WOM/bonus tokens, or forfeited vlMGP) are queued, with no vesting, streaming, or minimum holding period, and `MasterMagpie`/pool-helper deposits and withdrawals have no cooldown or lock for regular LP stakers.

### Finding Description
`_provisionReward` (called from `queueNewRewards`, `donateRewards`, and `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer`) immediately folds the newly-arrived reward amount into `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` at the instant of the call: [1](#0-0) 

This function is reached whenever `WombatStaking._sendRewards`/`_toMasterWomAndSendReward` harvests WOM/bonus rewards from Wombat and forwards them to the corresponding rewarder via `queueNewRewards`, and also whenever `vlMGPBaseRewarder.queueMGP`/`_sendReward` re-queues an expired-lock forfeit amount as a reward for remaining `vlMGP` holders: [2](#0-1) [3](#0-2) 

Because the reward is credited to `rewardPerTokenStored` as a function of `totalStaked()` at the exact moment of the transaction, and `MasterMagpie._deposit`/`_withdraw` (and the corresponding pool-helper `deposit`/`withdraw`, e.g. `WombatPoolHelperV2`) impose no cooldown, minimum staking duration, or exit fee for regular LP staking, a user can: [4](#0-3) [5](#0-4) 

1. Observe a pending harvest/reward-forwarding transaction (or a forfeit-driven re-queue) in the mempool.
2. Front-run it with a large `deposit`/`depositFor` into the same pool, instantly increasing their share of `totalStaked()` without having contributed to the accrual period.
3. Let the reward-queuing transaction land, which mints them a pro-rata share of `rewardPerTokenStored` for value they did not help generate.
4. Immediately `withdraw` and `getReward`, extracting the reward and exiting, since there is no lock on ordinary LP stake.

This mirrors the StRSR pattern exactly: value returned/added to a shared pool is distributed to whoever holds shares at settlement time rather than to those who held shares while the value was being generated, and the absence of any lock/streaming makes this atomically exploitable (deposit → reward lands → withdraw, possibly all across just two blocks by front-running the harvest tx).

### Impact Explanation
Legitimate long-term stakers have part of their harvested rewards siphoned by a flash-depositor who contributes capital only for the instant the reward is queued. This is a direct theft of yield that would otherwise accrue to existing stakers — matching the "theft or permanent freezing of unclaimed yield" impact bar, since every harvest cycle (which occurs on essentially every deposit/withdraw/vote due to `_toMasterWomAndSendReward`) is an opportunity for this attack, and it can be repeated indefinitely by any unprivileged wallet with capital, at no cost beyond gas.

### Likelihood Explanation
Likelihood is high: reward harvests happen very frequently (harvest is triggered by nearly every user deposit, withdrawal, and by `vote`), harvested amounts are visible/estimable, there is no deposit lock, and the attack requires only a plain wallet performing deposit/withdraw around a predictable/observable harvest transaction — no privileged role or governance action is needed.

### Recommendation
Introduce a minimum staking duration or a reward-vesting/streaming mechanism (rather than an instantaneous pro-rata credit) so that newly queued rewards accrue only to stakers who were staked during the period the rewards were generated, e.g., linearly stream new rewards over time (similar to a `periodFinish`/`rewardRate` streaming model) instead of updating `rewardPerTokenStored` in one lump sum, and/or impose a short cooldown on deposits before they count toward `rewardPerToken` accrual eligibility.

### Proof of Concept
1. Pool P has `totalStaked = 1000` LP and accrued no pending rewards yet.
2. WombatStaking is about to harvest and call `rewarder.queueNewRewards(100, WOM)` for pool P (visible in mempool as part of a user's deposit/withdraw tx that triggers `_toMasterWomAndSendReward`). [6](#0-5) 
3. Attacker front-runs with `depositFor`/`deposit` of 1000 LP into pool P via the pool helper, doubling `totalStaked` to 2000 right before the reward-queue tx lands.
4. `_provisionReward` executes: `rewardPerTokenStored += 100 * 1e18 / 2000`, crediting the attacker with 50% of the newly queued 100 WOM despite having staked for zero time during accrual. [1](#0-0) 
5. Attacker immediately calls `withdraw`/`withdrawFor` and `getReward`, exiting with ~50 WOM stolen from the original 1000 LP stakers who earned it. [7](#0-6)

    function _harvestAndUnstake(address _stakingToken, address _account, uint256 _amount, bool _isVlMgp) internal {
        updatePool(_stakingToken);

        UserInfo storage user = userInfo[_stakingToken][_account];

        if (!_isVlMgp && user.available < _amount)
            revert WithdrawAmountExceedsStaked();
        else if(user.amount < _amount && _isVlMgp)
            revert UnlockAmountExceedsLocked();
        
        _harvestMGP(_stakingToken, _account);
        _harvestBaseRewarder(_stakingToken, _account);

        user.amount = user.amount - _amount;
        
        if(!_isVlMgp)
            user.available = user.available - _amount;
        user.rewardDebt = (user.amount * tokenToPoolInfo[_stakingToken].accMGPPerShare) / 1e12;
    }
