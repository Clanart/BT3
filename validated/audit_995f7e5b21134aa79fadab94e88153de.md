### Title
Front-runnable instantaneous reward-per-token accounting in `BribeRewardPool`/`BaseRewardPoolV2` allows flash-vote reward sniping and dilution of existing stakers - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPoolV2.sol], [File: rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol], [File: wombat/WombatBribeManager.sol])

### Summary
`BribeRewardPool.stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` inherit `BaseRewardPoolV2`'s `updateRewards` modifier, which checkpoints a user's `_earned()` using the balance *before* the stake/withdraw amount is applied, and `queueNewRewards` distributes rewards instantly by dividing the injected amount by the pool's current `totalStaked()` with no time-weighting, streaming, or lockup. Because `WombatBribeManager.vote()` (stake), `castVotes()` (which triggers `queueNewRewards` via `WombatStaking.vote()`), `claimBribe()`, and `vote()` again (unstake) are all public/unprivileged and callable atomically by a single contract, an attacker can stake immediately before a bribe injection, claim a full pro-rata share, and withdraw immediately after, diluting all pre-existing stakers who were checkpointed before the attacker's temporary balance existed.

### Finding Description
- `BribeRewardPool.stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` are gated only by `onlyOperator` (the `WombatBribeManager`) and the `updateRewards(_for, rewardTokens)` modifier: [1](#0-0) 
- `updateRewards` in `BaseRewardPoolV2` computes `_earned()` using the pre-update `balanceOf(_account)`, then the caller mutates `totalSupply`/`_balances` after the modifier's pre-logic runs: [2](#0-1) 
- Rewards are injected instantly, not streamed over time: `_provisionReward` adds `_amountReward * 10**decimals / totalStaked()` directly to `rewardPerTokenStored` at the moment `queueNewRewards`/`donateRewards` is called, with no `periodFinish`/duration mechanism: [3](#0-2) 
- `WombatBribeManager.vote()` calls `IBribeRewardPool(pool.rewarder).stakeFor(msg.sender, uint256(delta))` for a positive delta and `withdrawFor` for a negative delta - fully permissionless, gated only by `pool.isActive`: [4](#0-3) 
- `castVotes()` is public and anyone can call it to trigger `wombatStaking.vote()`, which harvests the external Wombat bribe and calls `queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, rewardToken)` on the pool's rewarder in the same transaction: [5](#0-4) [6](#0-5) 
- `claimBribe()` is public and unprivileged, calling `getReward(_for, _for)` on the rewarder: [7](#0-6) 

Because none of `vote`, `castVotes`, `claimBribe` enforce a minimum holding period, block delay, or time-weighted share accounting, an attacker contract can atomically:
1. `vote(pool, +X)` → `stakeFor` increases attacker's balance and `totalSupply` with no prior contribution.
2. `castVotes()` (or piggyback on another user's call in the same block) → `queueNewRewards` divides the newly harvested bribe by the now-inflated `totalStaked()`, crediting the attacker a `X/totalStaked` pro-rata share of the entire distributed reward despite having staked for zero economically meaningful duration.
3. `claimBribe(pool)` → attacker collects the reward immediately.
4. `vote(pool, -X)` → `withdrawFor` removes the attacker's stake.

This dilutes all pre-existing stakers, whose `userRewardPerTokenPaid` was checkpointed prior to the attacker's temporary balance, since the newly distributed `rewardPerTokenStored` increment is shared across an inflated denominator that includes stake contributed for a single transaction only. Existing guards (`onlyOperator`, `updateRewards`) do not prevent this because they only gate *who* can call stake/withdraw (the `WombatBribeManager`, which itself is fully permissionless to any vlMGP holder) and correctly checkpoint balances - they do not address the instantaneous, non-time-weighted nature of `_provisionReward`.

### Impact Explanation
This is a direct theft-of-yield / dilution vulnerability: the attacker extracts a portion of bribe rewards that should have accrued only to genuine long-term voters, at the expense of pre-existing stakers' unclaimed yield. This matches the Immunefi impact class "theft of unclaimed yield" / "direct theft of user funds" (of reward tokens) from `BribeRewardPool`.

### Likelihood Explanation
Preconditions are modest: the attacker needs vlMGP votable balance (`getUserVotable`), which can be obtained by locking MGP (no special privilege). The whole attack (`vote`, `castVotes`, `claimBribe`, `vote` reverse) can be composed into a single attacker-controlled contract call, or split across closely-timed transactions/blocks, and is repeatable every time a bribe/reward injection occurs (i.e., every voting epoch harvest), making it a highly feasible and repeatable strategy with capital requirements limited to the vlMGP amount needed for a meaningful pro-rata share plus gas.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted reward accounting (e.g., streaming rewards over a fixed duration similar to Synthetix `StakingRewards`' `periodFinish`/`rewardRate` model) instead of crediting the full injected amount to `rewardPerTokenStored` in a single instant, and/or enforce a minimum vote-lock duration (cooldown) in `WombatBribeManager` before a user's `vote()` delta on a pool can be reversed, so that an attacker cannot open and close a position within the same or adjacent block/epoch around a `queueNewRewards` call.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `BribeRewardPool` (or `DelegateVoteRewardPool`) with a mock `WombatBribeManager`-like operator, mock `stakingToken`/`rewardToken`, and two initial stakers `Alice` (staked `A`) and `Bob` (staked `B`) via `stakeFor`.
2. Advance state so `Alice`/`Bob`'s `userRewardPerTokenPaid` is checkpointed at `rewardPerTokenStored = 0`.
3. Simulate attacker: call `stakeFor(attacker, X)` (mimicking `vote(pool,+X)`).
4. Call `queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, rewardToken)` (mimicking bribe harvest via `castVotes`), asserting `rewardPerTokenStored` increases by `rewardAmount * 1e18 / (A + B + X)`.
5. Call `getReward`/`_getReward` for attacker (mimicking `claimBribe`), record `attackerReward = earned(attacker, rewardToken)`.
6. Call `withdrawFor(attacker, X, false)` (mimicking `vote(pool,-X)`).
7. Assert: `attackerReward > 0` despite `X` being staked for a single transaction, and `sum(earned(Alice) + earned(Bob) + attackerReward) < historicalRewards` is NOT violated in absolute terms, but `earned(Alice) + earned(Bob)` is strictly less than what they would have earned had `X` not been staked (i.e., `rewardAmount * A/(A+B)` vs actual `rewardAmount * A/(A+B+X)`), demonstrating measurable dilution/theft of yield proportional to `X`.
