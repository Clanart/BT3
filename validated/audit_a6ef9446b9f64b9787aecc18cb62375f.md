### Title
Instant, undiluted `rewardPerTokenStored` bump lets a same-block depositor with no holding duration capture a full pro-rata share of newly queued rewards, diluting yield owed to existing long-term stakers - ([File: rewards/BaseRewardPool.sol])

### Summary
`BaseRewardPool._provisionReward` (invoked from `queueNewRewards`) applies the entire newly-queued reward instantly to `rewardPerTokenStored` based on `totalStaked()` at that exact moment, with no time-weighting, streaming, or minimum staking duration. Because `MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` are public, unrestricted, and freely composable in adjacent transactions, an attacker can inflate `totalStaked` immediately before a reward manager's `queueNewRewards` call and withdraw immediately after, capturing a pro-rata share of the reward for zero economic holding time at the expense of genuine long-term stakers.

### Finding Description
`MasterMagpie.deposit` → `_deposit` (rewards/MasterMagpie.sol:337-338, 482-505) updates `user.amount`/`user.available` and calls `_harvestBaseRewarder`, which internally triggers `BaseRewardPool.getReward` under the `updateReward` modifier, setting `userRewardPerTokenPaid[token][account] = rewardPerToken(token)` at the pre-reward value [1](#0-0) .

Immediately afterward, a reward manager calls `queueNewRewards` (`onlyManager`), which routes to `_provisionReward`: [2](#0-1) 
This computes `rewardPerTokenStored += (amountReward * 10**stakingDecimals) / totalStaked()` using the **current** `totalStaked()`, which already includes the attacker's newly deposited balance, since `balanceOf` reads live staking info from `MasterMagpie` [3](#0-2) .

The attacker then calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw` → `_withdraw` → `_harvestAndUnstake` → `_harvestBaseRewarder` → `BaseRewardPool.getReward`, which pays out `earned()` computed as `balanceOf(account) * (rewardPerToken - userRewardPerTokenPaid) / decimals` [4](#0-3) . Since `balanceOf(account)` is the attacker's large deposit and `rewardPerToken` just jumped, the attacker receives a full share of the reward despite zero holding duration.

None of the existing guards prevent this: `nonReentrant` on `deposit`/`withdraw` (rewards/MasterMagpie.sol:337-346) only blocks reentrancy *within* a single transaction, not sequences across separate transactions; `whenNotPaused` does not address timing; there is no reward streaming/vesting period (unlike a Synthetix-style `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` design), no minimum staking duration, and no deposit/withdrawal fee to disincentivize instantaneous stake-and-exit.

### Impact Explanation
This causes a real, quantifiable transfer of yield: the attacker's temporary inflation of `totalStaked` at the exact moment `_provisionReward` runs both (a) lets the attacker claim a nonzero share of the reward for 0 seconds of staking and (b) permanently reduces the `rewardPerTokenStored` increment that legitimate long-term stakers receive, since the reward is divided by an artificially inflated `totalStaked`. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class explicitly in scope.

### Likelihood Explanation
`MasterMagpie.deposit`/`withdraw` and the underlying `WombatPoolHelper.deposit`/`withdraw` paths are fully permissionless and callable by any EOA/contract holding the staking/deposit token. The main precondition is timing: the attacker must observe (from mempool) or predict a pending `queueNewRewards`/`vote`/`harvest` call (from `WombatStaking._sendRewards` or `WombatStaking.vote`, rewards/WombatStaking.sol:334, 411, 769) and land a deposit immediately before it and a withdrawal immediately after. Note that a strict single-transaction flash loan cannot span across the manager's intervening transaction; the attacker needs real (not necessarily flash-loaned) capital held for the duration of roughly one block (e.g., via a normal collateralized loan or existing capital), which is a materially different (and less trivially "free") precondition than the pure flash-loan framing in the question, but the exploit remains feasible and repeatable for any actor able to front-run the reward-queuing transaction, at the cost of temporarily parking capital rather than a true atomic flash loan.

### Recommendation
Introduce time-weighted reward distribution instead of instant `rewardPerTokenStored` application - e.g., stream newly queued rewards linearly over a fixed duration (Synthetix `rewardRate`/`periodFinish` pattern) so momentary stake inflation cannot capture a disproportionate share, and/or require a minimum staking duration or apply a withdrawal fee/cooldown in `MasterMagpie`/`BaseRewardPool` to prevent same-block or near-same-block deposit-then-withdraw reward extraction.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `MasterMagpie`, `BaseRewardPool` (or `BaseRewardPoolV2`), a mock staking token and reward token; register a pool and a reward manager.
2. Have a long-term staker deposit `X` staking tokens and let time pass (irrelevant to the accounting since no time-weighting exists, but included for contrast).
3. In block N: attacker calls `MasterMagpie.deposit(stakingToken, largeAmount)` with borrowed/parked capital.
4. Still in block N (or block N+1): reward manager calls `BaseRewardPool.queueNewRewards(rewardAmount, rewardToken)`.
5. Immediately after: attacker calls `MasterMagpie.withdraw(stakingToken, largeAmount)`.
6. Assert attacker's received reward (`RewardPaid` event / balance delta of reward token) is `> 0` despite `0`-block/second holding duration, and assert the long-term staker's subsequent `earned()` is measurably reduced compared to a control run where the attacker's deposit/withdraw is omitted (i.e., `rewardPerTokenStored` increment is smaller because `totalStaked()` was inflated by the attacker's temporary deposit).
