### Title
Flash-stake sniping of `DelegateVoteRewardPool.harvestAll()` reward distribution via `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` lets an attacker steal unclaimed yield from genuine delegate-pool depositors - (File: `rewards/DelegateVoteRewardPool.sol`, `rewards/BribeRewardPool.sol`)

### Summary
`DelegateVoteRewardPool` distributes newly harvested bribe rewards as an instantaneous "accumulated reward per share" update (`rewardPerTokenStored += amountReward * 1e18 / totalSupply`) rather than as a time-weighted stream. Because `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` are only gated by `onlyOperator` (the `WombatBribeManager`, reachable by any vlMGP holder via `vote`/`unvote`) and `harvestAll()` is fully permissionless, an attacker can inflate their balance immediately before a reward harvest, capture a share of the harvested rewards proportional to their point-in-time balance, and withdraw immediately after, extracting yield that should have accrued to depositors who held stake over the actual accrual period.

### Finding Description
- `stakeFor`/`withdrawFor` in `DelegateVoteRewardPool.sol` (lines 57-82) update `_balances`/`totalSupply` under `updateRewards(...)`, and are callable by the `operator` (`WombatBribeManager`) only — reachable indirectly and permissionlessly by any vlMGP holder through `vote()`/`unvote()` routed to a delegated pool. [1](#0-0) 
- `harvestAll()` has no access control at all: `function harvestAll() external { ... claimAllBribes ...; _manageRewards(...); }`. [2](#0-1) 
- The reward-per-share update in `_queueNewRewardsWithoutTransfer` is a lump-sum division by the *current* `totalSupply` at the moment of the call, with no time-weighting or streaming component: [3](#0-2) 
- Because this division uses the instantaneous `totalSupply`, and `earned()`/reward accounting (in `BaseRewardPoolV2`) pays each account `balance[account] * (rewardPerTokenStored - userRewardPerTokenPaid[account])`, a staker's share of *any single harvested reward event* is determined purely by their balance at that exact moment, not by how long they held stake. An attacker can therefore: (1) acquire/borrow vlMGP voting power, (2) call `vote()` to route a huge `stakeFor(attacker, hugeAmount)` into the target `delegatedPool` instant before/at the same block as `harvestAll()`, (3) trigger or wait for `harvestAll()` (which anyone can call) to pull in bribes and update `rewardPerTokenStored` while `totalSupply` is attacker-inflated, capturing a share of the newly harvested rewards proportional to `hugeAmount / totalSupply`, and (4) immediately call `unvote()`/`withdrawFor(..., true)` to claim via `_getReward` and exit.
- No cooldown, minimum holding period, or time-weighted accrual exists to prevent this; the `updateRewards` modifier only settles pending rewards at stake/unstake time, it does not weight rewards by duration held.

### Impact Explanation
This allows theft of unclaimed yield from genuine long-term delegate-pool depositors: rewards that should be split among stakers who contributed exposure over the full accrual period are instead diverted, in whole or in large part, to a flash-staker with no real economic exposure to the pool. This matches the "theft of unclaimed yield" impact class. The magnitude scales with `hugeAmount` relative to existing `totalSupply` — with sufficient capital (or if voting power can be looped/leveraged), the attacker can capture close to 100% of a given harvest's rewards.

### Likelihood Explanation
Feasibility depends on the attacker having enough vlMGP-derived voting power to route a large `stakeFor` through the target `delegatedPool`, and on `harvestAll()`'s permissionless, un-gated nature which lets the attacker time (or self-trigger) the harvest immediately after staking. This is fully repeatable every time bribe rewards are harvested for a delegated pool with lower existing `totalSupply`, and requires no privileged role — only capital and correct call ordering (`vote` → `harvestAll` → `unvote`/`withdrawFor`), all reachable by an ordinary vlMGP holder.

### Recommendation
Replace the instantaneous lump-sum reward-per-share update with a time-weighted/streaming reward model (e.g., Synthetix-style `rewardRate` over a fixed `rewardsDuration`), or introduce a minimum stake-holding period / cooldown before newly staked balance is eligible to receive freshly queued rewards, so that reward shares reflect duration of exposure rather than point-in-time balance at harvest time.

### Proof of Concept
Foundry test plan:
1. Deploy `DelegateVoteRewardPool` with mock `operator` (bribe manager) and reward token; seed an initial "genuine" staker with a stable balance via `stakeFor(genuine, X)`.
2. Advance time/blocks to simulate genuine staker's accrual period.
3. In one block: call `stakeFor(attacker, hugeAmount)` (simulating `vote()` routing), then call `harvestAll()` (simulating bribe manager returning a fixed `earnedRewards` amount via a mock `claimAllBribes`), then call `withdrawFor(attacker, hugeAmount, true)` (simulating `unvote()`/`_getReward`).
4. Assert: `attacker`'s claimed reward ≈ `hugeAmount/(X+hugeAmount) * harvestedReward`, disproportionate to zero time held, while `genuine` staker's `earned()` for that harvest event is diluted below what it would have been absent the flash-stake — demonstrating reward capture is bounded only by point-in-time balance, not time-weighted stake.

    function stakeFor(
        address _for,
        uint256 _amount
    ) external override onlyOperator updateRewards(_for, rewardTokens) {
        totalSupply = totalSupply + _amount;
        _balances[_for] = _balances[_for] + _amount;
        _updateVote();

        emit Staked(_for, _amount);
    }

    function withdrawFor(
        address _for,
        uint256 _amount,
        bool _claim
    ) external override onlyOperator updateRewards(_for, rewardTokens) {
        totalSupply = totalSupply - _amount;
        _balances[_for] = _balances[_for] - _amount;
        _updateVote();

        emit Withdrawn(_for, _amount);

        if (_claim) {
            _getReward(_for);
        }
    }
